# UNIT: Meeting and basic expressions | Greetings, pronouns, question words
## Greetings
o olá | hello, hi | Hello, Sofia! How are you?
bom dia | good morning | Good morning, teacher.
boa tarde | good afternoon | Good afternoon, my friend.
boa noite | good evening, good night | Good evening, see you tomorrow.
adeus | goodbye, farewell | Goodbye, Lucas, have a good trip.
até logo | see you later | Well, see you later, Maria.
até amanhã | see you tomorrow | I'm leaving. See you tomorrow!
tudo bem | how are you, all good | Hello, Diego, is everything alright?
prazer em o conhecer (e) | nice to meet you | I am Omar. Nice to meet you.
bem-vindo (e) | welcome | Welcome to Brazil, Ali!
## Politeness
obrigado (e) | thank you (said by male) | Thank you very much for everything.
obrigada (d) | thank you (said by female) | Thank you for the help.
por favor | please | A coffee, please.
de nada | you're welcome, not at all | Thank you. — You're welcome.
desculpa | sorry, excuse me | Sorry, I don't understand.
sinto muito | I'm so sorry, my apologies | I'm sorry, I can't.
com licença | excuse me, pardon me | Excuse me, where is the subway?
sim | yes | Yes, I am from Turkey.
não | no, not | No, thank you.
combinado | agreed, deal | At five o'clock? — Deal.
## Personal pronouns
eu | I | I am Yuki.
tu | you (informal singular) | And you, what is your name?
ele | he, it | He is my friend Lukas.
ela | she, it | She is from Brazil.
o senhor | you (formal male) | Are you the teacher?
a senhora | you (formal female) | You are very kind.
nós | we | We live in Salvador.
vocês | you (plural) | Are you students?
eles | they (male or mixed) | They are Brazilian.
elas | they (female only) | They speak Portuguese.
## Question words
o que | what | What is this?
quem | who | Who is that woman?
onde | where | Where is the hotel?
quando | when | When does the class start?
como | how | How are you?
por que | why | Why do you study Portuguese?
quanto (e) | how much, how many | How much does this book cost?
qual | which, what | What is your name?
para onde | where to | Where are you going?
de onde | where from | Where are you from?
## Basic verbs
ser (foi) | to be (permanent) | I am an engineer.
estar (estive) | to be (temporary) | I am very tired.
ter (tive) | to have | I have two brothers.
fazer (fiz) | to do, to make | What do you do?
ir (fui) | to go | I am going to the market.
vir (vim) | to come | I come from Istanbul.
falar | to speak, to talk | She speaks English and Spanish.
morar | to live, to reside | Where do you live?
gostar | to like, to enjoy | I like Brazilian music.
trabalhar | to work | I work in a bank.
## Introduction expressions
o nome | name, first name | My name is Anna.
o sobrenome | surname, last name | What is your last name?
a idade | age | What is your age?
a nacionalidade | nationality | What is your nationality?
o país | country | Turkey is a beautiful country.
a cidade | city | Lisbon is a historic city.
o idioma | language, tongue | How many languages do you speak?
a profissión | profession, occupation | What is your profession?
o estudante | student, pupil | He is a good student.
o amigo | friend, pal | This is my best friend.
## Classroom objects
o livro | book | Open the book to page ten.
o caderno | notebook, exercise book | I write notes in my notebook.
a caneta | pen, ballpoint pen | Do you have a blue pen?
o lápis | pencil | I use a pencil to draw.
a borracha | eraser, rubber | I need an eraser.
o papel | paper | Give me a sheet of paper.
a mesa | table, desk | The book is on the table.
a cadeira | chair | Sit in this chair, please.
a porta | door | Please, close the door.
a janela | window | Open the window, it's hot.
## Everyday objects
o telefone | telephone, phone | My phone is on the table.
a chave | key | Where are the house keys?
a bolsa | bag, purse | My bag is black.
o relógio | watch, clock | What time does the clock show?
o dinheiro | money, cash | I don't have money with me.
o óculos | glasses, spectacles | Where did I leave my glasses?
o guarda-chuva | umbrella | Take the umbrella, it's going to rain.
o carro | car, automobile | The red car is fast.
a casa | house, home | I am going home now.
o computador | computer, laptop | I work with a laptop.
## Basic adjectives
bom (e) | good, fine | This coffee is very good.
mau (e) | bad, poor | Today the weather is bad.
grande | big, large | Sao Paulo is a big city.
pequeno (e) | small, little | I live in a small house.
novo (e) | new, young | I bought a new car.
velho (e) | old, aged | This book is very old.
bonito (e) | beautiful, pretty | Rio de Janeiro is very beautiful.
fácil | easy, simple | Portuguese is an easy language.
difícil | difficult, hard | Grammar is not that difficult.
feliz | happy, joyful | I am very happy today.
## Numbers one to ten
um (e) | one | I have one brother and one sister.
dois (e) | two | They have two cars.
três | three | It is three o'clock in the afternoon.
quatro | four | The table has four chairs.
cinco | five | I work five days a week.
seis | six | The book has six chapters.
sete | seven | The week has seven days.
oito | eight | I wake up at eight in the morning.
nove | nine | The course lasts nine months.
dez | ten | I have ten reals in my wallet.
