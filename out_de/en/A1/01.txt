# UNIT: Introductions and basic expressions | Greetings, pronouns, question words
## Greetings
hallo | hello, hi | Hello, Anna! How are you?
guten morgen | good morning | Good morning, Mr. Müller.
guten tag | good afternoon, hello | Good afternoon, Mrs. Schmidt.
guten abend | good evening | Good evening, ladies and gentlemen.
gute nacht | good night | Good night, sleep well.
tschüss | bye, goodbye | Bye, Emre, see you soon.
auf wiedersehen | goodbye | Goodbye, Mrs. Wagner.
bis bald | see you soon | Bye, see you soon in Berlin.
bis morgen | see you tomorrow | We'll see each other. See you tomorrow!
wie geht es dir | how are you | Hello Lukas, how are you?
## Courtesy
danke | thanks, thank you | Thanks for your help.
bitte | please | A water, please.
gern geschehen | you're welcome | Thank you very much. — You're welcome.
entschuldigung | excuse me, sorry | Excuse me, where is the train station?
es tut mir leid | I'm sorry | I'm sorry, I don't have time.
ja | yes | Yes, I come from Turkey.
nein | no | No, thank you.
bitte schön | here you go, you're welcome | Here is your coffee. — Here you go.
prost | cheers | Cheers to our success!
guten appetit | enjoy your meal, bon appétit | The food is ready. Enjoy your meal!
## Personal pronouns
ich | I | I am Anna.
du | you (singular informal) | Where do you live, Emre?
er | he | His name is Lukas.
die frau | she [in this context] | The woman comes from Munich.
es | it | This is a book. It is new.
wir | we | We are learning German.
ihr | you (plural informal) | Where do you come from, children?
die leute | they [used as people] | The people live in Hamburg.
die formalität | formal address | What is your name, please? (Formal)
man | one, people (general subject) | German is spoken here.
## Verb "sein" (to be)
sein | to be | I would like to be a doctor.
ich bin | I am | I am tired.
du bist | you are | You are very nice.
er ist | he is | He is my brother.
wir sind | we are | We are students.
ihr seid | you are | You are clever.
die gruppe ist | the group is (they are) | The group is from Vienna.
der gast ist | the guest is (formal) | The guest is warmly welcome.
bist du | are you (question) | Are you ready?
sind wir | are we (question) | Are we too late?
## Question words
wer | who | Who is that?
was | what | What are you doing?
wo | where | Where do you live?
woher | where from | Where do you come from?
wohin | where to | Where are you going?
wann | when | When are you coming?
wie | how | What is your name?
warum | why | Why are you learning German?
wie viel | how much | How much does that cost?
welcher | which | What day is today?
## Basic verbs
haben | to have | I have a dog.
machen | to do, to make | What are we doing today?
gehen | to go | I am going home.
kommen | to come | Where do you come from?
wohnen | to live, to reside | I live in Frankfurt.
sprechen | to speak | Do you speak German?
lernen | to learn | We are learning Spanish.
lesen | to read | She is reading a book.
schreiben | to write | He is writing a letter.
sehen | to see | I see you.
## Adjectives
gut | good | That is a good idea.
schlecht | bad | The weather is bad today.
groß | big, tall | Berlin is a big city.
klein | small | The cat is small.
neu | new | My car is new.
alt | old | The house is very old.
schön | beautiful, nice | That is a beautiful picture.
kalt | cold | It is cold in winter.
warm | warm | The coffee is still warm.
schnell | fast | The train is very fast.
## Everyday objects
das Buch | book | I am reading a book.
der Tisch | table | The book is on the table.
der Stuhl | chair | Sit down on the chair.
das Fenster | window | Please open the window.
die Tür | door | The door is closed.
der Stift | pen, pencil | Do you have a pen for me?
das Heft | notebook | Write that in your notebook.
die Tasche | bag | My bag is heavy.
das Handy | mobile phone, cell phone | Where is my mobile phone?
der Computer | computer | He is working on the computer.
## Family
die Familie | family | My family lives in Izmir.
die Mutter | mother | My mother likes cooking.
der Vater | father | My father is reading the newspaper.
die Schwester | sister | I have a sister.
der Bruder | brother | His brother is a doctor.
das Kind | child | The child is playing in the park.
die Eltern | parents | My parents like traveling.
die Großeltern | grandparents | Our grandparents live in the countryside.
die Tante | aunt | My aunt lives in Cologne.
der Onkel | uncle | My uncle is very funny.
## Numbers (1-10)
eins | one | I only have one.
zwei | two | We have two cats.
drei | three | She has three brothers.
vier | four | It is four o'clock.
fünf | five | The week has five days.
sechs | six | He is coming at six o'clock.
sieben | seven | The cat has seven lives.
acht | eight | I work eight hours.
neun | nine | It is nine degrees cold.
zehn | ten | The child is ten years old.
