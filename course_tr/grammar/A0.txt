# TOPIC: Turkish letters and sounds | çay, şeker, dağ, ılık, göz
> Turkish is written exactly as it is spoken: each letter always has the same sound. There are 29 letters, and q, w and x are not used.
> Six letters are special: ç (ch), ş (sh), ğ (soft g, which lengthens the vowel before it), ı (dotless i), and the rounded front vowels ö and ü.
= çay | tea
= şeker | sugar
= dağ | mountain
= ılık | lukewarm
= göz | eye

? Which letter sounds like the "j" in "jam"? | c | j | ç
? Which letter never starts a word? | ğ | g | h
? Which word means "tea"? | çay | cay | şay
? How do you write a capital dotted i in Turkish? | İ | I | Î

# TOPIC: Vowel harmony | evler, kitaplar, evde, okulda
> Turkish vowels are either back vowels (a, ı, o, u) or front vowels (e, i, ö, ü). The vowel in a suffix changes to match the last vowel of the word.
> Two-way suffixes use a or e (-lar / -ler). Four-way suffixes use ı, i, u or ü (-ı / -i / -u / -ü).
= kitaplar | books
= evler | houses
= okulda | at school
= evde | at home
= güzel mi? | is it nice?

# TOPIC: This and that | bu, şu, o, Bu ne?
> "Bu" means "this" (near me), "şu" means "that" (nearby, when pointing) and "o" means "that" (further away). To ask "What is this?" say "Bu ne?".
> Turkish needs no word for "is" in these sentences, and it has no word for "the". "Bir" (one) can be used like "a".
= Bu ne? | What is this?
= Bu bir kitap. | This is a book.
= Şu kim? | Who is that?
= O bir kedi. | That is a cat.

# TOPIC: There is, there isn't | var, yok, var mı?
> "Var" means "there is / there are" and "yok" means "there isn't / there aren't". Both come at the end of the sentence.
> To ask a question, add "mı": "Çay var mı?" (Is there any tea?). The short answers are "Var." and "Yok."
= Çay var. | There's tea.
= Süt yok. | There's no milk.
= Ekmek var mı? | Is there any bread?
= Evet, var. Hayır, yok. | Yes, there is. No, there isn't.
? How do you say "There is no water."? | Su yok. | Su var. | Yok su var.
? How do you ask "Is there any coffee?" | Kahve var mı? | Kahve mı var? | Var kahve mı?
? Answer "no" to "Simit var mı?" | Hayır, yok. | Hayır, var. | Evet, yok.
? Where do "var" and "yok" go in the sentence? | at the end | at the beginning | before the noun
