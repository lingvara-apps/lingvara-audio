# UNIT: Ser vs. estar and meaning differences | ser listo / estar listo, character vs. state, permanent vs. temporary qualities
## Ser listo: Being clever
ser listo | to be smart, to be clever | My brother is very good at mathematics.
ser listo, por fin | to be smart finally [note on ser listo] | It is not easy to be clever in everything.
ser un hombre listo | to be a smart man | The professor is a smart and cultured man.
ser una persona lista | to be a smart person | She is a smart person who solves problems.
ser tan listo | to be as smart | I don't pretend to be as smart as you.
ser más listo | to be smarter | You must be smarter in your business.
ser el más listo | to be the smartest | He wants to be the smartest in the class.
ser listo de verdad | to be truly smart | He proves to be truly smart today.
ser lo bastante listo | to be smart enough | You have to be smart enough to understand it.
ser tan listo como | to be as smart as | They want to be as smart as their mentor.
## Estar listo: Being ready
estar listo | to be ready | Dinner is ready in the kitchen.
estar listo para salir | to be ready to leave | We are ready to go on a trip.
estar listo ya | to be ready already | Everything is ready for the event.
estar listo el informe | for the report to be ready | The financial report is ready to review.
estar listo el documento | for the document to be ready | The legal document is ready today.
estar listo el pedido | for the order to be ready | Your order is ready at the counter.
estar listo el proyecto | for the project to be ready | The technical project is ready for delivery.
estar listo el desayuno | for breakfast to be ready | Breakfast is ready on the table.
estar listo el café | for coffee to be ready | Hot coffee is ready to drink.
estar listo el coche | for the car to be ready | The car is ready for the long trip.
## Ser rico: Being rich (permanent quality)
ser rico | to be rich, to be abundant | That country is rich in natural resources.
ser rico en historia | to be rich in history | The old city is rich in history.
ser una persona rica | to be a wealthy person | He doesn't seek to be a person rich in money.
ser rico culturalmente | to be culturally rich | Our region is culturally rich.
ser rico en tradiciones | to be rich in traditions | Their culture is rich in ancient traditions.
ser rico en matices | to be rich in nuances | The poem is rich in deep nuances.

ser rico en flora | to be rich in flora | The national park is rich in diverse flora.
ser rico en fauna | to be rich in fauna | The jungle is rich in tropical fauna.
ser rico en experiencia | to be rich in experience | A good teacher tends to be rich in experience.
## Estar rico: Being delicious (temporary state)

estar rico el postre | for the dessert to be delicious | I think the dessert is delicious.

estar rico el queso | for the cheese to be delicious | The fresh cheese is delicious with bread.
estar rico el pescado | for the fish to be delicious | The fried fish is delicious and crispy.

estar rico el pan | for the bread to be delicious | Freshly baked bread is delicious.
estar rico el almuerzo | for the lunch to be delicious | The whole lunch is delicious today.
## Ser seguro: Being reliable (character)
ser seguro | to be safe, to be reliable | It is a safe car for the family.
ser un método seguro | to be a reliable method | This is a reliable working method.
ser un lugar seguro | to be a safe place | Her house has always been a safe place.
ser seguro de sí mismo | to be self-confident | To speak in public, you have to be self-confident.
ser un refugio seguro | to be a safe haven | The mountain offers a safe haven.
ser un valor seguro | to be a sure bet [figurative] | His friendship is a sure value.
ser una apuesta segura | to be a safe bet | Investing there seemed like a safe bet.
ser un puerto seguro | to be a safe harbor | The old port is a safe harbor.
ser un camino seguro | to be a secure path | We aim to be a safe path to success.
ser un sistema seguro | to be a secure system | They aim to be a fail-safe system.
## Estar seguro: Being sure (state)
estar seguro | to be sure | I am sure he will come tomorrow.
estar seguro de la respuesta | to be sure of the answer | She is sure of the correct answer.
estar seguro de todo | to be sure about everything | You cannot be sure of everything in life.
estar seguro de su decisión | to be sure of one's decision | We are sure of our final decision.
estar seguro del éxito | to be sure of success | They are sure of the project's success.
estar seguro al cien por cien | to be one hundred percent sure | I am one hundred percent sure of this data.
estar seguro de la fecha | to be sure of the date | Are you sure about the date of the trip?
estar seguro de su palabra | to be sure of one's word | I am sure of his sincere word.
estar seguro de los hechos | to be sure of the facts | Nobody is sure of all the facts.
estar seguro del camino | to be sure of the way | Are you sure about the right way?

estar verde en computación | to be green at computing | I am green in advanced computing.

## Ser vivo: Being lively, smart, crafty (character)
ser vivo | to be sharp, to be shrewd | The boy is very sharp and learns fast.
ser un hombre vivo | to be a shrewd man | They say he is a shrewd man in business.
ser una persona viva | to be a lively person | She is a lively and very alert person.
ser muy vivo en clase | to be very sharp in class | The student tends to be very sharp in class.
ser un comerciante vivo | to be a shrewd merchant | He proves to be a shrewd and cunning merchant.
ser más vivo que nadie | to be sharper than anyone | He believes he is sharper than anyone in the group.
