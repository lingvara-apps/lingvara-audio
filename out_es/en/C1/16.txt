# UNIT: Spoken Spanish | Particles, exclamations, everyday slang
## Filler words
bueno | well, alright | Well, nothing is wrong at all.
o sea | I mean, in other words | I mean, the situation is complex.
pues | well then, so | Well, I don't have any doubt.
osease | that is to say, meaning | That is to say, everything turned out perfect.
fíjate | check this out, look at that | Look what a great surprise.
mira | look, listen | Look, it's an excellent idea.
escucha | listen, hear me out | Listen to what I'm telling you.
oye | hey, listen | Hey, do you have a minute?
vaya | wow, goodness | Wow, what a complete disaster.
caramba | goodness, darn | Goodness, I didn't expect that.
## Terms of address and agreement
chacho | buddy, pal (regional) | Hello, buddy, how's it going?
tío | dude, mate (informal) | Hey, dude, let's go already.
hombre | man, come on (interjection) | Come on man, don't worry so much.
mujer | woman, come on (to a woman) | Come on, be patient.
claro que sí | of course, definitely | Are you coming? —Definitely!
ni hablar | no way, out of the question | Lend money? —No way!
por supuesto | of course, certainly | Do you accept the proposal? —Of course.
efectivamente | indeed, exactly | Indeed, it was true.
exacto | precisely, that's right | Exactly, you hit the nail on the head.
negativo | negative, nope | Any problem? —Negative.
## Diminutives and augmentatives
chiquitito | tiny, very small | It was a tiny kitten.
rapidito | quickly, in a jiffy | Do it quickly, please.
ahorita | right now, in a moment | I'm coming right now.
cerquita | very close, right nearby | We live very close to here.
calentito | nice and warm, piping hot | The bread is nice and warm.
grandote | huge, big and burly | He had a huge dog.
listillo | smart aleck, know-it-all | Don't go around acting like a know-it-all.
bonito | pretty, lovely | What a pretty dress.
fresquito | cool, pleasantly brisk | There is a cool breeze.
chulito | cocky, smug | Don't be so cocky.
## Surprise and astonishment
madre mía | oh my gosh, goodness me | Oh my gosh, what a height.
anda ya | come on, get out | You won? —Come on!
no me digas | you don't say, no way | I passed the exam. —You don't say!
estoy alucinando | I'm blown away, I can't believe my eyes | I'm blown away by this.
me has dejado de piedra | you've stunned me, I'm speechless | Your news has stunned me.
no me lo creo | I don't believe it | I still don't believe it.
qué fuerte | how shocking, unbelievable | What happened is really shocking.
madre | goodness, heavens | Goodness, there are so many people here.
valientes cosas | what strange things, what a mess | What strange things happen nowadays.
jolines | blimey, darn it | Darn it, I forgot the key.
## Anger and annoyance
menuda fauna | what a crowd, what characters | There was quite a crowd at that party.
qué fastidio | what a hassle, what a nuisance | What a hassle having to wait.
vete a freir espárragos | go jump in a lake, get lost | You're annoying, go jump in a lake.
estoy hasta las narices | I'm fed up, I've had it up to here | I've had it up to here with lies.
vaya chasco | what a letdown, what a disappointment | The trip was a huge letdown.
qué lata | what a drag, what a pain | What a drag of a lecture.
menudo rollo | what a bore, what nonsense | His speech was a complete bore.
qué morro | what nerve, what cheek | What nerve that guy has.
estoy harto | I've had enough, I'm sick of it | I'm fed up with excuses.
vaya broma | what a bad joke, what a poor prank | What an annoying practical joke.
## Slang and everyday idioms
dar el toque | give a ring, give a heads-up | I'll give you a ring when I arrive.
estar mosca | be suspicious, smell a rat | The boss is very suspicious today.
irse al mazo | give up, fold | I don't want to fold so soon.
currar como un chino | work like a dog, bust one's hump | I had to work like a dog.
estar petado | be packed, be rammed | The metro was completely packed.
irse de copas | go out for drinks | We are going out for drinks today.
montar un pollo | make a scene, cause a fuss | He started making a scene in the street.
irse por peteneras | beat around the bush, go off on a tangent | Don't beat around the bush.
ser un panoli | be a sucker, be a fool | Don't be such a sucker with them.
pillar el tranquillo | get the hang of, get the knack | I've already got the hang of the program.
## Calls and interjections
chis | shh, hush | Shh, don't make so much noise.
puf | phew, ugh (exhaustion) | Phew, what a tiring day.
oh | oh | Oh, what a pleasant surprise.
ay | ouch, oh (pain) | Ouch, my leg hurts.
uy | oops, whoa | Oops, I almost fell to the ground.
eh | right?, huh? | That's right, isn't it, huh?
bah | bah, whatever | Bah, don't talk nonsense.
hala | come on, let's go | Come on, everybody run.
uf | whew, ugh (overwhelm) | Whew, I can't take any more heat.
epa | hey, whoa | Hey, where are you going so fast?
## Abbreviations and shortenings
cole | school (informal) | I'm running to school.
profe | teacher, prof | The teacher explained everything to us.
bici | bike | I left the bike in the courtyard.
peli | movie, flick | We watched a great movie.
finde | weekend (informal) | What plans do you have for the weekend?
mates | math, maths | I hate math exams.
insti | high school (informal) | We meet at the high school gate.
boli | pen, ballpoint | Can you lend me your blue pen?
bici_cleta | bicycle | I bought a new bike.
ofi | office (informal) | I'm going right back to the office.
## Reactions and conversational transitions
en fin | anyway, in short | Anyway, we have to keep moving forward.
a ver | let's see | Let's see what happens tomorrow.
anda | come on, as if | Come on, don't tell me fairy tales.
vamos | come on, well now | Come on, don't be so shy.
venga | alright, come on then | Alright, let's finish up now.
bueno pues | well then, alright then | Well then, see you soon.
total | in the end, long story short | In the end, we missed the flight.
hombre ya | that's enough, come on already | That's enough, don't push it.
vaya por dios | oh dear, for heaven's sake | Oh dear, it broke.
ya ves | you see, indeed | You see how things change.
## Informal terms of address
chiquillo | kid, youngster | Come here, kid.
chaval | lad, boy | He is a very smart lad.
tía | girl, babe (informal address) | Hey girl, that's amazing.
che | hey, man (regional) | Hey, listen for a second.
quillo | mate, bro (southern Spain) | What's up, mate?
veterano | old hand, veteran | Hello, my veteran friend.
pibe | kid, guy (Rioplatense) | He is a nice kid.
socio | partner, pal | Take it easy, partner.
maestro | champ, boss (respectful address) | Thanks for everything, champ.
jefe | chief, boss (informal address) | Tell me, boss.
