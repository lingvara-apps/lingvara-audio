# UNIT: Political Science | Ideologies, power, electoral systems, security
## Ideologies and currents of thought
el liberalizm | liberalism | Liberalism defends individual freedoms against the state.
el socializm | socialism | Socialism seeks social equality through collective property.
el conservadurizm | conservatism | Conservatism values traditions and the established order.
el nacionalizm | nationalism | Nationalism emphasizes the cultural identity of the homeland.
el comunizm | communism | Communism proposes a society without social classes or private property.
el fascizm | fascism | Fascism imposes extreme nationalist and authoritarian power.
el anarquizm | anarchism | Anarchism rejects any form of state authority.
el ecologizm | environmentalism | Environmentalism prioritizes the conservation of the environment.
el feminizm | feminism | Feminism fights for equal rights between genders.
el populizm | populism | Populism appeals to the people against traditional elites.
## Political structures and power

el poder ejecutivo | executive branch | The executive branch applies the laws passed in parliament.
el poder legislativo principal | primary legislative power | The primary legislative power drafts and votes on legal norms.
el poder judicial | judiciary | The judiciary judges according to the legal system.
la constitución política | political constitution | The political constitution defines the fundamental rights of the country.
el estado de derecho | rule of law | The rule of law subjects all rulers to the law.
la autoridad legítima | legitimate authority | Legitimate authority arises from transparent popular voting.
el sistema republicano | republican system | The republican system elects its leaders for a limited period.
la monarquía parlamentaria | parliamentary monarchy | Parliamentary monarchy limits the king through parliament.

la abstención ciudadana | citizen abstention | Citizen abstention reflects a certain disaffection towards parties.
