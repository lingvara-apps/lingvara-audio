# UNIT: Verb patterns | ir a, acabar de, volver a, dejar de, ponerse a…
## Upcoming action
ir a + infinitivo | be going to, about to | I'm going to cook a paella today.
empezar a + infinitivo | start, begin to | I'm going to start studying Russian.
comenzar a + infinitivo | commence, begin | You must start running now.
ponerse a + infinitivo | set about, start doing | I am going to set about cleaning the house.
echarse a + infinitivo | burst out, start suddenly | The boy is going to burst into tears.
romper a + infinitivo | burst into, suddenly start | She is going to burst out laughing soon.
llegar a + infinitivo | come to, manage to | We are going to come to understand this.
venir a + infinitivo | amount to, come to be | This comes to cost a hundred euros.
pasar a + infinitivo | move on to, proceed to | We are going to move on to another topic now.
animarse a + infinitivo | dare to, pluck up courage to | You must dare to travel alone.
## Recently finished action
acabar de + infinitivo | have just [done something] | I have just arrived from the office.
terminar de + infinitivo | finish [doing something] | I have just finished reading this.
pasar de + infinitivo | couldn't care less about, pass on | I'm going to pass on arguing with you.
dar en + infinitivo | take to, have a habit of | He takes to criticizing all day long.
quedar en + infinitivo | agree to, arrange to | We agreed to meet at eight.
tardar en + infinitivo | be long in, take time to | I am not going to be long in leaving here.
acertar a + infinitivo | happen to, manage to [by chance] | I happened to see my old friend.
alcanzar a + infinitivo | manage to, reach to | I can't manage to hear the music well.
toser y toser | cough and cough | I am going to stop coughing a bit.
parar de + infinitivo | stop [doing something] | It doesn't stop raining all day long.
## Repeated action
volver a + infinitivo | do again, [verb] again | I am going to call my boss again.
tornar a + infinitivo | turn to again, do anew | I believe in good luck anew.
soler + infinitivo | usually, tend to | We usually have a late dinner in summer.
acostumbrar a + infinitivo | be used to, be accustomed to | I am accustomed to reading before sleeping.
repetir el intento | repeat the attempt | I am going to repeat the attempt again.
reintentar la tarea | retry the task | You must retry the task tomorrow.
frecuentar la zona | frequent the area | I frequent this old park very often.
continuar + gerundio | continue, keep on | I continue studying despite everything.
seguir + gerundio | keep on, still [doing] | I keep on waiting for an answer from you.
proseguir el viaje | pursue the journey | We are proceeding on the road towards the north.
## Stopped action
dejar de + infinitivo | stop, quit [doing] | You must stop smoking right now.
parar de llover | stop raining | It's going to stop snowing this afternoon.
suspender la obra | suspend the work | They are going to suspend the project soon.
interrumpir la marcha | interrupt the march | We had to interrupt the journey.
cesar en el cargo | step down from office | The director is going to step down from his post.
abandonar el hábito | quit the habit | I managed to quit coffee at night.
prescindir de ayuda | do without help | I am going to do without your car today.
renunciar al puesto | resign from the post | I am going to resign from this hard job.
evitar el conflicto | avoid conflict | You must avoid arguing with your neighbor.
prohibir fumar | ban smoking | They are going to forbid entering with dogs here.
## Expression of obligation
tener que + infinitivo | have to, must | I have to buy fresh fruit today.
haber de + infinitivo | have to, ought to | You have to study more to pass.
deber + infinitivo | should, must | You must take care of your health every day.
haber que + infinitivo | be necessary, one must | One must respect the rules here.
hacer falta + infinitivo | be necessary, be needed | This room needs to be cleaned.
ser necesario + infinitivo | be necessary | It is necessary to deliver this on Monday.
ser preciso + infinitivo | be essential, be required | It is essential to sign this paper today.
tocar + infinitivo | be one's turn | It's my turn to cook dinner.
corresponder + infinitivo | be one's duty, be up to | It is up to everyone to pay for the electricity.
obligar a + infinitivo | force to, oblige to | They are going to force us to sign that.
## Permission and ability
poder + infinitivo | can, be able to | Can I use your phone a moment?
saber + infinitivo | know how to | I know how to speak three languages fluently.
conseguir + infinitivo | succeed in, manage to | I managed to pass the difficult exam.
lograr + infinitivo | achieve, manage to | I managed to finish the report on time.
alcanzar la meta | reach the goal | We managed to see the top of the mountain.
tener permiso | have permission | We have permission to enter here.
permitir la entrada | allow entry | They don't allow smoking inside the bar.
dejar entrar | let in, allow to enter | My mother lets me go out for a while.
dar permiso | give permission | The boss gave permission to leave.
autorizar la obra | authorize the work | They are going to authorize opening the new venue.
## Attempt and effort
intentar + infinitivo | try, attempt | I try to speak Spanish every afternoon.
probar a + infinitivo | try to, test out | I am going to try cooking without salt.
tratar de + infinitivo | try to, endeavor | I try to understand your reasons.
esforzarse en + infinitivo | strive to, make an effort to | I strive to improve my accent.
luchar por + infinitivo | fight for, strive for | We are fighting to get the scholarship.
procurar + infinitivo | try to, make sure to | I try to arrive punctual to appointments.
dar una oportunidad | give a chance | I am going to give the boy a chance.
hacer el intento | make an attempt | I made an attempt to fix the radio.
poner empeño | put effort into | I put a lot of effort into this project.
dar lo mejor | give one's best | I give the best of myself at work.
## Thought and intention
pensar + infinitivo | plan to, intend to | I plan to travel to Madrid in June.
querer + infinitivo | want to, wish to | I want to buy an art book.
desear + infinitivo | desire to, wish to | I desire to get to know the south of Spain.
tener ganas de + infinitivo | feel like [doing] | I feel like eating an ice cream.
tener la intención | have the intention | I have the intention of changing apartment.
proponerse + infinitivo | set out to, resolve to | I set out to learn all the grammar.
aspirar a + infinitivo | aspire to, aim for | I aspire to get a good position.
pretender + infinitivo | aim to, intend to | I intend to finish this before Friday.
sonar con + infinitivo | dream of [doing] | I dream of living near the sea.
esperar + infinitivo | hope to, expect to | I hope to pass all the subjects.
## Beginning and preparation
disponerse a + infinitivo | get ready to, prepare to | I am getting ready to have dinner with my family.
prepararse para + infinitivo | prepare for | I am preparing to run the marathon.
listo para + infinitivo | ready to | I am ready to start the trip.
dar comienzo | begin, commence | The concert is going to begin now.
romper el hielo | break the ice | I tried to break the ice with a joke.
dar el primer paso | take the first step | You must take the first step today.
abrir el camino | pave the way, open the way | This invention is going to pave the way.
iniciar el proceso | start the process | They are going to start the selection process.
poner en marcha | set in motion, launch | We are going to launch the project.
tomar la iniciativa | take the initiative | I took the initiative to speak with him.
## Result and state
terminar por + infinitivo | end up [doing] | He ended up accepting our help.
acabar por + infinitivo | end up, wind up | I ended up buying the red car.
venir a parar | wind up, come down to | Everything comes down to the same thing.
quedar en nada | come to nothing | The meeting came to nothing in the end.
dar resultado | yield results, pay off | The effort is going to pay off well.
venir bien + infinitivo | do good to, be a good idea | It does good to rest a bit now.
costar trabajo + infinitivo | have trouble, find it hard | I have trouble understanding this rule.
dar pereza + infinitivo | be too lazy to, feel lazy about | I feel too lazy to cook late today.
valer la pena + infinitivo | be worth it | This museum is worth visiting.
resultar difícil + infinitivo | turn out difficult to | Learning so much vocabulary turns out difficult.
