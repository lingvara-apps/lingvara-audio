# UNIT: Education and science | University, research, scientific language
## University life
la facultad | faculty, department | She studied at the faculty of medicine.
el campus | campus | The university campus is very big.
la beca | scholarship, grant | He got a scholarship to do research.
el examen | exam, test | Tomorrow I have an important exam.
la nota | grade, mark | She got a good grade on the test.
el título | degree, title | He received his university degree today.
el alumno | student, pupil | The student is reading a book in the library.
el docente | teacher, instructor | The teacher explains the lesson clearly.
la clase | class, lesson | The class starts at eight o'clock sharp.
la asignatura | subject, course [curriculum] | This subject is very interesting.
