# UNIT: The Spanish Alphabet | 27 letters, ñ, accent mark, and diaeresis
## A – I
A a | like 'a' in "father"; letter name is "a" | friend
B b | like 'b' in "boat"; letter name is "be" | boat
C c | 'k' before a, o, u; 'th' (Spain) or 's' (Latin America) before e, i; letter name is "ce" | car
CH ch | like 'ch' in "chair"; formerly a separate letter, named "che" | boy
D d | like 'd' in "door"; letter name is "de" | finger
E e | like 'e' in "pet"; letter name is "e" | school
F f | like 'f' in "fire"; letter name is "efe" | fire
G g | hard 'g' before a, o, u; raspy 'h' before e, i; letter name is "ge" | glasses
H h | always silent; letter name is "hache" | egg
I i | like 'ee' in "see"; letter name is "i" | island
## J – R
J j | raspy 'h' sound as in Scottish "loch"; letter name is "jota" | garden
K k | like 'k' in "kite"; only in loanwords; letter name is "ka" | kilo
L l | light 'l' as in "light"; letter name is "ele" | moon
M m | like 'm' in "mother"; letter name is "eme" | sea
N n | like 'n' in "nest"; letter name is "ene" | cloud
Ñ ñ | like 'ny' in "canyon"; unique to Spanish, named "eñe" | Spain
O o | like 'o' in "for" (short, pure); letter name is "o" | eye
P p | like 'p' in "pen", unaspirated; letter name is "pe" | beach
Q q | like 'k'; always written as "qu" with silent 'u'; letter name is "cu" | chemistry
R r | single tap 'r'; rolled 'rr' at word start; letter name is "erre" | river
## S – Z ve işaretler
S s | always voiceless 's' as in "sun"; letter name is "ese" | sun
T t | unaspirated 't' as in "stop"; letter name is "te" | tower
U u | like 'oo' in "boot"; letter name is "u" | university
V v | pronounced identically to 'b'; letter name is "uve" | summer
W w | used in loanwords, pronounced like 'v' or 'w'; letter name is "uve doble" | Wi-Fi
X x | like 'x' in "taxi" (ks); letter name is "equis" | xylophone
Y y | like 'y' in "yes" before vowels, like 'ee' at end; letter name is "ye" | yogurt
Z z | 'th' in "think" (Spain) or 's' (Latin America); letter name is "zeta" | juice
á é í ó ú | accent mark (tilde); indicates stress, vowel sound does not change | coffee
ü | diaeresis; shows 'u' is pronounced in "güe" and "güi" | shame
# UNIT: Tricky Sounds | r and rr, lisping c/z, j, ll, h, diphthongs, and stress rules
## R ve L sesleri
-r- | single tap 'r' between vowels, like 'tt' in American "butter" | but
rr | rolled or trilled 'r', tip of tongue vibrates | dog
r- | rolled 'rr' sound at the start of a word | fast
nr | rolled 'rr' sound after 'n' | honor
-r | short single tap at the end of a word | to speak
tr | 't' blends with a single tap 'r' | train
ll | like 'y' in "yes" in most of Spain; like 'sh' in Argentina | key
ya, yo, yu | like 'y' in "yes" before vowels; like 'sh' in Argentina | help
-y | pronounced like 'ee' in "see" at word end or alone | very
l | light 'l' as in "leaf", never dark as in "ball" | salt
## C, Z, G, J
ce, ci | 'th' in "think" (Spain) or 's' (Latin America) | cinema
z | 'th' in "think" (Spain) or 's' (Latin America) | shoe
ca, co, cu | hard 'c' as in "cat" | thing
que, qui | pronounced 'ke', 'ki'; 'u' is silent | cheese
j | raspy 'h' like Scottish "loch" (Spain) or softer 'h' (Latin America) | ham
ge, gi | raspy 'h' sound, identical to 'j' | people
ga, go, gu | hard 'g' as in "game" | fat
gue, gui | hard 'g', 'u' is silent: pronounced 'ge', 'gi' | guitar
güe, güi | 'u' with diaeresis is pronounced: 'gwe', 'gwi' | penguin
x | usually 'ks' as in "box"; 'h' in some traditional names | taxi
## H, B/V, D, S
h | always silent | hotel
hie- | pronounced like 'ye' in "yes" | ice
b, v | both pronounced identically as 'b' | wine
-b-, -v- | soft 'b' between vowels; lips do not fully close | grape
nv | 'n' before 'v' is pronounced as 'm': "mb" | winter
-d- | soft 'th' between vowels, like 'th' in "this" | each
-d | very soft or silent at the end of a word | city
s | always voiceless 's' as in "sun", never voiced like 'z' | house
-s | clear 's' at word end; softened or dropped in southern Spain and Caribbean | goodbye
cc | 'k' followed by 'th' (Spain) or 'ks' (Latin America) | action
## Ünlü birleşimleri ve vurgu
ie | diphthong pronounced in one syllable, like "ye" | well
ue | blended diphthong, like "we" | new
ai, ay | like 'eye' or 'ai' in "aisle" | air
ei, ey | like 'ay' in "say" or "they" | king
oi, oy | like 'oy' in "boy" | today
au | like 'ow' in "cow" | car
ua | blended diphthong, like "wa" in "water" | water
io | diphthong pronounced in one syllable, like "yo" | half
-a, -e, -o, -n, -s | words ending in these letters are stressed on the second-to-last syllable | young
-l, -r, -z | words ending in other consonants are stressed on the last syllable | paper
