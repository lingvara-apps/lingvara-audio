# UNIT: Meeting and basic expressions | Greetings, pronouns, question words
## Greetings
hola | hello, hi | Hello Anna! How are you?
buenos días | good morning | Good morning, Mr. García.
buenas tardes | good afternoon | Good afternoon, how are you?
buenas noches | good evening, good night | Good night, see you tomorrow.
adiós | goodbye, bye | Goodbye Emre, have a good trip.
hasta luego | see you later | Well, see you later, María.
hasta mañana | see you tomorrow | I'm leaving. See you tomorrow!
¿qué tal? | how is it going?, how are you? | Hello Diego, how's it going?
encantado | nice to meet you [male] | I'm Omar. Nice to meet you.
bienvenido | welcome | Welcome to Madrid, Ali!
## Politeness
gracias | thanks, thank you | Thank you very much for everything.
por favor | please | A coffee, please.
de nada | you're welcome, not at all | Thank you. — You're welcome.
perdón | pardon, sorry | Pardon, I don't understand.
lo siento | I'm sorry, excuse me | I'm sorry, I can't.
disculpe | excuse me [formal] | Excuse me, where is the metro?
sí | yes | Yes, I am from Turkey.
no | no, not | No, thank you.
vale | okay, alright | At five? — Alright.
con permiso | excuse me, with your permission | Excuse me, may I pass?
## Personal pronouns
yo | I | I am Yuki.
tú | you [singular informal] | And you, what is your name?
él | he | He is my friend Lukas.
ella | she | She is from Italy.
usted | you [singular formal] | Are you the teacher?
nosotros | we | We live in Seville.
vosotros | you [plural informal, Spain] | Are you from here?
ellos | they [male or mixed group] | They speak Spanish.
ellas | they [female only] | They are sisters.
ustedes | you [plural formal/general Latin America] | Would you like anything else?
## Self-introduction
llamarse | to be called | My name is Camille.
el nombre | name, first name | What is your name?
el apellido | surname, last name | My last name is López.
ser (soy, eres, es) | to be [identity, origin] | I am from Istanbul.
estar (estoy, está) | to be [state, location] | I am very well, thanks.
tener (tengo, tiene) | to have | I am twenty years old.
vivir | to live | I live in Barcelona.
la edad | age | What is your age?
conocer (conozco) | to know, to meet | Nice to meet you.
presentar | to introduce, to present | Let me introduce you to Sofía.
## Countries and languages
España | Spain | I live in Spain.
español | Spaniard, Spanish | I speak a little Spanish.
Turquía | Turkey | I am from Turkey.
turco | Turk, Turkish | Ali is Turkish.
inglés | Englishman, English | Do you speak English?
el idioma | language, tongue | I speak three languages.
el país | country | What country are you from?
México | Mexico | Diego is from Mexico.
Latinoamérica | Latin America | I am traveling through Latin America.
extranjero | foreigner, foreign | I am a foreigner here.
## Question words
qué | what | What is this?
quién | who | Who is she?
dónde | where | Where do you live?
cuándo | when | When is the class?
cómo | how | How are you?
cuánto | how much, how many | How much does it cost?
cuál | which, what | What is your number?
por qué | why | Why are you studying Spanish?
adónde | where to | Where are you going?
de dónde | whence, where from | Where are you from?
## Small but important words
y | and | Anna and me.
o | or | Tea or coffee?
pero | but | It's small, but pretty.
porque | because | I study because I like it.
también | also, too | I am a student too.
muy | very | He is very nice.
bien | well, good | You speak very well.
mal | badly, bad | I am doing poorly today.
aquí | here | I live here.
allí | there | My house is there.
## Demonstratives and possessives
este | this [nearby masculine] | This is my brother.
ese | that [masculine] | Who is that boy?
aquel | that over there [masculine] | That building over there is new.
esto | this [thing] | What is this? It's a gift.
eso | that [thing] | That is true.
mi | my | My house is small.
tu | your [singular] | What is your mother's name?
su | his, her, your [formal], their | His car is red.
nuestro | our | Our apartment is here.
vuestro | your [plural informal, Spain] | Is this your dog?
## Understanding and speaking
hablar | to speak, to talk | I speak Turkish and Spanish.
entender (entiendo) | to understand | I don't understand this word.
saber (sé) | to know [fact] | I don't know.
repetir (repito) | to repeat | Could you repeat, please?
preguntar | to ask | I want to ask something.
responder | to answer, to reply | Answer in Spanish.
la palabra | word | It is a new word.
la pregunta | question | I have a question.
despacio | slowly | Speak more slowly, please.
¿cómo se dice? | how do you say? | How do you say "kedi" in Spanish?
## Forms of address and daily phrases
el señor | Mr., gentleman | Good morning, Mr. Ruiz.
la señora | Mrs., lady | Mrs. Pérez is a teacher.
la señorita | Miss, young lady | Thank you, miss.
¿cómo estás? | how are you? [informal] | Hello Maria, how are you?
¿y tú? | and you? [informal] | I'm fine, and you?
más o menos | so-so, more or less | How are you? — So-so.
nos vemos | see you | See you on Monday.
hasta pronto | see you soon | Goodbye, see you soon!
¡claro! | of course, sure | Will you help me? — Of course!
¡buena suerte! | good luck | Tomorrow is the exam. Good luck!
