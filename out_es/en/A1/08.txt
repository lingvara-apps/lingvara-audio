# UNIT: Basic verbs and daily life | Most frequent verbs, daily routine, conjunctions
## Basic verbs
pensar | to think | I think about my vacation.
querer | to want, to love | I want a glass of water.
poder | to be able to, can | Can you help me, please?
comprender | to understand, to comprehend | He understands the teacher's explanation.
reconocer | to recognize, to admit | I admit my mistake in this matter.
creer | to believe, to think | I think you are right.
decir | to say, to tell | What do you say?
ver | to see | I see a pretty house on the hill.
dar | to give | I am giving a gift to my friend.
hallar | to find | I find an old coin on the ground.
## Daily movements and actions
venir | to come | Are you coming with us today?
partir | to leave, to depart | We depart for the coast very early.
entrar | to enter, to go in | We enter the museum together.
subir | to go up, to climb | We get on the bus quickly.
bajar | to go down, to descend | I go down the stairs carefully.
correr | to run | I run through the park every morning.
caminar | to walk | We walk along the main street.
manejar | to drive, to manage | I drive my car to work.
marchar | to leave, to march | We are going on a trip next week.
volver | to return, to come back | We return home in the evening.
## Communication and thought
escribir | to write | I am writing a letter to my grandmother.
leer | to read | I read an interesting book at night.
explicar | to explain | The teacher explains the lesson clearly.
contar | to tell, to count | I tell a funny story to the children.
escuchar | to listen | I listen to classical music in my room.
llamar | to call | I call my mother on the phone.
contestar | to answer, to reply | I answer all the exam questions.
consultar | to consult, to check | I consult the map to find the address.
percibir | to perceive, to sense | I perceive a pleasant smell in the kitchen.
aprender | to learn | We learn new words every day.
## Work and school
trabajar | to work | We work eight hours a day.
estudiar | to study | You study Spanish with great enthusiasm.
ayudar | to help | I help my father in the garden.
adquirir | to acquire, to purchase | I acquire new clothes for the winter.
obsequiar | to gift, to present | They give a free map to the customers.
abonar | to pay, to credit | I pay the amount with a credit card.
buscar | to look for, to search | I am looking for a good history book.
encontrar | to find | We find a quiet place to rest.
iniciar | to start, to initiate | The movie starts at the cinema.
terminar | to finish, to end | We finish work very late.
## Leisure and relaxation
descansar | to rest | I rest for a while on the sofa.
jugar | to play | We play football on the weekends.
cantar | to sing | You sing a very cheerful song.
bailar | to dance | We dance traditional music at the party.
nadar | to swim | I swim in the municipal pool.
pasear | to stroll, to take a walk | We stroll along the riverbank.
traza | to trace, to draw | I draw a sketch on the paper.
elaborar | to prepare, to make | You prepare a delicious dinner for everyone.
degustar | to taste, to sample | We taste typical dishes at noon.
sorber | to sip, to slurp | You sip some hot soup.
## Emotions and conditions
sentir | to feel, to be sorry | I am very sorry about what happened.
agradar | to please, to be grateful for | I appreciate the help of my good friends.
fascinar | to fascinate, to love | They love visiting ancient monuments.
interesar | to interest | The culture of this country interests us.
importar | to matter, to care | I don't mind waiting a little bit.
parecer | to seem, to look like | It seems like an excellent idea to me.
concertar | to arrange, to schedule | I schedule an appointment with the doctor today.
faltar | to lack, to be missing | There is little time left for the trip.
suceder | to happen, to occur | What is happening in the street?
completar | to complete | I complete the exercise without errors.
## Adverbs of time and quantity
habitualmente | usually, habitually | I usually eat bread with butter for breakfast.
jamás | never | I never travel without my passport.
diariamente | daily | We read the news daily.
ocasionalmente | occasionally | I occasionally read before sleeping.
mucho | a lot, much | You talk a lot on the phone.
poco | little, few | I sleep little during the week.
bastante | quite, enough | I have quite a bit of work today.
demasiado | too much, excessively | It is too hot in summer.
pronto | soon, early | Please return home soon.
demorado | late | We arrived late to the meeting.
## Conjunctions and transition words
e | and [used before i/hi] | I buy bread and figs at the shop.
u | or [used before o/ho] | Do you prefer one or the other?
mas | but [literary] | I am hungry, but there is no food.
puesto que | since, because | I am not going because I am tired.
igualmente | likewise, equally | I want to go to the cinema as well.
tampoco | neither, not either | I don't speak French, nor Italian either.
así | like this, thus | I do the exercise like this.
entonces | then, so | Then, let's go together.
luego | then, afterwards | First I read, then I sleep.
además | furthermore, besides | Besides, the museum is free.
## Location and direction adverbs
acá | here [this side] | I have been living here for two years.
allá | there [that side] | I leave my books there on the table.
próximo | near, close | My house is close to the park.
distante | distant, far | The airport is far from the city.
dentro | inside, within | The children are playing inside the house.
fuera | outside | We wait outside the building.
arriba | upstairs, above | The upstairs floor is very bright.
abajo | downstairs, below | The cat sleeps downstairs on the floor.
delante | in front | The car is in front of the garage.
detrás | behind | There is a big garden behind the house.
## Question words and details
cuyo | whose | Whose book is this?
interrogativo | interrogative | It is an interrogative tone.
cuáles | which ones | Which are your favorite colors?
donde | where | The house where you keep the keys.
adonde | where to | Where are you going this evening?
cuando | when | When does the course start?
como | how | How you prepare this dish is an art.
monto | amount, total | The total amount is high.
por lo cual | which is why, therefore | That is why you study Spanish.
cuantos | how many | How many brothers do you have?
