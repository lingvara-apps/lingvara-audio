# UNIT: Nuances of synonymous verbs | Looking, speaking, walking, laughing... subtle differences
## Distinctions in verbs of looking
contemplar | gaze at, contemplate | He prefers to contemplate the sea horizon.
mirar | look at | She decided to look straight ahead.
atisbar | catch a glimpse of, peer at | He managed to catch a glimpse of a distant figure.
divisar | descry, make out from afar | From the hill you can make out the sea.
otear | scout, scan from above | Pirates used to scan the horizon from the mast.
asomarse | lean out, look out [pronominal] | He usually leans out the balcony in the afternoon.
espiar | spy on, watch secretly | Curious neighbors try to spy on the neighbor.
atesar | tighten, haul taut | Sailors must tighten the ropes well.
fisgonear | prying, snooping around | The student started snooping around in the drawer.
oquedad | cavity, hollow [noun] | They found a small hollow in the rock.
