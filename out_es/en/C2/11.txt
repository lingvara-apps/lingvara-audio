# UNIT: Expressions triggering the subjunctive | ojalá, es posible que, aunque…
## Probability and wishes
ojalá | hopefully, I wish | Hopefully the weather will be good tomorrow.
quiera_dios | God willing, may God grant | God willing, everything will turn out well.
mal_grado | reluctantly, grudgingly | He acts reluctantly even though they pay him.
supóngase | suppose that, let's assume | Let's suppose that we don't have time.
temer_que | to fear that, to be afraid that | I fear that they will arrive late.
desear_que | to wish that, to desire that | I want you to pass the exam.
esperar_que | to hope that | I hope that you understand me.
necesitar_que | to need someone to | I need you to help me today.
pedir_que | to ask that, to request that | I ask that you keep quiet.
exigir_que | to demand that | I demand that they give me an explanation.
## Doubt and probability
dudar_que | to doubt that | I doubt that they will come today.
negar_que | to deny that | I deny that they have stolen anything.
improbabilidad | improbability, unlikelihood | The improbability of it happening is high.
cabalgar_dudalar | to be plagued by doubts, to linger (of doubts) | Doubts linger over whether we will win.
suponer_que | to suppose that, to assume that | Let's suppose that it rains all afternoon.
creer_posible | to think possible, to consider possible | I don't think it's possible that they finish soon.
parecer_mentira | to seem unbelievable, to be hard to believe | It seems incredible that they don't know this.
resultar_dudoso | to seem doubtful, to appear dubious | It seems doubtful that they will accept the offer.
ser_improbable | to be unlikely, to be improbable | It is unlikely that it will snow in July.
haber_indicios | to be indications, there to be signs | There are indications that they are lying.
## Emotion and reaction
alegrarse_de_que | to be glad that, to be pleased that | I am glad that you are here.
sentir_que | to be sorry that, to regret that | I am sorry that you are feeling unwell.
sorprenderse_de_que | to be surprised that | It surprises me that you don't greet me.
molestarse_de_que | to be annoyed that, to be bothered that | It bothers me that they talk so much.
temor_fundado | well-founded fear, justified fear | There is a well-founded fear that they will fail.
lamentar_que | to lament that, to bemoan that | I regret that you have left.
extrañarse_de_que | to find it strange that, to wonder that | I find it strange that no one answers.
entristecerse_de_que | to be saddened that | She is saddened that they ignore her.
indignarse_de_que | to be outraged that, to be indignant that | He is outraged that they lie like that.
gustar_que | to like that | I like that you read so much.
## Orders and prohibitions
mandar_que | to command that, to order that | He orders that you clean the living room.
prohibir_que | to forbid that, to prohibit | I forbid smoking here.
impedir_que | to prevent from, to impede | They prevent us from entering without a pass.
aconsejar_que | to advise that | I advise that you rest a little.
sugerir_que | to suggest that | I suggest that we go to the cinema.
recomendar_que | to recommend that | I recommend that you read this book.
permitir_que | to allow that, to permit | They allow us to play outside.
rogar_que | to beg that, to plead that | I beg that you forgive me.
insistir_que | to insist that | They insist that we pay now.
proponer_que | to propose that | I propose that we start right now.
## Evaluation and judgment
ser_bueno_que | to be good that | It is good that you practice every day.
ser_necesario_que | to be necessary that | It is necessary that you sign today.
ser_importante_que | to be important that | It is important that you arrive on time.
ser_mejor_que | to be better that | It is better that we leave now.
ser_conveniente_que | to be advisable that, to be fitting that | It is advisable that you study more.
ser_justo_que | to be fair that, to be just that | It is fair that they reward your effort.
ser_lógico_que | to be logical that | It is logical that you have doubts.
ser_extraño_que | to be strange that | It is strange that they haven't called.
ser_evidente_que | to be evident that, to be obvious that | It is obvious that you always lie.
ser_cierto_que | to be true that | It is true that they know the truth.
## Condition and conjunctions
salvo_que_llueva | unless it rains | We will go unless it rains heavily.
con_tal_de_que | provided that, as long as | I will give you the money provided that you pay.
supuesto_que_llueva | in case it rains, supposing it rains | In case it rains, stay here.
sin_que | without | He left without anyone seeing him.
antes_de_que | before | Come back before it gets dark.
para_que | so that, in order that | He studied hard so that he would pass.
a_pesar_de_que | even though, although | They came even though it was snowing.
por_mucho_que | no matter how much | No matter how much you insist, I will say no.
cualquiera_que | whoever, anyone who | Whoever comes will be welcome.
dondequiera_que | wherever | I will go wherever you call me.
## Time and the future
en_cuanto | as soon as, once | As soon as you arrive, let me know.
hasta_que | until | I will wait until you finish.
tan_pronto_como | the instant that, as soon as | As soon as day breaks, we set off.
después_de_que | after | After we eat, we will go out.
a_medida_que | as, gradually as | You grow as you read more.
mientras | while | Make dinner while I work.
siempre_que | as long as, whenever | We will go as long as it is sunny.
en_el_momento_que | the moment that | Call me the moment you arrive.
apenas | scarcely, hardly | Scarcely does day break, we will set off.
luego_que | right after, immediately after | Right after I finish, I'll call you.
## Negative imperative forms
no_hagas | don't do, don't make | Don't make noise when entering.
no_digas | don't say, don't tell | Don't tell lies to your parents.
no_vayas | don't go | Don't go alone at night.
no_seas | don't be | Don't be so impatient today.
no_tengas | don't have | Don't be afraid to ask.
no_salgas | don't go out, don't leave | Don't go out into the cold without a coat.
no_comas | don't eat | Don't eat so many sweets in a row.
no_duermas | don't sleep | Don't sleep during class.
no_pongas | don't put, don't place | Don't put your feet on the table.
no_escuches | don't listen to | Don't listen to bad advice.
## Subjunctive and counterfactuals
ojalá_hubiera_sido | if only it had been, I wish it had been | If only it had been easier.
si_fuera | if I were, if it were | If I were rich, I would travel more.
si_hubiera_sabido | if I had known | If I had known, I wouldn't have come.
como_si_fuera | as if it were, as if one were | He speaks as if he were the boss.
caso_omiso | turning a blind eye, disregarding | They ignore what I say.
quiera_el_cielo | heaven grant, may heaven wish | Heaven grant that we see each other again.
valga_la_pena | to be worth it, may it be worth the trouble | I want the effort to be worth it.
dado_el_caso | in that case, if that is the case | In the event that it is missing, let me know.
quede_entre_nous | between us, strictly between us | Let this secret stay between us.
sea_como_sea | either way, be that as it may | Whatever happens, we will do it well.
## Alternative conjunctions
en_cualquier_caso | in any case, at any rate | In any case, we will support you.
por_si_acaso | just in case | Take an umbrella just in case it rains.
a_efectos_de_que | with the intent that, for the purpose of | I speak loudly so that they can hear.
con_la_mira_puesta | with an eye toward, aiming at | He works with his sights set on promotion.
de_modo_que | in such a way that, so that | He studied a lot, so he passed.
a_fin_de_que | in order that, so that | I came so that you could explain it to me.
no_obstante_que | notwithstanding that, even though | They came even though it was cold.
en_tanto_cuanto | to the extent that, as long as | I will help you as long as you can.
a_cambio_de_que | in return for, in exchange for | I will look after the dog in exchange for you traveling.
bajo_condición_de_que | on condition that, under the condition that | You will enter on condition that you pay.
