# UNIT: Street language | Informal rough expressions, interjections (no swearing)
## Rebuke and reaction
recalcitrante | stubborn, obstinate | That boy is very stubborn.
nefario | heinous, nefarious | It seems like a heinous act to me.
majadero | foolish, annoying | Please don't be so foolish.
mendaz | mendacious, lying | His testimony turned out to be mendacious.
zafio | crude, unrefined | His behavior was very crude.
inane | pointless, vacuous | They made a pointless comment.
necio | foolish, stupid | Don't argue with a fool.
memo | dimwit, dopey | He stood there looking like a dopey dimwit.
vacuo | empty, vacuous | It was an extremely vacuous speech.
verecundo | bashful, timid | He was a very timid young man.
## Slang and forms of address
chabacano | tacky, vulgar | That party turned out to be very tacky.
patán | boor, lout | He behaved like an absolute boor.
mastuerzo | jerk, fool [slang] | Stop talking nonsense, jerk!
tontaina | silly, gullible | That silly person fell down again.
pelma | bore, pain [in the neck] | What a bore you are being today.
pesado | annoying, pushy | Don't be so pushy with me.
borde | rude, unpleasant | The shop assistant was very rude.
ufano | arrogant, smug | He has a very smug attitude.
pasmarote | stunned, dumbfounded | He stood stunned upon seeing him.
gorrón | freeloading, sponger | He hates freeloading people.
## Surprise and interjections
recontra | extremely, super | I am extremely tired today.
carambas | gosh, goodness me | Goodness me, what a surprise!
arre | giddy-up, mush | Giddy-up, horse, faster!
ox | shoo, get away | Shoo, get out of here!
eco | exactly, right you are | Exactly, I was thinking just that!
tate | hold on, aha | Aha, I know where it is now!
chisum | gossipy [regional] | He is a very gossipy neighbor.
zote | ignorant, blockhead | He turned out to be a total blockhead.
tontoelhabas | silly goose, jerk [humorous] | Don't be a jerk, man.
soplagaitas | bungler, idiot | That bungler ruined everything.
## Expressions of anger
aburar | to irritate, to burn up | His attitude irritates me.
zaherir | to taunt, to needle | He likes to needle others.
denostar | to revile, to disparage | He started to disparage without reason.
escarnecer | to mock, to scorn | You must not scorn the weak.
vilipendiar | to revile, to disparage | He tried to disparage her work.
motejar | to nickname, to brand | He usually brands his colleagues with nicknames.
imprecar | to curse, to rail | He started to rail out loud.
proferir | to utter, to spew | He managed to utter severe insults.
fustigar | to chastise, to lambast | The press is going to lambast the plan.
execrar | to detest, to execrate | You must detest such actions.
## Suspudicion and scorn
desacreditar | to discredit, to defame | They want to discredit his reputation.
menospreciar | to belittle, to scorn | You shouldn't belittle his advice.
despreciar | to spurn, to reject | She decided to reject his offer.
infamar | to defame, to slander | They seek to defame her good name.
ultrajar | to outrage, to desecrate | They are going to desecrate the memorial.
vejar | to humiliate, to harass | No one has the right to humiliate others.
mortificar | to torment, to vex | He likes to vex his brother.
fadiga | fatigue, distress [regional] | He feels a lot of mental distress.
zacapela | ruckus, brawl | A huge brawl broke out.
bronca | scolding, row | Her boss gave her a severe scolding.
## Everyday reactions
arrechucho | sudden ailment, shivers | He had a sudden mild spell of illness.
zarpazo | claw blow, financial blow | He suffered a heavy financial blow.
tarantantantán | beating, spanking [playful] | He earned a good spanking.
atrabiliario | cranky, ill-tempered | He has a cranky disposition.
desaborido | dull, unpleasant | It was a very unpleasant treatment.
zonzorrión | sluggish, doltish | Don't be so sluggish today.
papanatas | gullible, nincompoop | He fell for it like a nincompoop.
ademán | gesture, movement | He made a gesture of contempt.
desaire | snub, slight | He took her silence as a slight.
desplante | insolence, snub | Her answer was a total snub.
## Harsh outbursts
zahid | ascetic, austere | He leads a very austere life.
botarate | reckless spender, scatterbrain | That young man is a scatterbrain.
calavera | rake, wild liver | He lived like an old rake.
perdulario | slovenly, disheveled | He is a very slovenly man.
zopenco | thickheaded, dolt | Please don't be so thickheaded.
zambombo | clumsy, loutish | He acted like a clumsy lout.
tiquismiquis | fussy, finicky | You are way too fussy.
pedigüeño | beggar-like, constantly asking | I detest people who are always begging for things.
petulante | smug, pretentious | His tone was very pretentious.
pedante | pedantic, know-it-all | He speaks with a pedantic air.
## Mildly mocking words
fariseo | hypocrite, pharisaical | Don't be a lying hypocrite.
zorrastrón | crafty, sly fox | He is an old sly fox.
pícaro | cheeky, rogue | He acted in a very cheeky way.
chismoso | gossipy, nosy | He is a very gossipy employee.
rajado | cowardly, chicken | He ran away like a chicken upon seeing the problem.
gallito | cocky, swaggering | Don't go swaggering around here.
fantoche | show-off, poseur | He was a pathetic, ridiculous show-off.
fanfarrón | braggart, show-off | He is an unbearable braggart.
chafardero | meddlesome, mischief-maker | Don't be so meddlesome.
zorrero | crafty, shifty | He proved to be a shifty guy.
## Reaction and interjections
rebufar | to snort, to puff | He started to snort with rage.
resollar | to wheeze, to gasp | He arrived alone without even gasping.
bufar | to fume, to rage | The boss started to fume today.
refunfuñar | to grumble, to mutter | He tends to grumble about everything.
berrear | to bawl, to squall | The child started to bawl.
chillar | to scream, to yell | There's no need to yell so much.
vociferar | to vociferate, to shout | He started to shout in the square.
protestar | to protest, to complain | He has a habit of complaining.
patalear | to stamp one's feet, to throw a tantrum | He started to throw a tantrum like a child.
polemizar | to argue, to polemicize | I don't want to argue over nonsense.
## Closing and slang labels
chabacanería | tackiness, vulgarity | I hate such tackiness.
grosería | crudeness, obscenity | He made an unacceptable crude remark.
impertinencia | impertinence, cheek | He committed a major impertinence.
insolencia | insolence, cheek | I cannot tolerate such insolence.
desvergüenza | shamelessness, effrontery | He acted with total shamelessness.
desacato | contempt [of court/authority], insubordination | He was charged with contempt.
desfachatez | audacity, brazenness | What tremendous brazenness he has.
barbaridad | outrage, monstrosity | He said a monstrous thing.
fatualdad | foolishness, stupidity | Don't speak any more foolishness.
estupidez | stupidity, nonsense | It was total stupidity.
