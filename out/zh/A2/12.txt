## 连词一
however | 然而，不过 | The hotel was nice. However, it was expensive.
though | 尽管，不过 | The film was long. I enjoyed it, though.
because of | 因为，由于 | The match was cancelled because of the rain.
thanks to | 多亏，由于 | We arrived early thanks to you.
as if | 好像，仿佛 | He looks as if he hasn't slept.
rather than | 而不是 | I'd like tea rather than coffee.
instead | 代替，顶替 | There was no rice, so we had pasta instead.
in fact | 事实上，其实 | I'm not tired. In fact, I feel great.
actually | 实际上，其实 | Actually, my name is Sofia, not Sonia.
what's more | 并且，更为重要的是 | The flat is big and, what's more, it's cheap.
## 连词二
either ... or | 要么……要么…… | You can either walk or take the bus.
neither ... nor | 既不……也不…… | Neither Ali nor Emre came to the party.
both ... and | 既……又……，不但……而且…… | Both my mother and my father are teachers.
instead of | 代替，而不是 | Let's walk instead of taking a taxi.
in order to | 为了，以便 | He moved to London in order to study.
so as to | 为了（较正式） | We left early so as to avoid the traffic.
firstly | 首先，第一 | Firstly, I'd like to thank you all.
finally | 最后，终于 | Finally, don't forget your homework.
for example | 例如 | I love fruit, for example apples and pears.
at first | 起初，最初 | At first I didn't like the city.
