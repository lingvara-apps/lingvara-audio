minimalist | 极简主义的 | Camille prefers minimalist interiors with bare walls and very little furniture.
ornate | 华丽的，装饰复杂的 | The theatre's ornate balconies were restored using the original drawings.

## 承重系统

beam | 梁 | Cracks appeared along the main beam after the extra floor was added.
slab | 楼板，平板 | The engineers checked the slab thickness before approving the heavy machinery.
footing | 基础，基础底座 | Each column rests on a reinforced footing about two metres wide.
cantilever | 悬臂 | The balcony is a cantilever that projects three metres beyond the wall.
lintel | 过梁 | A concrete lintel spans the top of the new doorway.
truss | 桁架 | Timber trusses support the roof of the sports hall without any internal columns.
shear wall | 剪力墙 | Shear walls help the tower resist strong winds and earthquakes.
deflection | 挠度，变形 | The deflection of the floor under full load stayed within acceptable limits.
load-bearing capacity | 承载能力 | Engineers tested the load-bearing capacity of the old bridge before reopening it.

girder | 钢大梁 | A huge girder was lifted into place above the shopping centre entrance.
