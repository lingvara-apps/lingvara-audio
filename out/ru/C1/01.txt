# UNIT: Академический дискурс | Научное письмо, аргументы, академические шаблоны
## Академический процесс
monograph | монография | She published a monograph on medieval trade routes across the North Sea.
dissertation | диссертация | Omar spent four years writing his dissertation on coastal erosion.
peer review | рецензирование | The article finally passed peer review after two rounds of revisions.
viva | защита диссертации | Her viva is scheduled for the second week of June.
tenure | постоянная должность (в вузе) | After ten years of teaching, he was finally granted tenure.
citation | цитата, цитирование | Every citation must follow the style required by the journal.
bibliography | список литературы, библиография | The bibliography runs to more than forty pages.
footnote | сноска | The author explains the term in a short footnote on page twelve.
appendix (appendices) | приложение | The full questionnaire is included in the appendix at the end.
symposium (symposia) | симпозиум | Yuki presented her early findings at an international symposium in Lisbon.
## Логика и аргументы
line of reasoning | ход мыслей, логика рассуждения | I find it hard to follow her line of reasoning in the second chapter.
inference | умозаключение, вывод | The inference he draws from such limited data seems rather bold.
logical leap | логический скачок | Moving from one survey to a universal law is a huge logical leap.
induction | индукция | Most scientific laws are reached through induction from repeated observations.
syllogism | силлогизм | The lecturer opened the class with a classic syllogism about mortality.
fallacy | логическая ошибка, софизм | Blaming the speaker instead of the idea is a common fallacy.
sweeping generalisation | огульное обобщение | Saying that all students dislike statistics is a sweeping generalisation.
red herring | отвлекающий маневр | The question about funding was a red herring that distracted from the real issue.
corollary | прямое следствие, логический вывод | Rising prices are a natural corollary of the shortage.
tautology | тавтология | Saying that free gifts cost nothing is a tautology.
## Методология исследования
methodology | методология | The reviewers questioned the methodology rather than the results.
hypothesis (hypotheses) | гипотеза | The data did not support our original hypothesis.
variable | переменная | We controlled for age, income and education as separate variables.
sample size | объем выборки | With such a small sample size, the results cannot be generalised.
control group | контрольная группа | Participants in the control group received no treatment at all.
longitudinal | лонгитюдный, продольный | A longitudinal study followed the same families for twenty years.
qualitative | качественный | The project combines qualitative interviews with a large online survey.
quantitative | количественный | Quantitative methods alone could not explain why people changed their habits.
replicate | воспроизводить (эксперимент) | Other labs have so far failed to replicate the experiment.
fieldwork | полевое исследование | Diego did his fieldwork in small fishing villages along the coast.
## Данные и анализ
dataset | набор данных | The researchers made their entire dataset publicly available.
variance | дисперсия, вариация | The variance between the two groups was larger than the team had expected.
causation | причинно-следственная связь | The authors are careful not to confuse correlation with causation.
outlier | выброс, аномальное значение | One outlier distorted the average, so we removed it from the analysis.
statistically significant | статистически значимый | The difference between the two groups was not statistically significant.
margin of error | погрешность, ошибка выборки | The poll has a margin of error of three percentage points.
standard deviation | стандартное отклонение | A small standard deviation means that most results lie close to the average.
skew | искажать | A few extremely rich households can skew the income figures.
extrapolate | экстраполировать | It is risky to extrapolate from one season's data to the next decade.
anomaly | аномалия | At first, the team dismissed the strange reading as a minor anomaly.
## Глаголы утверждения и доказательства
posit | утверждать, полагать | The theory posits that language shapes the way we perceive colour.
purport | претендовать, заявлять | The study purports to show a link between sleep and memory.
bear out (bore, borne) | подтверждать | The latest findings bear out what Yuki suspected years ago.
corroborate | подкреплять, подтверждать | Two independent witnesses corroborate her version of events.
invalidate | опровергать, делать недействительным | A single error in the sampling could invalidate the entire experiment.
bolster | подкреплять, усиливать | Diego added three new case studies to bolster his argument.
attest | свидетельствовать, подтверждать | Hundreds of surviving letters attest to their long friendship.
allege | утверждать (без доказательств) | Critics allege that the figures were adjusted to look more favourable.
vindicate | оправдывать, доказывать правоту | The latest findings seem to vindicate her controversial theory.
debunk | разоблачать | The documentary sets out to debunk several popular myths about diet.
## Глаголы анализа и интерпретации
scrutinise | тщательно изучать | The committee will scrutinise every page of the final report.
elucidate | прояснять, разъяснять | The second chapter elucidates the link between climate and migration.
delineate | описывать, очерчивать | The introduction clearly delineates the scope of the study.
juxtapose | сопоставлять | The author juxtaposes urban and rural attitudes towards family.
synthesise | синтезировать | A good review should synthesise decades of research into a clear picture.
conceptualise | концептуализировать | The author conceptualises memory as a social rather than an individual process.
construe | истолковывать, трактовать | Her silence was construed as agreement by the rest of the committee.
expound | излагать, подробно объяснять | In his final lecture, he expounded his views on moral responsibility.
problematise | проблематизировать | Recent studies problematise the simple division between nature and culture.
contextualise | рассматривать в контексте | It is important to contextualise these letters within the politics of the time.
## Теория и концепции
paradigm | парадигма | Quantum theory marked a profound shift in the scientific paradigm.
framework | рамки, структура | The study uses a theoretical framework borrowed from economics.
construct | конструкт | Many sociologists see childhood as a social construct.
axiom | аксиома | The whole system rests on a handful of simple axioms.
postulate | постулат | The second postulate of the theory remains highly controversial.
dichotomy | дихотомия | The book challenges the false dichotomy between reason and emotion.
nuance | нюанс | The translation loses much of the nuance of the original poem.
discourse | дискурс | Environmental issues now dominate public discourse in many countries.
epistemology | эпистемология, теория познания | Her course on epistemology asks how we can know anything for certain.
taxonomy | таксономия, классификация | The researchers proposed a new taxonomy of learning difficulties.
## Осторожный язык
arguably | возможно, пожалуй | This is arguably the most important discovery of the century.
ostensibly | якобы, по видимости | The meeting was ostensibly about funding, but nobody mentioned money.
presumably | предположительно | The manuscript was presumably written in the early eighteenth century.
tentative | предварительный | These are only tentative conclusions based on the first year of data.
speculative | умозрительный, предположительный | At this stage, any explanation for the pattern remains largely speculative.
conceivably | вполне возможно | The effect could conceivably be stronger in younger children.
to some extent | в некоторой степени | To some extent, the results confirm what teachers had long suspected.
it would appear that | судя по всему, по-видимому | It would appear that the virus spreads more slower in dry weather.
by and large | в целом, по большому счету | By and large, the students responded positively to the new course.
caveat | оговорка | One important caveat is that all participants were university students.
## Академические прилагательные
empirical | эмпирический | There is little empirical evidence to support this popular claim.
rigorous | строгий, тщательный | The new drug went through rigorous testing before it was approved.
salient | бросающийся в глаза, главный | The most salient feature of the data is the sharp drop in 2009.
cogent | убедительный | She made a cogent case for changing the way we measure poverty.
seminal | эпохальный, основополагающий | His seminal paper on memory is still cited by thousands of researchers.
inconclusive | неубедительный, безрезультатный | The results of the trial were inconclusive, so a larger study is planned.
interdisciplinary | междисциплинарный | The centre encourages interdisciplinary research between biology and computing.
peripheral | второстепенный | These questions are peripheral to the main argument of the book.
robust | надежный, убедительный | The findings proved robust across several different countries.
spurious | ложный, мнимый | The apparent link between the two trends turned out to be spurious.
## Академические шаблоны
in light of | в свете | In light of these findings, the policy should be reconsidered.
with regard to | в отношении, касательно | With regard to funding, the university has promised further support.
notwithstanding | несмотря на | Notwithstanding its limitations, the study offers valuable insights.
vis-a-vis | по отношению к, в сравнении с | The paper examines the position of small states vis-a-vis their larger neighbours.
inter alia | среди прочего | The report covers, inter alia, housing, transport and public health.
it is worth noting that | стоит отметить, что | It is worth noting that the survey was carried out before the crisis.
to put it another way | иными словами | To put it another way, the cheaper option may cost more in the long run.
as outlined above | как указано выше | As outlined above, the method has three main stages.
this paper argues that | в данной статье утверждается, что | This paper argues that early bilingualism improves concentration.
further research is needed | требуются дальнейшие исследования | Further research is needed to confirm these results in older adults.
