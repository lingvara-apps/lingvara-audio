## Научный метод
observation | наблюдение | Careful observation is the first step of any good study.
testable | проверяемый | A scientific idea must be testable, or it is only an opinion.
falsify | опровергать | A good theory makes predictions that could, in principle, be falsified.
conjecture | предположение | For now, the link between the two events is pure conjecture.
reproducible | воспроизводимый | Results are only trusted when they are reproducible in other labs.
measurement | измерение | We took three measurements and used the average.
precision | точность | This instrument measures temperature with remarkable precision.
accuracy | правильность, точность | Nobody questioned the accuracy of her calculations.
double-blind | двойной слепой | In a double-blind study, neither doctors nor patients know who gets the real drug.
placebo | плацебо | Half of the volunteers were given a placebo instead of the medicine.

calibrate | калибровать | We calibrate the scales every morning before we start work.

## Академическое письмо
paraphrase | перефразировать | Try to paraphrase the quotation instead of copying it word for word.
quote | цитировать | If you quote another writer, always give the page number.
summary (summaries) | резюме, краткое изложение | Write a short summary of the article in your own words.
heading | заголовок | Each section of the report should have a clear heading.
literature review | литературный обзор | Her literature review covers more than fifty earlier studies.
acknowledgements | благодарности | In the acknowledgements, Ali thanked his parents and his supervisor.
thesis statement | тезис, основная мысль | Your thesis statement should appear at the end of the introduction.
word count | количество слов | Check the word count before you hand in your essay.
glossary (glossaries) | глоссарий | Technical terms are explained in the glossary at the back.
publication | публикация | The paper is not yet ready for publication.
## Академические глаголы

demonstrate | демонстрировать | The study demonstrates a clear link between diet and mood.
imply | подразумевать | These results imply that the old model was too simple.
infer | делать вывод | From these figures, we can infer that prices will rise.
assess | оценивать | It is too early to assess the long-term impact of the policy.
derive | выводить | The formula is derived from a few simple observations.
validate | подтверждать, проверять | The model was validated using data from five countries.
undermine | подрывать | One mistake in the data could undermine the whole argument.
establish | устанавливать | Scientists have not yet established what causes the disease.
illustrate | иллюстрировать | The diagram illustrates how the heart pumps blood.
