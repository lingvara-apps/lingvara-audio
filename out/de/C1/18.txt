# UNIT: Formeller Stil | Bürokratische Sprache, formeller Schriftverkehr, Redemittel
## Formeller Schriftverkehr
herewith | anbei, hiermit | Please find herewith a copy of the signed agreement for your records.
enclose | beifügen, beilegen | I enclose a copy of my birth certificate as requested.
covering letter | Anschreiben | Always send a short covering letter with your CV.
the undersigned | die Unterzeichneten | We, the undersigned, request that the library remain open on Sundays.
memorandum (memoranda) | Memorandum, Aktennotiz | The director circulated a memorandum about the new security procedures.
addressee | Empfänger | If the addressee has moved, the letter will be returned to the sender.
letterhead | Briefkopf | The reference must be printed on the university's official letterhead.
carbon copy | Kopie zur Kenntnis | A carbon copy of the decision was sent to the regional office.
acknowledge receipt | den Empfang bestätigen | Please acknowledge receipt of this notice by replying to this email.
in receipt of | im Besitz von | We are in receipt of your letter dated the fourth of March.
## Formelle Verben
commence | beginnen, antreten | Construction work will commence on the first of next month.
terminate | kündigen, beenden | Either party may terminate the contract with thirty days' notice.
endeavour | sich bemühen, streben | We will endeavour to respond to all enquiries within two working days.
ascertain | feststellen, ermitteln | The inspectors are trying to ascertain the cause of the fire.
expedite | beschleunigen | Paying an extra fee will expedite the processing of your visa.
rectify | beheben, berichtigen | The bank has promised to rectify the error as soon as possible.
procure | beschaffen, besorgen | The hospital struggled to procure enough equipment during the winter.
reimburse | erstatten, rückvergüten | The company will reimburse your travel expenses on presentation of receipts.
remit | überweisen | Kindly remit the outstanding amount within fourteen days.
dispatch | versenden, abschicken | Your order was dispatched this morning and should arrive on Friday.
## Bürokratische Abläufe
paperwork | Papierkram, Schriftverkehr | Ali spent the whole afternoon dealing with paperwork for the new office.
directive | Richtlinie, Anweisung | All departments must follow the new directive on data storage.
bylaw | Satzung, Ortsgesetz | A local bylaw forbids cycling on the seafront promenade.
formalities | Formalitäten, Behördengänge | The customs formalities took longer than the flight itself.
documentation | Unterlagen, Dokumentation | Without proper documentation, your application cannot be processed.
submission | Einreichung, Abgabe | The deadline for the submission of proposals is the end of May.
processing fee | Bearbeitungsgebühr | A processing fee of twenty euros is charged for each application.
stipulation | Auflage, Bedingung | The grant came with the stipulation that the results be made public.
provision | Bestimmung, Klausel | A new provision allows employees to work from home two days a week.
inquiry | Untersuchung, Ermittlung | The ministry has launched an inquiry into the delays at the border.
## Antrag und Unterlagen
eligibility | Anspruchsberechtigung, Eignung | Eligibility for the grant depends on your income and place of residence.
waiver | Verzichtserklärung | Participants must sign a waiver before taking part in the climb.
notarize | beglaubigen lassen | The translation must be notarized before you submit it to the consulate.
proof of address | Adressnachweis | A recent utility bill is accepted as proof of address.
dossier | Akte, Dossier | The lawyer prepared a thick dossier on the company's past contracts.
expiry | Ablauf, Verfallsdatum | Check the expiry date on your passport before booking the trip.
exemption | Befreiung | Students on low incomes can apply for an exemption from the fee.
countersign | Gegenzeichnen, mitzeichnen | The form must be countersigned by your line manager.
attestation | Bescheinigung, Attest | The embassy asked for an attestation of her degree from the university.
supporting documents | Belege, unterstützende Dokumente | Upload all supporting documents in a single PDF file.
## Formelle Präpositionen
in accordance with | gemäß, entsprechend | The waste must be disposed of in accordance with local regulations.
thereof | davon, dessen | The tenant is responsible for the property and any part thereof.
whereby | wodurch, wonach | They set up a system whereby staff can report problems anonymously.
prior to | vor, vorher | Passengers must check in at least two hours prior to departure.
in lieu of | anstelle von | Staff may take extra days off in lieu of overtime pay.
subject to | vorbehaltlich, abhängig von | All prices are subject to change without prior notice.
in respect of | bezüglich, hinsichtlich | No payment has been received in respect of the March invoice.
save for | abgesehen von, außer | The building was empty, save for a security guard on the ground floor.
for the purposes of | zum Zwecke von | For the purposes of this agreement, a working day excludes public holidays.
in the absence of | in Abwesenheit von | In the absence of the chair, her deputy will lead the meeting.
## Briefklauseln
please do not hesitate to contact us | zögern Sie nicht, uns zu kontaktieren | If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
we regret to inform you | wir müssen Ihnen leider mitteilen | We regret to inform you that your application has been unsuccessful.
further to | im Anschluss an | Further to our telephone conversation, I am sending the revised schedule.
with reference to | unter Bezugnahme auf | I am writing with reference to the vacancy advertised on your website.
yours faithfully | mit freundlichen Grüßen (bei unbekanntem Empfänger) | The letter began with "Dear Sir or Madam" and ended with "Yours faithfully".
kind regards | herzliche Grüße, mit freundlichen Grüßen | She always ends her work emails with "Kind regards" and her full name.
to whom it may concern | sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, an wen es betrifft | The reference letter was addressed "To whom it may concern".
for your attention | zu Ihrer Kenntnisnahme | I am forwarding this complaint for your attention and further action.
please find attached | im Anhang finden Sie | Please find attached the minutes of yesterday's meeting.
I look forward to hearing from you | ich sehe Ihrer Antwort entgegen | Thank you for considering my application; I look forward to hearing from you.
## Institutionen und Amtspersonen
town clerk | Stadtschreiber, leitender Verwaltungsbeamter | The town clerk keeps the official records of every council meeting.
municipality | Gemeinde, Stadtverwaltung | The municipality is responsible for street lighting and rubbish collection.
commissioner | Kommissar, Beauftragter | The commissioner announced new rules for public transport fares.
bureaucrat | Bürokrat | Sofia complained that a single bureaucrat had delayed her permit for months.
functionary | Funktionär, kleiner Beamter | A minor functionary stamped the papers without even reading them.
secretariat | Sekretariat | Questions about the conference should be sent to the secretariat.
officialdom | Beamtentum, Behördenapparat | Lukas spent weeks battling officialdom to get his residence permit.
inspector | Prüfer, Inspektor | A health inspector visits the restaurant twice a year.
auditor | Wirtschaftsprüfer, Rechnungsprüfer | The external auditor found several gaps in the accounts.
liaison officer | Verbindungsbeamter, Ansprechpartner | Each school has a liaison officer who works with the local council.
## Formelle Adjektive
mandatory | obligatorisch, verpflichtend | Wearing a helmet on the construction site is mandatory.
provisional | provisorisch, vorläufig | The dates are provisional and may still change.
statutory | gesetzlich vorgeschrieben | Employees are entitled to twenty days of statutory annual leave.
administrative | administrativ, verwaltungstechnisch | Half of the budget goes on administrative costs.
pertinent | sachdienlich, relevant | Please include only the information that is pertinent to your claim.
requisite | erforderlich, notwendig | Few candidates had the requisite experience for the position.
applicable | anwendbar, geltend | Please tick the box if this condition is applicable to you.
discretionary | nach Ermessen, freiwillig | The manager has discretionary power to approve small expenses.
ineligible | nicht berechtigt, unzulässig | Applicants who miss the deadline are ineligible for funding.
pending | ausstehend, hängig | His application is still pending, so he cannot travel yet.
## Sitzungsordnung
agenda | Tagesordnung | The new parking rules are the first item on the agenda.
matters arising | aus dem Protokoll entstehende Punkte | Under matters arising, the treasurer reported on last month's repairs.
convene | einberufen, zusammenrufen | The board will convene an emergency meeting on Monday.
apologies for absence | Entschuldigungen für das Fernbleiben | The secretary read out the apologies for absence before the meeting began.
unanimous | einstimmig | The decision to close the old bridge was unanimous.
any other business | Verschiedenes (am Ende einer Sitzung) | Under any other business, Emre raised the issue of the broken lift.
point of order | Geschäftsordnungsantrag | She rose on a point of order to question the voting procedure.
proxy | Stellvertreter, Vollmacht | If you cannot attend, you may vote by proxy.
standing orders | Geschäftsordnung | Under the standing orders, each speaker has five minutes.
casting vote | Stichentscheid, ausschlaggebende Stimme | With the votes tied, the chair used her casting vote.
## Formelle Adverbien und Fristen
duly | ordnungsgemäß | The form was duly signed and returned before the deadline.
forthwith | unverzüglich, sofort | Any employee breaking these rules will be dismissed forthwith.
expressly | ausdrücklich | Smoking is expressly forbidden in all parts of the building.
hereafter | nachfolgend, im Folgenden | The company, hereafter called "the employer", agrees to the following terms.
with immediate effect | mit sofortiger Wirkung | The director has resigned with immediate effect.
until further notice | bis auf Weiteres | The museum will remain closed until further notice.
in due course | zu gegebener Zeit | You will be informed of the result in due course.
as of | mit Wirkung ab, ab | As of the first of January, all fees will be paid online.
regrettably | bedauerlicherweise | Regrettably, we are unable to offer you a place this year.
kindly | höflich, bitte | Visitors are kindly requested to switch off their mobile phones.
