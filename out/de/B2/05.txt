## Wissenschaftliche Methode

testable | überprüfbar, testbar | A scientific idea must be testable, or it is only an opinion.
falsify | falsifizieren, widerlegen | A good theory makes predictions that could, in principle, be falsified.
conjecture | Vermutung, Spekulation | For now, the link between the two events is pure conjecture.
reproducible | reproduzierbar | Results are only trusted when they are reproducible in other labs.
measurement | Messung | We took three measurements and used the average.
precision | Präzision, Genauigkeit | This instrument measures temperature with remarkable precision.
accuracy | Genauigkeit, Richtigkeit | Nobody questioned the accuracy of her calculations.
double-blind | doppelblind | In a double-blind study, neither doctors nor patients know who gets the real drug.
placebo | Placebo, Scheinmedikament | Half of the volunteers were given a placebo instead of the medicine.
