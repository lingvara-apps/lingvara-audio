# UNIT: Rhetorik und Diskussion | Debatte, Überredungskunst, Medienanalyse
## Grundlagen der Debatte
motion | Antrag, Diskussionsgegenstand | The motion for tonight's debate concerns banning cars from the city centre.
rebuttal | Gegenargument, Widerlegung | Her rebuttal exposed several weaknesses in the other team's case.
proponent | Befürworter, Anhänger | Proponents of the plan say it will reduce traffic considerably.
moderator | Moderator, Diskussionsleiter | The moderator reminded both speakers to respect the time limit.
concede | zugeben, einräumen | Diego conceded that his opponent had made a fair point.
counterargument | Gegenargument, Einwand | A strong essay should address at least one counterargument.
premise | Prämisse, Annahme | The whole argument rests on a false premise.
stance | Haltung, Standpunkt | What is your stance on working from home?
contend | behaupten, geltend machen | Critics contend that the new law will hurt small businesses.
refute | widerlegen | Camille refuted every claim with clear evidence and calm logic.
## Argumente aufbauen
assertion | Behauptung | His assertion was not supported by any reliable data.
substantiate | untermauern, belegen | Can you substantiate that claim with real figures?
validity | Gültigkeit, Validität | Several experts questioned the validity of the survey results.
coherent | schlüssig, kohärent | Sofia presented a coherent argument from start to finish.
compelling | überzeugend, zwingend | The lawyer made a compelling case for her client's innocence.
flawed | fehlerhaft, mangelhaft | The study was flawed because the sample was too small.
underpin | untermauern, stützen | Solid research underpins every claim in her speech.
contention | Behauptung, Ansicht | It is my contention that schools should start later in the morning.
deduction | Schlussfolgerung, Deduktion | His deduction seemed logical, but it ignored one important fact.
plausible | plausibel, einleuchtend | Her explanation sounds plausible, but we still need proof.
## Überredungskunst
appeal | ansprechen, appellieren | The advert appeals to parents who worry about their children's health.
emotive | emotional, gefühlsbetont | Politicians often use emotive language to win support.
sway | beeinflussen, umstimmen | Nothing he said could sway the undecided voters.
rhetorical | rhetorisch | That was a rhetorical question, so you don't need to answer it.
hyperbole | Übertreibung, Hyperbel | Saying the queue was a mile long was pure hyperbole.
underscore | unterstreichen, hervorheben | The speaker underscored the need for urgent action on pollution.
credibility | Glaubwürdigkeit | One factual error can damage a speaker's credibility.
win over | gewinnen, überzeugen | Her honest answers won over even the sceptical audience.
manipulate | manipulieren, beeinflussen | Some advertisers manipulate our fears to sell their products.
catchphrase | Slogan, Schlagwort | The campaign's catchphrase was repeated on every billboard in town.
## Vortrag und Präsentation
lectern | Rednerpult | Sofia placed her notes on the lectern and smiled at the audience.
rapport | Draht, gutes Verhältnis | A good speaker builds rapport with the audience in the first minute.
anecdote | Anekdote, Kurzgeschichte | He opened his talk with a funny anecdote about his first job.
podium | Podium, Bühne | The mayor stepped up to the podium and thanked everyone.
digress | abschweifen | Sorry, I digress; let's return to the main question.
concise | prägnant, kurz und bündig | Keep your answers concise because each speaker has two minutes.
recap | zusammenfassen | Let me briefly recap the three main points of today's talk.
ramble | abschweifen, fahrig reden | When he gets nervous, he tends to ramble for ages.
captivate | fesseln, in den Bann ziehen | Her story captivated the audience from the very first minute.
heckle | stören, dazwischenrufen | A man at the back began to heckle the speaker.
## Medienanalyse
slant | Schlagseite, tendenziöse Darstellung | The article had a clear slant against the new mayor.
coverage | Berichterstattung | The coverage of the floods was detailed and fair.
sensationalism | Sensationsgier, Sensationsjournalismus | Readers are tired of sensationalism and want calm, accurate reporting.
misleading | irreführend, täuschend | The headline was misleading and caused unnecessary panic.
editorial | Leitartikel | Today's editorial criticises the government's housing policy.
spin | Dreh, geschönte Darstellung | The minister's team put a positive spin on the bad figures.
scrutiny | genaue Prüfung, kritischer Blick | The company's accounts are now under close scrutiny.
fact-check | überprüfen, Fakten prüfen | Journalists should fact-check every quote before publishing it.
objectivity | Objektivität | A good reporter tries to maintain objectivity at all times.
sound bite | O-Ton, prägnantes Zitat | The evening news showed only a short sound bite from the interview.
## Zustimmung und Widerspruch
concur | übereinstimmen, zustimmen | I concur with my colleague on this particular point.
dissent | Meinungsverschiedenheit, Dissens | There was strong dissent among the members about the new rules.
misgiving | Bedenken, Skepsis | Many residents expressed serious misgivings about the new parking plan.
contradict | widersprechen | His latest statement contradicts what he said last week.
dispute | bestreiten, anzweifeln | Nobody seriously disputes that the climate is changing.
acknowledge | anerkennen, zugeben | She acknowledged that the plan had some weaknesses.
side with | Partei ergreifen für | In the end, most of the class sided with Omar on this issue.
dismiss | abtun, abwinken | He dismissed the criticism as unfair and badly informed.
take issue with | Einspruch erheben gegen, kritisieren | I take issue with your claim that young people never read.
common ground | gemeinsame Basis | Despite their differences, the two sides found some common ground.
## Beweise und Logik
anecdotal | anekdotisch, auf Einzelfällen beruhend | The evidence is only anecdotal and needs proper research.
generalization | Verallgemeinerung | Saying all teenagers are lazy is an unfair generalization.
assumption | Annahme, Vermutung | Your plan is based on the assumption that prices will fall.
conclusive | schlüssig, eindeutig | There is no conclusive proof that the new drug works.
irrelevant | irrelevant, unerheblich | Her age is irrelevant to the question we are discussing.
inconsistency | Widersprüchlichkeit, Inkonsequenz | The reporter noticed an inconsistency in the witness's story.
circular | im Kreis drehend, zirkulär | His reasoning is circular because the conclusion simply repeats the premise.
verify | verifizieren, überprüfen | We could not verify the information from any other source.
rationale | Begründung, Hintergedanke | Can you explain the rationale behind this decision?
correlation | Korrelation, Zusammenhang | Correlation does not always mean that one thing causes another.
## Diskussionston
heated | hitzig, geladen | The meeting turned into a heated argument about money.
civil | zivilisiert, höflich | Let's keep this discussion civil and avoid personal comments, please.
confrontational | konfrontativ | His confrontational style made the negotiations even harder.
cut in | unterbrechen, dazwischenreden | Sorry to cut in, but I have a quick question.
backtrack | zurückrudern, zurücknehmen | The company was forced to backtrack after customers complained loudly.
bicker | zanken, streiten | The two brothers bicker about everything, even the weather.
hostile | feindselig | The audience grew hostile when the speaker ignored their questions.
composure | Fassung, Ruhe | Even under pressure, Emre never lost his composure.
talk over | überreden, über den Mund fahren | It is rude to talk over people who disagree with you.
patronising | herablassend | Please don't use that patronising tone when you explain it to me.
## Öffentliche Meinung und Kampagnen
petition | Petition, Unterschriftenliste | Over ten thousand people signed the petition to save the park.
advocate | befürworten, eintreten für | Maria advocates more cycle lanes in the city.
lobby | Lobbyarbeit betreiben | Local farmers lobbied the council for lower water prices.
grassroots | basisnah, von der Basis ausgehend | The grassroots movement started with just a few neighbours.
rally | Kundgebung, Demonstration | Thousands of people attended the rally in the main square.
mobilize | mobilisieren | The group mobilized hundreds of volunteers through social media.
controversy | Kontroverse, Streit | The new stadium caused controversy among local residents.
propaganda | Propaganda | The posters were pure propaganda with no real facts.
outcry | Aufschrei, Empörung | The plan to close the hospital caused a public outcry.
backlash | heftige Gegenreaktion | The decision to raise ticket prices led to a strong backlash from fans.
## Diskussionsformeln
all things considered | alles in allem | All things considered, the debate went better than we expected.
by the same token | gleichermaßen, ebenso | Teachers should respect students; by the same token, students should respect teachers.
it stands to reason | es liegt auf der Hand | It stands to reason that tired drivers cause more accidents.
play devil's advocate | sich als Anwalt des Teufels aufspielen | Let me play devil's advocate and argue for the opposite view.
beg the question | die Frage aufwerfen | The high price begs the question of who can actually afford it.
moot point | nebensächliche Frage, offene Frage | Whether we could have won is now a moot point.
in a nutshell | kurz und gut, auf den Punkt gebracht | In a nutshell, the project failed because of poor planning.
the long and the short of it | kurz gesagt, der Kern der Sache | The long and the short of it is that we need more time.
for the sake of argument | rein hypothetisch, angenommen | For the sake of argument, let's say the price doubles next year.
conversely | umgekehrt | Some people find the city exciting; conversely, others find it exhausting.
