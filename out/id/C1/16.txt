# UNIT: Bahasa percakapan | Kata penegas, seruan, bahasa gaul sehari-hari
## Kata pengisi jeda
you know | tahu kan; begitulah | It was one of those days when, you know, nothing really works.
I mean | maksudku; begini | The film was fine, I mean, it just wasn't what I expected.
kind of | agak; semacam | I'm kind of tired after the trip, so let's stay in tonight.
sort of | agak; bisa dibilang | Emre sort of agreed, but he didn't sound very convinced.
basically | pada dasarnya; intinya | Basically, we need more time and a bit more money to finish.
literally | benar-benar; secara harfiah | I was literally the only person in the whole cinema last night.
or something | atau semacamnya | Let's grab a coffee or something before the meeting starts.
and stuff | dan lain-lain; dan semacamnya | We talked about work and stuff, nothing too serious really.
if you like | bisa dibilang; ibaratnya | The village was, if you like, a little world of its own.
you see | tahu sendiri; begini ya | You see, I never learned to drive, so I always take the bus.
## Seruan
whoa | pelan-pelan; eh (seruan terkejut) | Whoa, slow down, you're driving far too fast on this road!
gosh | ya ampun; wah | Gosh, I didn't realise it was already so late in the evening.
blimey | ya ampun (BrE) | Blimey, have you seen how much they charge for parking here?
yikes | waduh; gawat | Yikes, I just noticed I sent that email to the wrong person.
whoops | ups | Whoops, I think I've just spilled coffee all over your notes.
phew | syukurlah; untunglah | Phew, we caught the last train just before the doors closed.
ugh | aduh; cih | Ugh, it's raining again and I forgot my umbrella at home.
eww | iuh; ih | Eww, there's something sticky all over the handle of this door.
aha | aha; o begitu | Aha, so that's why the printer kept jamming all morning!
huh | hah?; masa sih | Huh, I never thought Omar would actually move abroad one day.
## Ungkapan reaksi
no way | tidak mungkin; yang benar saja | No way, you got the job after only one interview?
you bet | tentu saja; pastilah | Are you coming to the party on Saturday? You bet I am!
fair enough | masuk akal; baiklah | You need more time to decide? Fair enough, let's talk next week.
tell me about it | tidak usah ditanya lagi; setuju banget | This traffic is unbearable today. Tell me about it, I've been stuck for an hour.
you don't say | masa sih; sungguh | The shop is closed on Sunday? You don't say, what a surprise.
big deal | bukan masalah besar; hebat banget (sindiran) | So he has a new car, big deal, it doesn't make him a better person.
so what | terus kenapa; emangnya kenapa | So what if we're a little late, the party goes on until midnight.
my bad | salahku; maaf ya | My bad, I totally forgot to send you the address yesterday.
no kidding | serius; tidak main-main | No kidding, Yuki really ran the whole marathon without any training.
go figure | aneh tapi nyata; tebak sendiri | He hates travelling but became a pilot, go figure.
## Panggilan dan sapaan
mate | kawan; sob (BrE) | Cheers for the help yesterday, mate, I really owe you one.
buddy | kawan; sobat (AmE) | Hey buddy, could you give me a hand with these heavy boxes?
dude | bro; sob (AmE) | Dude, you have to try the burgers at that new place downtown.
folks | teman-teman; semuanya | Okay folks, the bus leaves in ten minutes, so please don't be late.
cheers | terima kasih; bersulang | Cheers for holding the door, that bag was heavier than I thought.
ta | terima kasih (BrE, akrab) | Ta for the tea, love, it's exactly what I needed after work.
howdy | halo (AmE, pedesaan) | Howdy, neighbour, are you coming to the barbecue this weekend?
what's up | ada apa; bagaimana kabarmu | What's up, Diego? I haven't heard from you in weeks.
long time no see | sudah lama tidak jumpa | Long time no see, Camille, you look great after your holiday!
catch you later | sampai jumpa nanti | I've got to run to my lecture now, catch you later at lunch.
## Kata sifat gaul
gutted | sangat kecewa; hancur hatinya | Lukas was absolutely gutted when his team lost the final.
knackered | sangat lelah; kecapekkan | I'm completely knackered after moving furniture all day long.
chuffed | sangat senang; puas | Maria was really chuffed with the birthday present we got her.
dodgy | mencurigakan; tidak aman | That website looks a bit dodgy, I wouldn't enter my card details there.
skint | tidak punya uang; bokek (BrE) | I'd love to come to the festival, but I'm a bit skint this month.
hyped | sangat antusias; bersemangat | The kids are so hyped about the school trip to the seaside.
chill | santai; tenang | The new manager is pretty chill, she never shouts at anyone.
peckish | agak lapar; setengah lapar | I'm feeling a bit peckish, shall we grab a sandwich somewhere?
gobsmacked | sangat terkejut; tercengang | We were gobsmacked when Ali announced he was leaving for Australia.
minted | kaya raya; berlimpah harta (BrE) | Her parents must be minted to own a house like that by the sea.
## Kata benda gaul
bloke | pria; cowok (BrE) | The bloke next door helped me fix my bike this morning.
quid | pon (mata uang Inggris, gaul) | The tickets were only ten quid each, so we bought four.
buck | dolar (mata uang AS, gaul) | Can you lend me twenty bucks until Friday?
grand | ribuan dolar/pon | They paid about five grand for that old car, which seems a lot.
telly | televisi; tipi (BrE) | There's nothing good on the telly tonight, let's read instead.
loo | toilet; kamar kecil (BrE) | Excuse me, do you know where the loo is in this building?
fiver | uang lima dolar/pon | I found a fiver in the pocket of my old winter coat.
grub | makanan; santapan | The grub at the student canteen is cheap but surprisingly tasty.
cuppa | secangkir teh (BrE) | Sit down and have a cuppa, you look like you need a break.
brekkie | sarapan (gaul) | We had a huge brekkie at the café before going for a hike.
## Kata kerja gaul
crash | menumpang tidur; numpang menginap | Can I crash at your place tonight? I missed the last bus.
bail | membatalkan janji; mangkir | Sorry, I have to bail tonight, something came up at work.
ditch | meninggalkan; membuang | We ditched the car and walked the rest of the way into town.
freak out | panik; ketakutan | Don't freak out, but I think I've lost the keys to the flat.
binge | menonton maraton; menikmati secara berlebihan | We binged the whole series in one weekend and regretted nothing.
splurge | berfoya-foya; menghamburkan uang | After the exams, Anna splurged on a fancy dinner with her friends.
nick | mencuri; maling (BrE) | Someone nicked my bike from outside the library yesterday afternoon.
chuck | melempar; mencampakkan | Just chuck your coat on the sofa and make yourself at home.
fancy | menginginkan; menaksir | Do you fancy going to the cinema after dinner tonight?
reckon | memperkirakan; mengira | I reckon it'll rain later, so take a jacket with you.
## Kata penegas
dead | sangat; amat (BrE, gaul) | The new restaurant is dead good, you should definitely go there.
way | jauh lebih | This phone is way better than the one I had before.
wicked | keren sekali; hebat (gaul) | That concert last night was wicked, the crowd was amazing.
proper | sungguh-sungguh; benar (BrE) | We had a proper English breakfast with beans, eggs and toast.
mega | sangat; luar biasa | The queue for the new ride at the park was mega long.
hella | sangat; teramat (AmE, gaul) | That road trip along the coast was hella fun, let's do it again.
flipping | sialan; keparat (halus) | The flipping printer has broken down again just before my deadline.
jolly | cukup; lumayan (BrE, kuno) | That was a jolly good effort, you should be proud of yourself.
ever so | amat sangat; luar biasa (BrE, sopan) | Thank you ever so much for looking after the cat while we were away.
a tad | sedikit; agak | The soup is a tad too salty, but otherwise it's delicious.
## Singkatan percakapan
gonna | akan (going to) | I'm gonna call my mum after work and tell her the news.
wanna | ingin (want to) | Do you wanna come with us to the beach this afternoon?
gotta | harus (got to) | Sorry, I've gotta go, my train leaves in fifteen minutes.
dunno | tidak tahu (don't know) | I dunno where Omar put the keys, ask him when he gets back.
lemme | biarkan aku (let me) | Lemme check my calendar before I promise anything for next week.
gimme | beri aku (give me) | Gimme a minute, I'm just finishing this email to the client.
y'all | kalian semua (AmE selatan) | Are y'all coming to the family dinner on Sunday evening?
ain't | bukan; tidak (isn't/am not/haven't) | That ain't the way we do things around here, my friend.
innit | kan; bukan (BrE, gaul) | It's freezing outside today, innit? Better take a scarf.
cos | karena (because) | I stayed home cos I wasn't feeling great this morning.
## Ungkapan transisi dan pelunak
that reminds me | ngomong-ngomong; jadi teringat | You mentioned Lisbon; that reminds me, Diego is moving there in May.
come to think of it | kalau dipikir-pikir; kalau diingat lagi | Come to think of it, I haven't seen Yuki at the gym for weeks.
long story short | singkat cerita | Long story short, we missed the ferry and spent the night on the island.
if you ask me | kalau menurutku | If you ask me, the old design looked much better than the new one.
to be fair | sejujurnya; adilnya | To be fair, he did warn us that the road would be closed.
just saying | sekadar bilang; cuma ngomong | That jacket looked better on you than this one, just saying.
no offence | jangan tersinggung ya | No offence, but your cooking has been a bit salty lately.
between you and me | antara kita saja; rahasia ya | Between you and me, I think Lukas is planning to quit next month.
for what it's worth | terlepas dari berguna atau tidak | For what it's worth, I think you made the right decision.
at the end of the day | bagaimanapun juga; pada akhirnya | At the end of the day, it's your money, so it's your choice.
