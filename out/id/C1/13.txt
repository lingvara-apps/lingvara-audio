# UNIT: Filsafat dan etika | Cabang filsafat, teori moral, perdebatan etis
## Cabang filsafat
philosophy of mind | filsafat pikiran | Philosophy of mind asks how thoughts and feelings relate to the brain.
aesthetics | estetika | Her lecture on aesthetics explored why we find certain landscapes beautiful.
applied ethics | etika terapan | Applied ethics deals with real issues such as medicine, business and the environment.
philosophy of language | filsafat bahasa | Philosophy of language asks how words manage to refer to things in the world.
philosophy of science | filsafat sains | Philosophy of science examines what makes a theory truly scientific.
axiology | aksiologi | Axiology deals with the nature of value, both moral and aesthetic.
political philosophy | filsafat politik | Political philosophy asks what makes a government legitimate in the first place.
metaethics | metaetika | Metaethics asks whether moral statements can be true or false at all.
ethicist | ahli etika | The hospital consulted an ethicist before making the difficult decision.
ontological | ontologis | The debate raises an ontological question about whether numbers really exist.
## Aliran pemikiran
humanism | humanisme | Humanism places human reason and dignity at the centre of moral thinking.
rationalism | rasionalisme | Rationalism argues that reason, not experience, is the main source of knowledge.
positivism | positivisme | Positivism claimed that only statements tested by observation have real meaning.
stoicism | stoisisme | Emre turned to stoicism to cope with the stress of his new job.
pragmatism | pragmatisme | Pragmatism judges ideas by their practical results rather than by abstract truth.
nihilism | nihilisme | The novel's hero slides into nihilism after losing everything he believed in.
idealism | idealisme | In philosophy, idealism claims that reality is fundamentally mental.
materialism | materialisme | Materialism holds that everything, including thought, is made of matter.
determinism | determinisme | If determinism is true, every choice we make was fixed long before we were born.
scepticism | skeptisisme | Philosophical scepticism questions whether we can know anything at all.
## Teori moral
utilitarianism | utilitarianisme | Utilitarianism judges an action by how much happiness it produces for everyone affected.
deontology | deontologi | According to deontology, some acts are wrong even if they lead to good results.
virtue ethics | etika kebajikan | Virtue ethics asks what a person of good character would do in your place.
consequentialism | konsekuesialisme | Consequentialism says that only the outcomes of an action matter morally.
categorical imperative | imperatif kategoris | The categorical imperative tells us to act only in ways we could wish everyone to act.
social contract | kontrak sosial | Social contract theory explains why free individuals agree to obey a government.
hedonism | hedonisme | Ancient hedonism was less about parties and more about avoiding pain.
ethical egoism | egoisme etis | Ethical egoism claims that we ought to act in our own long-term interest.
natural law | hukum kodrat | Natural law theorists believe moral rules can be discovered through reason alone.
greater good | kebaikan bersama | She agreed to give up her land for the greater good of the village.
## Konsep dasar filsafat
a priori | a priori | Mathematical truths are often described as a priori knowledge.
a posteriori | a posteriori | That water boils at a hundred degrees is an a posteriori truth.
dualism | dualisme | Dualism treats the mind and the body as two completely different substances.
monism | monisme | Monism holds that mind and matter are ultimately made of one single substance.
transcendent | transenden | Many traditions speak of a transcendent reality beyond the physical world.
causality | kausalitas | Some physicists now question whether causality works the same way at tiny scales.
moral agent | agen moral | Only a moral agent, able to understand right and wrong, can be held responsible.
sentience | kapasitas perasa | The question of animal sentience is central to modern debates about farming.
personhood | status kepribadian | Lawyers and philosophers disagree about when personhood begins.
intrinsic | intrinsik | She believes that nature has intrinsic value, not just value for humans.
## Metode penalaran
thought experiment | eksperimen pemikiran | The famous thought experiment asks whether you would pull a lever to save five lives.
reductio ad absurdum | reductio ad absurdum | He used a reductio ad absurdum to show that the rule would forbid all eating.
sound argument | argumen sahih | A sound argument has true premises and a conclusion that follows from them.
principle of charity | asas kemurahan hati | The principle of charity means interpreting your opponent's view in its strongest form.
first principles | prinsip dasar | Instead of copying others, she reasoned from first principles.
counterexample | contoh tandingan | A single counterexample is enough to disprove a general rule.
Occam's razor | pisau cukur Occam | By Occam's razor, the simplest explanation is usually the one to prefer.
proposition | proposisi | Every proposition in logic must be either true or false.
necessary condition | syarat perlu | Oxygen is a necessary condition for fire, but it is not enough on its own.
sufficient condition | syarat cukup | Being a square is a sufficient condition for being a rectangle.
