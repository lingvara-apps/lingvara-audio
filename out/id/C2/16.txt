# UNIT: Formula dialog | Ungkapan sopan, salam, dan ekspresi sehari-hari
## Ungkapan sopan dan kuno
good morrow | selamat pagi (kuno) | The innkeeper bowed and wished every guest good morrow at breakfast.
well met | senang bisa bertemu (kuno) | "Well met, traveller," said the old guard, opening the heavy gate.
how fare you | apa kabar (kuno) | The duke greeted his cousin warmly and asked, "How fare you this winter?"
pray be seated | silakan duduk (resmi, kuno) | The elderly professor gestured to the armchair and said, "Pray be seated."
I am much obliged | saya sangat berterima kasih | I am much obliged to you for driving me all the way to the airport.
to what do I owe the pleasure | apa kehormatan ini | Seeing her former student at the door, she smiled: "To what do I owe the pleasure?"
if I may be so bold | mohon maaf jika lancang | If I may be so bold, madam, that hat suits you perfectly.
begging your pardon | mohon maaf | Begging your pardon, sir, but the train to the coast has already left.
I should be delighted | dengan senang hati | "Would you join us for dinner?" "I should be delighted, thank you."
would you be so kind as to | bolehkah saya minta tolong | Would you be so kind as to close the window? It's rather cold.

fancy seeing you here | tidak menyangka ketemu di sini | Fancy seeing you here, I didn't know you liked jazz.

## Penolakan sopan

with all due respect | dengan segala hormat | With all due respect, professor, the data doesn't support that theory.
I must respectfully decline | mohon maaf saya harus menolak | Thank you for the generous offer, but I must respectfully decline.
thanks, but no thanks | terima kasih, tapi tidak dulu | Another night shift on my birthday? Thanks, but no thanks.
I'll give it a miss | saya lewatkan saja | Karaoke again tonight? I think I'll give it a miss, I'm exhausted.
I'd sooner not | lebih baik tidak | I'd sooner not discuss my salary in front of the whole team.
that may be so | mungkin saja benar | That may be so, but the client still expects the order on Monday.
I take your point | aku mengerti maksudmu | I take your point about the cost, but safety must come first.
far be it from me | bukan maksudku mencampuri | Far be it from me to criticise, but that dress is a bit too bright for a funeral.
I'm not sure I follow | aku kurang paham | I'm not sure I follow, could you explain the second step again?
