# UNIT: Bahasa Gaul | Ungkapan informal, seruan, dan bahasa sehari-hari
## Seruan sehari-hari
crikey | ya ampun, wah (Inggris/Australia) | Crikey, look at the size of that wave coming towards the pier!
jeez | ya ampun, aduh | Jeez, how long does it take to make one cup of coffee?
oi | hei, woi | Oi, you can't park your van in front of my garage!
strewth | ya ampun, astaga (Australia) | Strewth, it's hot enough to fry an egg on the pavement today!
cor | wah, gila (Inggris) | Cor, that pie smells amazing, did you bake it yourself?
dang | sialan, aduh (Amerika) | Dang, I left my phone charger at the motel again.
darn | sial, aduh | Darn, the shop closed five minutes before we got there.
heck | sialan, apa-an sih | What the heck is that strange noise coming from the engine?
shucks | ah sudahlah, astaga (Amerika) | Aw shucks, it was nothing, anyone would have helped you.
sheesh | ya ampun, ampun deh | Sheesh, they want fifteen dollars for a sandwich here?

lad | anak muda, pemuda; (jamak) geng cowok | Diego is going out with the lads to watch the match tonight.
cuz | sepupu, bro (Amerika) | Hey cuz, are you coming to the family barbecue on Sunday?
pardner | kawan, sobat (Amerika) | Howdy, pardner, are you looking for a room for the night?
fella | cowok, orang | The fella behind the counter gave us an extra portion of chips.
guv | bos, om (Inggris) | Where to, guv? The traffic on the bridge is terrible this morning.
pal | kawan, sobat (bisa bernada ancaman) | Listen, pal, this is my parking space, so find another one.
bruv | bro, kawan (Londra) | Calm down, bruv, it was only a joke about your haircut.
the missus | istri, bini (candaan) | I can't stay for another pint, the missus is expecting me home.

## Kata kerja sehari-hari

pull a sickie | pura-pura sakit agar tidak masuk kerja | Half the office pulled a sickie on the day of the big match.
leg it | kabur, lari terbirit-birit | When the dog started barking, we legged it over the fence.
scarper | kabur, ngibrit (Inggris) | The pickpocket scarpered before anyone could grab him.
natter | mengobrol, bergosip | The two neighbours nattered over the garden fence for an hour.
nab | menyambar, menangkap | Maria nabbed the last seat on the bus just before the old man.
blag | mendapatkan dengan lihai, melobi | Omar somehow blagged two free tickets to the concert.
suss out | menebak, mencari tahu | It didn't take long for the teacher to suss out who had written the note.
bodge | memperbaiki asal-asalan | The landlord just bodged the leaking pipe with some tape.
cadge | menumpang, meminta gratisan | He's always trying to cadge cigarettes off strangers at the bus stop.

cheesed off | jengkel, bosan | She was cheesed off with waiting for the plumber all day.
hacked off | kesal, jengkel | The fans were hacked off when the concert was cancelled at the last minute.
do someone's head in | membuat pusing, membuat stres | That car alarm going off every night is doing my head in.
throw a wobbly | mengamuk, ngambek | The singer threw a wobbly because his dressing room was too small.
have a go at | memarahi, menyerang | The driver had a go at me for standing too close to his car.
