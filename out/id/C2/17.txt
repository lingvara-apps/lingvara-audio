# UNIT: Nuansa Emosi III | Ketakutan, kecemasan, kemarahan, kesedihan, malu, kesombongan, ketenangan, antusiasme
## Ketakutan dan kengerian
trepidation | rasa cemas; gentar | Emre opened the letter from the hospital with considerable trepidation.
aghast | ngeri; terkejut | Maria stood aghast as the floodwater poured into the ground floor.
petrified | ketakutan setengah mati; kaku karena takut | The little boy was petrified of the huge dog next door.
cower | meringkuk karena takut | The kittens cowered under the bed during the thunderstorm.
quail | gentar; ciut nyali | Even experienced climbers quail at the sight of that vertical ice wall.
unnerve | membuat gentar; meresahkan | The silence in the empty house began to unnerve Yuki.
blood-curdling | mengerikan; membekukan darah | A blood-curdling scream echoed through the old castle at midnight.
spine-chilling | sangat menakutkan; membuat bulu kuduk berdiri | Omar told us a spine-chilling ghost story around the campfire.
daunted | gentar; patah semangat | Sofia was not daunted by the size of the task ahead of her.
make someone's blood run cold | membuat darah mendidih karena ngeri; menakuti setengah mati | The look in the stranger's eyes made my blood run cold.
## Kecemasan dan kegelisahan
disquiet | keresahan; kecemasan | There is growing disquiet among staff about the planned changes.
perturbed | gelisah; cemas | Lukas seemed perturbed by the sudden silence on the phone.
fretful | rewel; gelisah | The baby was fretful all night because of a new tooth.
overwrought | sangat tegang; kelelahan mental | After three sleepless nights, Anna was tired and overwrought.
jittery | gugup; gelisah | Too much coffee always makes me jittery before an interview.
angst | kecemasan mendalam; kegelisahan | The novel captures the angst of teenagers growing up in a small town.
fraught | penuh ketegangan; sarat masalah | The negotiations were fraught, and nobody slept much that week.
on tenterhooks | bagai duduk di atas duri; cemas menunggu | We were on tenterhooks waiting for the exam results.
qualm | keraguan hati; perasaan bersalah | Diego had no qualms about asking his manager for a raise.
foreboding | firasat buruk | Camille had a strange sense of foreboding before the flight.
## Kemarahan dan amukan
ire | kemurkaan; kemarahan | The new parking rules have drawn the ire of local residents.
wrath | murka; amarah besar | Nobody wanted to face the wrath of the head chef after the mistake.
irate | sangat marah; murka | An irate customer demanded to speak to the manager immediately.
incensed | sangat geram; naik pitam | Residents were incensed by the decision to close the library.
livid | sangat marah; merah padam | Ali was livid when he found a scratch on his new car.
apoplectic | sangat marah hingga gemetar | My grandfather went apoplectic when the referee cancelled the goal.
seethe | mendidih karena marah; sangat jengkel | She smiled politely, but inside she was seething with rage.
bristle | menubruk marah; bulu kuduk berdiri karena kesal | Tom bristled at the suggestion that he had been careless.
exasperation | kekesalan; kejenuhan | The teacher sighed with exasperation as the class ignored her again.
fly into a rage | mendadak mengamuk; naik pitam | He flies into a rage whenever anyone criticises his cooking.
## Kesedihan dan kedukaan
forlorn | sangat malang; merana | A forlorn little dog sat outside the closed shop in the rain.
woebegone | tampak suram; berwajah sedih | The woebegone players walked slowly off the pitch after the defeat.
doleful | amat sedih; muram | The old dog looked at us with big doleful eyes.
despondent | putus asa; muram | After months without work, Emre was becoming despondent.
disconsolate | sangat berduka; tak dapat dihibur | The disconsolate child refused to leave the beach without her lost toy.
wistful | penuh kerinduan; murung | Maria gave a wistful smile as she looked at her old photos.
crestfallen | patah semangat; sedih | Omar looked crestfallen when he heard he had not been chosen.
lugubrious | sangat suram; muram | The waiter had a lugubrious face that made everyone feel gloomy.

desolation | kehancuran batin; rasa kosong yang mendalam | A feeling of utter desolation came over him after the funeral.
## Malu dan kecanggungan
chagrin | kekecewaan bercampur malu | Much to his chagrin, Lukas arrived after the doors had closed.
mortified | sangat malu; merasa ingin menghilang | Anna was mortified when her phone rang during the silent ceremony.
sheepish | tersipu-sipu; malu | Diego gave a sheepish grin and admitted he had forgotten the tickets.
abashed | merasa malu; tersipu | The student looked abashed when the professor corrected him in public.
shamefaced | berwajah malu; penuh rasa bersalah | The shamefaced teenager returned the money he had taken.
contrite | menyesal; bertobat | He sounded genuinely contrite when he called to apologise.
rueful | penuh penyesalan; masygul | Camille gave a rueful laugh about the money she had wasted.
ignominy | keaiban; noda malu | The champion suffered the ignominy of losing in the first round.
discomfiture | rasa canggung; kebingungan karena malu | She enjoyed his obvious discomfiture when the truth came out.
want the ground to swallow you up | ingin bumi menelan diri karena malu | When I tripped on stage, I wanted the ground to swallow you up.
## Kesombongan dan keangkuhan
hubris | kesombongan; keangkuhan berlebih | The company's collapse was a classic case of hubris.
conceit | keangkuhan; rasa bangga diri berlebihan | His conceit made it hard for colleagues to work with him.
haughty | tinggi hati; angkuh | The haughty receptionist barely looked up from her screen.
supercilious | memandang rendah; pongah | He raised one eyebrow in a supercilious way and said nothing.
smug | puas diri; songong | Ali had a smug look on his face after winning the argument.
vainglorious | suka menyombongkan diri; pamer | The general made a vainglorious speech about his own victories.
overweening | terlalu sombong; berlebihan | Her overweening ambition eventually cost her most of her friends.
swagger | gaya angkuh; langkah sombong | He walked into the room with the swagger of a movie star.
self-importance | sifat mentang-mentang; merasa paling penting | The manager's self-importance annoyed everyone at the meeting.
lord it over | bertindak sewenang-wenang; memerintah sesuka hati | Since his promotion, Mark has been lording it over his old friends.
## Ketenangan dan kedamaian
equanimity | ketenangan batin; keseimbangan jiwa | Sofia accepted the bad news with remarkable equanimity.
serenity | ketenteraman; kedamaian | The garden has a serenity that makes visitors speak in whispers.
aplomb | ketenangan diri; kepercayaan diri yang mantap | Camille answered the journalists' awkward questions with great aplomb.
unflappable | tidak mudah panik; tenang terkendali | Our unflappable guide calmly found us another hotel at midnight.
imperturbable | sangat tenang; tak tergoyahkan | The judge remained imperturbable while the crowd shouted outside.
placid | tenang; damai | The placid surface of the lake reflected the snowy peaks.
unruffled | tidak terpengaruh; tenang sentosa | Despite the chaos, the waiter seemed completely unruffled.
poise | ketenangan anggun; pembawaan tenang | Despite her young age, the violinist performed with great poise.
tranquillity | kedamaian; ketenangan | We moved to the countryside in search of peace and tranquillity.
nonchalance | sikap acuh tak acuh; ketidakpedulian santai | He shrugged with studied nonchalance, though he was clearly nervous.
## Antusiasme dan kegembiraan

exuberance | keceriaan meluap-luap; semangat tinggi | The children's exuberance filled the whole house with noise.
rapture | keterpesonaan; kebahagiaan sempurna | The audience listened in rapture as the choir began to sing.
euphoria | eforia; rasa bahagia yang meluap | The euphoria of winning the final lasted for several days.
jubilant | bersorak gembira; riang gembira | Jubilant fans poured into the streets after the match.
ebullient | sangat bersemangat; antusias | Our ebullient host greeted every guest with a hug.
giddy | pusing karena gembira; sangat bersemangat | Anna felt giddy with excitement on the morning of her wedding.
exhilaration | kegairahan; sensasi menyenangkan | Nothing compares to the exhilaration of skiing down a fresh slope.
zest | gairah hidup; semangat tinggi | At ninety, my grandmother still has an amazing zest for life.
walk on air | merasa seperti melayang di udara; sangat bahagia | After she said yes, Lukas was walking on air for weeks.
## Iri hati dan kedengkian
rancour | dendam kesumat; kebencian mendalam | They divorced years ago, but there is still no rancour between them.
spite | kedengkian; rasa iri | He broke the toy out of spite because his sister would not share.
covet | menginginkan milik orang lain; mendambakan | Many collectors covet this rare edition of the novel.
begrudge | merasa iri; menyayangkan | Nobody begrudges her success because she worked so hard for it.
malice | niat jahat; dengki | I am sure he said it without any malice, just carelessly.
schadenfreude | rasa senang atas penderitaan orang lain | I felt a little schadenfreude when the arrogant player missed the penalty.
umbrage | rasa tersinggung; kejengkelan | Omar took umbrage at the suggestion that he was lazy.
pique | rasa sakit hati; kejengkelan sesaat | She left the party in a fit of pique after the argument.
sullen | cemberut; muram | The sullen teenager refused to say a word during dinner.
petulant | mudah merajuk; rewel | His petulant reaction to losing surprised the other players.
## Kata kerja emosi
languish | menderita; merana lama | The project languished for years without proper funding.
wallow | berkubang dalam perasaan; meratapi nasib | It is time to stop wallowing in self-pity and find a new job.
brood | merenung sedih; memikirkan terus | He spent the whole evening brooding over what she had said.
mope | merana; bermuram durja | Stop moping around the house and come for a walk with us.
rejoice | bersukacita; bergembira | The whole village rejoiced when the lost children were found safe.
simmer | mendidih dalam hati; menahan amarah | Anger had been simmering in the village for months before the protest.
exult | bersorak menang; melompat kegirangan | The team exulted in their unexpected victory over the champions.
revel | menikmati sepuas-puasnya; bersenang-senang | Maria revelled in the freedom of her first holiday alone.
rankle | mengganjal di hati; menyiksa batin | The unfair criticism still rankles with him after all these years.
gloat | menyombongkan kemenangan; bersenang-senang atas kesusahan orang lain | It is not nice to gloat when your friends lose a game.
