## योजक I
however | तथापि, हालाँकि | The hotel was nice. However, it was expensive.
though | फिर भी, हालाँकि | The film was long. I enjoyed it, though.
because of | के कारण, की वजह से | The match was cancelled because of the rain.
thanks to | की कृपा से, के बदौलत | We arrived early thanks to you.
as if | मानो, जैसे कि | He looks as if he hasn't slept.
rather than | के बजाय, की अपेक्षा | I'd like tea rather than coffee.
instead | इसके बजाय, उसके स्थान पर | There was no rice, so we had pasta instead.
in fact | वास्तव में, असल में | I'm not tired. In fact, I feel great.
actually | दरअसल, वास्तव में | Actually, my name is Sofia, not Sonia.
what's more | इसके अलावा, ऊपर से | The flat is big and, what's more, it's cheap.
## योजक II
either ... or | या तो ... या | You can either walk or take the bus.
neither ... nor | न तो ... और न ही | Neither Ali nor Emre came to the party.
both ... and | दोनों ... और | Both my mother and my father are teachers.
instead of | के बजाय, के स्थान पर | Let's walk instead of taking a taxi.
in order to | के लिए, के उद्देश्य से | He moved to London in order to study.
so as to | ताकि, के वास्ते | We left early so as to avoid the traffic.
firstly | सबसे पहले, प्रथम | Firstly, I'd like to thank you all.
finally | अंत में, आखिरकार | Finally, don't forget your homework.
for example | उदाहरण के लिए | I love fruit, for example apples and pears.
at first | शुरुआत में, पहले | At first I didn't like the city.
