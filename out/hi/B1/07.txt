# UNIT: यात्रा और संस्कृति | यात्रा, पर्यटन, सांस्कृतिक अंतर
## यात्रा योजना
itinerary | यात्रा कार्यक्रम | Our itinerary includes three days in Edinburgh and two in York.
getaway | छोटी छुट्टी, अवकाश | We booked a weekend getaway in a small cottage by the lake.
off-season | ऑफ़-सीज़न, ऑफ-सीजन (कम व्यस्त समय) | Prices are much lower if you travel in the off-season.
budget airline | सस्ती विमान कंपनी, बजट एयरलाइन | We flew with a budget airline, so we paid extra for our bags.
package holiday | पैकेज हॉलिडे, सामूहिक अवकाश पैकेज | A package holiday includes flights, the hotel and sometimes meals.
travel agent | यात्रा एजेंट | The travel agent found us a cheaper flight with fewer stops.
day trip | दिनभर की यात्रा, एक दिवसीय यात्रा | From London, Oxford is an easy day trip by train.
one-way | एकतरफा | Ali bought a one-way ticket because he had no plans to return soon.
layover | ठहराव, लेओवर | We had a six-hour layover in Frankfurt on the way to Tokyo.
peak season | व्यस्त सीजन, पीक सीजन | Hotels by the sea are often fully booked during peak season.
## आवास
accommodation | आवास, रहने की जगह | Finding cheap accommodation in London can be really difficult.
homestay | होमस्टे (परिवार के साथ रहना) | During her language course, Sofia stayed in a homestay with a Scottish family.
holiday let | किराये का हॉलिडे होम | We rented a holiday let near the beach for two weeks.
resort | रिसॉर्ट, विश्राम गृह | The ski resort was full of families during the winter break.
check-in | चेक-इन, आगमन पंजीकरण | Check-in starts at two o'clock, but you can leave your bags earlier.
amenities | सुविधाएँ | The resort has excellent amenities, including a spa and two pools.
complimentary | मुफ़्त, मानार्थ | Every room comes with complimentary tea, coffee and biscuits.
en suite | संलग्न बाथरूम वाला | All the bedrooms in the cottage are en suite and very spacious.
self-catering | स्व-पाचन, रसोईघर वाला (आवास) | A self-catering apartment is cheaper for a large family.
host | मेज़बान | Our host gave us a map and recommended a great local restaurant.
## हवाई अड्डा और सीमा
passport control | पासपोर्ट नियंत्रण | Have your documents ready before you reach passport control.
declare | घोषणा करना | You must declare any large amounts of cash when you arrive.
departure lounge | प्रस्थान कक्ष | We had a coffee in the departure lounge while we waited for our flight.
security check | सुरक्षा जाँच | Liquids must be in a clear bag for the security check.
carry-on | कैरी-ऑन बैग, केबिन सामान | My carry-on was too heavy, so I had to pay a fee.
arrivals hall | आगमन हॉल | Maria's brother was waiting for her in the arrivals hall with flowers.
duty-free | ड्यूटी-फ्री, करमुक्त | Sofia bought some perfume at the duty-free shop before her flight.
immigration | आप्रवासन नियंत्रण | The queue at immigration was long because three flights landed together.
long-haul | लंबी दूरी की (उड़ान) | After a long-haul flight, I always need a day to recover.
excess baggage | अतिरिक्त सामान | Diego paid sixty euros for excess baggage on the way home.
## दर्शनीय स्थल यात्रा
heritage site | विरासत स्थल | Bath is a World Heritage Site famous for its Roman baths.
must-see | अवश्य देखने योग्य | The old harbour is a must-see if you visit Bristol.
audio guide | ऑडियो गाइड, श्रव्य मार्गदर्शक | The audio guide in the museum is available in twelve languages.
tour guide | टूर गाइड, मार्गदर्शक | Our tour guide told funny stories about the history of each building.
memento | स्मृति चिह्न | Diego kept the old train ticket as a memento of his trip.
picturesque | सुरम्य, दर्शनीय | We stopped in a picturesque village with stone cottages and a tiny church.
breathtaking | लुभावना, साँस रोक देने वाला | The view from the top of the cliff was absolutely breathtaking.
panoramic | मनोरम, पैनोरमिक | The tower offers a panoramic view of the whole city.
tourist trap | पर्यटक जाल (महँगा और बेकार स्थान) | That restaurant is a tourist trap with high prices and average food.
excursion | भ्रमण, सैर | The hotel organised an excursion to a nearby island for the guests.
## यात्रा की समस्याएँ
divert | मार्ग बदलना, डायवर्ट करना | Because of the fog, our plane was diverted to Manchester.
rebook | पुनर्निर्धारण करना, पुनः बुकिंग करना | The airline rebooked us on the next flight at no extra cost.
overbooked | अत्यधिक बुक किया गया | The flight was overbooked, so they offered us a free hotel night.
connecting flight | कनेक्टिंग उड़ान, जोड़ने वाली उड़ान | Because of the delay, Lukas missed his connecting flight to Rome.
strand | फँसा देना | The strike left thousands of travellers stranded at the airport.
pickpocket | जेबकतरे | Watch your bag on the metro because pickpockets often work there.
lost luggage | खोया हुआ सामान | Report lost luggage at the airline desk before you leave the airport.
overcharge | अधिक शुल्क लेना | The taxi driver tried to overcharge us because we were tourists.
compensation | मुआवज़ा | Passengers received compensation for the long delay.
travel advisory | यात्रा सलाह | Check the official travel advisory before you visit the region.
## सांस्कृतिक अंतर
custom | रीति-रिवाज, प्रथा | It is a custom in many countries to remove your shoes at home.
etiquette | शिष्टाचार, तौर-तरीके | Table etiquette can be very different from one country to another.
culture shock | संस्कृति आघात (नया माहौल समझने में कठिनाई) | Emre experienced culture shock during his first month in Canada.
stereotype | रूढ़िवादिता, पूर्वाग्रह | Not every British person drinks tea all day; that is just a stereotype.
diversity | विविधता | London is famous for its cultural diversity and many languages.
taboo | वर्जित विषय, टैबू | In some cultures, talking about salary is still a taboo.
offend | ठेस पहुँचाना, नाराज़ करना | I didn't want to offend our hosts, so I tried every dish.
appropriate | उचित, उपयुक्त | Shorts are not appropriate clothing when you visit a temple.
gesture | हावभाव, मुद्रा | A thumbs-up is a friendly gesture in some countries but rude in others.
adapt | अनुकूल होना, ढल जाना | Children usually adapt to a new culture faster than adults.
## परंपराएँ और शिष्टाचार

jump the queue | लाइन तोड़ना, आगे निकल जाना | In Britain, it is considered very rude to jump the queue.
handshake | हाथ मिलाना | A firm handshake is a common greeting in business meetings.
bow | झुककर अभिवादन करना | In Japan, people often bow when they greet each other.
punctual | समय का पाबंद, पंचुअल | Please be punctual because the tour bus leaves at exactly eight.
body language | शारीरिक भाषा | Body language can mean different things in different cultures.
toast | टोस्ट, शुभकामना में गिलास उठाना | At the wedding dinner, the bride's father made a touching toast.
hospitality | आतिथ्य-सत्कार | We were amazed by the warm hospitality of the local people.
modest | शालीन, मर्यादित | Visitors should wear modest clothes inside religious buildings.
greet | अभिवादन करना | In France, friends often greet each other with a kiss on each cheek.
## विदेश में रहना
overseas | विदेश में | Camille has lived overseas for six years and speaks four languages.
expat | प्रवासी (देश से बाहर रहने वाला) | There is a large community of expats in this part of the city.
homesick | घर की याद से पीड़ित | During his first winter in Moscow, Ali felt very homesick.
residence permit | निवास परमिट | You need a residence permit if you want to stay longer than a year.
work permit | कार्य अनुमति, वर्क परमिट | Anna needed a work permit before she could start her job in Australia.
foreigner | विदेशी | As a foreigner, I found the local bureaucracy quite confusing.
native | मूल निवासी | It is easier to learn the local slang if you live with native speakers.
fit in | मेल खाना, घुलमिल जाना | Yuki was worried that she wouldn't fit in at her new office in Toronto.
emigrate | विदेश बस जाना, प्रवास करना | His grandparents emigrated to New Zealand in the 1960s.
integrate | एकीकृत होना, घुलना-मिलना | Learning the language helps newcomers integrate into society.

explore | खोजना, अन्वेषण करना | We rented bikes to explore the countryside around Oxford.
