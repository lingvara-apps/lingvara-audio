# UNIT: विज्ञान और अनुसंधान | वैज्ञानिक पद्धति, शैक्षणिक भाषा, खोज
## वैज्ञानिक पद्धति
observation | अवलोकन, प्रेक्षण | Careful observation is the first step of any good study.
testable | परीक्षण योग्य | A scientific idea must be testable, or it is only an opinion.
falsify | गलत साबित करना | A good theory makes predictions that could, in principle, be falsified.
conjecture | अनुमान, परिकल्पना | For now, the link between the two events is pure conjecture.
reproducible | पुनरुत्पादनीय | Results are only trusted when they are reproducible in other labs.
measurement | माप, पैमाइश | We took three measurements and used the average.
precision | सटीकता, शुद्धता | This instrument measures temperature with remarkable precision.
accuracy | यथार्थता, शुद्धता [सटीकता] | Nobody questioned the accuracy of her calculations.
double-blind | डबल-ब्लाइंड (दोनों पक्षों से छुपा हुआ) | In a double-blind study, neither doctors nor patients know who gets the real drug.
placebo | प्लेसबो (प्रभावहीन दवा) | Half of the volunteers were given a placebo instead of the medicine.
## प्रयोगशाला में
specimen | नमूना | Each specimen was labelled and stored in the freezer.
beaker | बीकर | Sofia carefully poured the blue liquid into a glass beaker.
pipette | पिपेट | Use a pipette to add exactly three drops of the solution.
apparatus | उपकरण, यंत्र | The apparatus has to be cleaned after every single use.
sterile | रोगाणुमुक्त, स्टरलाइज़्ड | All the instruments must be kept completely sterile.
contaminate | दूषित करना | A single hair can contaminate the whole sample.
dissolve | घोलना | Stir the water until the salt dissolves completely.
residue | अवशेष | A thin white residue remained at the bottom of the glass.
protective goggles | सुरक्षात्मक चश्मा | Never mix chemicals without wearing protective goggles.
calibrate | अंशांकित करना, कैलिब्रेट करना | We calibrate the scales every morning before we start work.
## डेटा और आँकड़े
statistically | सांख्यिकीय रूप से | The difference between the two groups was not statistically significant.
average | औसत | On average, the participants slept six hours a night.
median | माध्यिका | The median age of the volunteers was thirty-four.
percentage | प्रतिशत | A small percentage of patients reported mild headaches.
ratio | अनुपात | The ratio of men to women in the study was roughly equal.
respondent | उत्तरदाता | Most respondents said they exercise less than once a week.
questionnaire | प्रश्नावली | Please fill in the questionnaire and return it by Friday.
deviation | विचलन | Even a small deviation from the plan can change the results.
random | यादृच्छिक, रैंडम | The computer picks a random sample of a thousand households.
trend | रुझान, प्रवृत्ति | The graph shows a clear upward trend over the last decade.
## शैक्षणिक लेखन
paraphrase | अपने शब्दों में कहना | Try to paraphrase the quotation instead of copying it word for word.
quote | उद्धृत करना | If you quote another writer, always give the page number.
summary (summaries) | सारांश | Write a short summary of the article in your own words.
heading | शीर्षक | Each section of the report should have a clear heading.
literature review | साहित्य समीक्षा | Her literature review covers more than fifty earlier studies.
acknowledgements | आभार ज्ञापन | In the acknowledgements, Ali thanked his parents and his supervisor.
thesis statement | शोध प्रबंध कथन | Your thesis statement should appear at the end of the introduction.
word count | शब्द संख्या | Check the word count before you hand in your essay.
glossary (glossaries) | शब्दावली | Technical terms are explained in the glossary at the back.
publication | प्रकाशन | The paper is not yet ready for publication.
## शैक्षणिक क्रियाएँ
hypothesise | परिकल्पना करना | We hypothesise that the effect will be stronger in children.
demonstrate | प्रदर्शित करना, साबित करना | The study demonstrates a clear link between diet and mood.
imply | संकेत देना, निहित होना | These results imply that the old model was too simple.
infer | अनुमान लगाना, निष्कर्ष निकालना | From these figures, we can infer that prices will rise.
assess | आकलन करना | It is too early to assess the long-term impact of the policy.
derive | व्युत्पन्न करना, प्राप्त करना | The formula is derived from a few simple observations.
validate | मान्य ठहराना, सत्यापित करना | The model was validated using data from five countries.
undermine | कमज़ोर करना | One mistake in the data could undermine the whole argument.
establish | स्थापित करना, निश्चित करना | Scientists have not yet established what causes the disease.
illustrate | समझाना, चित्रित करना | The diagram illustrates how the heart pumps blood.

molecule | अणु | A water molecule consists of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.
compound | यौगिक | Salt is a compound of sodium and chlorine.
element | तत्व | Gold is one of the least reactive elements.
radiation | विकिरण | Workers wear special badges to measure their exposure to radiation.
magnetic | चुंबकीय | The Earth has a magnetic field that protects us from the sun.
momentum | संवेग | The heavy ball kept rolling because of its momentum.
