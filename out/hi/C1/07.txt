# UNIT: साहित्यिक आलोचना | विधाएँ, संरचना, आलंकारिक भाषा, धाराएँ
## साहित्यिक विधाएँ
novella | लघु उपन्यास, छोटी कहानी | The novella is short enough to read in a single evening.
epic | महाकाव्य | The ancient epic tells of a hero's long journey home after the war.
sonnet | सोनट (चौदह पंक्तियों की कविता) | Every sonnet in the collection has exactly fourteen lines.
ode | स्तुतिगान, ओड | The poet wrote a famous ode to autumn and its quiet abundance.
elegy | शोकगीत, मर्सिया | She read a moving elegy at her grandfather's funeral.
ballad | ballad (लोककथा आधारित कविता), गाथागीत | The old Scottish ballad tells the story of two lovers lost at sea.
satire | व्यंग्य | The novel is a sharp satire of life in a small provincial town.
fable | नीतिकथा, द्यूपकथा | Each fable ends with a short moral that children can easily remember.
memoir | संस्मरण, आत्मकथात्मक प्रसंग | In her memoir, the former nurse describes her years in a mountain village.
picaresque | पिकारेस्क (घुमक्कड़ नायक के कारनामों पर आधारित) | The picaresque novel follows a clever young rogue from one adventure to the next.
## कथानक की संरचना
exposition | कथा-परिचय, आरंभिक भाग | The exposition introduces the family and the old house where everything happens.
rising action | तनाव वृद्धि, उत्थान (कथानक का) | During the rising action, the detective slowly uncovers a series of lies.
climax | चरमोत्कर्ष | The climax of the play comes when the brothers finally face each other.
denouement | उपसंहार, रहस्योद्घाट | In the denouement, all the secrets are revealed and the family is reunited.
subplot | उप-कथानक, गौण कथा | A romantic subplot adds some lightness to an otherwise dark story.
foreshadowing | पूर्वसंकेत, आभास | The broken mirror in the first chapter is a clear piece of foreshadowing.
frame narrative | फ्रेम कथा (कथा के भीतर कथा) | The book uses a frame narrative in which travellers tell stories at an inn.
prologue | प्रस्तावना, आमुख | The prologue is set fifty years before the main events of the novel.
epilogue | उपसंहार, अंत्यांश | A short epilogue tells us what happened to each character years later.
in medias res | सीधे घटना के बीच से आरंभ | The story begins in medias res, with the hero already running from the guards.
## कथावाचक और पात्र
omniscient | सर्वज्ञ (सब कुछ जानने वाला) | An omniscient narrator lets us see the thoughts of every character.
unreliable narrator | अविश्वसनीय कथावाचक | We slowly realise that the novel has an unreliable narrator who hides the truth.
first-person | प्रथम पुरुष (दृष्टिकोण) | The story is told in the first-person voice of a twelve-year-old girl.
third-person | तृतीय पुरुष (दृष्टिकोण) | Switching to a third-person narrative gave the author more distance from the hero.
stream of consciousness | चेतना-प्रवाह (मन के विचारों का प्रवाह) | Long passages of stream of consciousness make the novel difficult but rewarding.
interior monologue | आंतरिक एकालाप, स्वगत भाषण | The chapter is a single interior monologue as Emre waits at the station.
protagonist | मुख्य पात्र, नायक | The protagonist is a retired teacher who decides to walk across the country.
antagonist | खलनायक, विरोधी पात्र | The real antagonist turns out to be the hero's own brother.
narrative voice | कथा-शैली, कथा-स्वर | Her distinctive narrative voice is warm, funny and slightly sad.
characterisation | चरित्र-चित्रण | Critics praised the subtle characterisation of the minor figures in the novel.
## आलंकारिक भाषा
metaphor | रूपक | Calling the city a sleeping giant is a metaphor for its hidden power.
simile | उपमा | The phrase 'her eyes were like stars' is a simile that has been used far too often.
personification | मानवीकरण | In the poem, the wind is given a voice through personification.
irony | विडंबना, व्यंगोक्ति | The irony is that the fire station itself burned down last year.
onomatopoeia | ध्वनि-सूचक शब्द (जैसे छपाक, भिनभिनाहट) | Words like buzz and splash are examples of onomatopoeia.
allusion | संदर्भ संकेत, अन्योक्ति | The title is an allusion to an old Greek myth about a flying boy.
imagery | बिंब-विधान, कल्पना-चित्र | The poem is full of vivid imagery of the sea at night.
symbolism | प्रतीकवाद | The author uses colour symbolism, with red always linked to danger.
juxtaposition | साहचर्य, आमने-सामने रखना (तुलना के लिए) | The juxtaposition of rich and poor neighbourhoods is central to the novel.
assonance | स्वर-साम्य, अनुप्रास (स्वरों का) | The repeated long vowels create a gentle assonance in the second line.
## अर्थ की परतें
motif | केंद्रीय प्रसंग, भाव-प्रतीक | Birds are a recurring motif throughout the novel, linked to freedom.
allegory | रूपक कथा, प्रतीक कथा | The story of the farm animals is often read as an allegory of revolution.
dramatic irony | नाट्य विडंबना (दर्शकों को सच पता होना) | Dramatic irony arises when the audience knows something the characters do not.
trope | पारंपरिक साहित्यिक रूढ़ि | The lost twin is a familiar trope in nineteenth-century novels.
epigram | सूक्ति, चमत्कारी लघु कथन | The playwright was famous for witty epigrams about love and money.
bathos | अचानक हास्यास्पद पतन (गंभीर से ओछे की ओर) | The poem ends in bathos, moving from death and glory to a cup of tea.
purple prose | अति-अलंकारिक भाषा, अत्यधिक सजा हुआ गद्य | The editor cut several pages of purple prose describing the sunset.
extended metaphor | विस्तृत रूपक (पूरे प्रसंग में चलने वाला रूपक) | The whole poem is an extended metaphor comparing life to a river journey.
pun | श्लेष, शब्द-युग्म क्रीड़ा | The title contains a clever pun that only works in English.
pathetic fallacy | मानवेतर प्रकृति में मानवीय भावना का आरोपण | The storm that breaks during the funeral is a classic example of pathetic fallacy.
## छंद और कविता के रूप
stanza | श्लोक, पद, छंद-बंध | Each stanza of the poem describes a different season.
couplet | युग्म-छंद, दोहा | The sonnet ends with a rhyming couplet that sums up its message.
quatrain | चतुष्पदी, चौपाई | Traditional ballads are often written in simple quatrains.
metre | छंद-लय, कविता की माप | The poet breaks the regular metre in the last line for dramatic effect.
iambic pentameter | आंबिक पेंटामीटर (दस अक्षरों की अंग्रेजी पद्य पंक्ति) | Most of the play is written in iambic pentameter, with ten syllables per line.
free verse | मुक्त छंद | Modern poets often prefer free verse to strict rhyme and metre.
blank verse | अमित्रक्षरा (बिना काफ़िए की लयबद्ध कविता) | The long poem is written entirely in blank verse, without any rhyme.
refrain | टेक, बार-बार दोहराई जाने वाली पंक्ति | The refrain returns at the end of every stanza, like a heartbeat.
caesura | यति, कविता के बीच का विराम | A strong caesura in the middle of the line forces the reader to pause.
enjambment | पंक्ति-विस्तार (बिना रुके अगली पंक्ति में जाना) | Through enjambment, the sentence spills over into the next line.
## साहित्यिक आंदोलन
Romanticism | स्वच्छंदतावाद, रोमांटिसिज़्म | Romanticism celebrated nature, emotion and the power of the imagination.
Realism | यथार्थवाद | Realism tried to show ordinary life without decoration or idealisation.
Naturalism | प्रकृतिवाद, नैचुरलिज़्म | Naturalism showed characters shaped by poverty, heredity and their environment.
Modernism | आधुनिकतावाद | Modernism broke with traditional forms after the First World War.
Postmodernism | उत्तर-आधुनिकतावाद | Postmodernism often plays with the reader and mixes high and popular culture.
the Gothic | गॉथिक शैली (रहस्य और भय से भरपूर साहित्य) | Gothic fiction is full of ruined castles, family curses and mysterious strangers.
magic realism | जादुई यथार्थवाद | In works of magic realism, ghosts sit at the dinner table as if nothing were strange.
aestheticism | कलावाद, सौंदर्यवाद | Aestheticism defended the idea of art for art's sake.
the avant-garde | अवांट-गार्ड, प्रयोगधर्मी क्रांतिकारी कला | The avant-garde rejected the rules that earlier writers had followed.
existentialism | अस्तित्ववाद | Existentialism shaped many novels about freedom, choice and the absurd.
## आलोचना और व्याख्या

close reading | गहन पठन, सूक्ष्म अध्ययन | A close reading of the first paragraph reveals the whole theme of the book.
subtext | अंतर्निहित अर्थ, उप-पाठ | Beneath the polite conversation, there is a subtext of jealousy.
canon | साहित्यिक प्रामाणिक कृतियाँ (मुख्यधारा का साहित्य) | Should modern graphic novels be part of the literary canon?
intertextuality | अंतर्पाठता (अन्य ग्रंथों के संदर्भ) | The novel's intertextuality rewards readers who know the older myths.
literary theory | साहित्यिक सिद्धांत | Sofia is taking a seminar on literary theory this semester.
deconstruction | विखंडनवाद | Deconstruction questions whether any text can have a single fixed meaning.
explication | विस्तृत व्याख्या, विश्लेषण | The exam asks for a detailed explication of a short unseen poem.
pastiche | अनुकृति, पैस्टिश (अन्य शैली की नकल) | The story is a playful pastiche of classic detective fiction.
textual analysis | पाठ्य विश्लेषण | Our first assignment is a textual analysis of a magazine advertisement.
## शैली और स्वर

terse | संक्षिप्त, अल्पभाषी | His terse style uses short sentences and almost no adjectives.

## चरित्र प्रकार और विषय-वस्तु
archetype | मूल-प्ररूप, आदि-प्ररूप (सार्वभौमिक पात्र/प्रतीक) | The wise old mentor is an archetype found in stories all over the world.
foil | विरोधाभासी पात्र (जो दूसरे की विशेषता उभारे) | The cautious sister acts as a foil to the reckless heroine.
anti-hero | एंटी-हीरो (दोषों से युक्त नायक) | The anti-hero lies and steals, yet readers still want him to succeed.
stock character | पारंपरिक घिसा-पिटा पात्र | The jealous stepmother is a stock character in many fairy tales.
tragic flaw | पतन का कारण, त्रैजिक कमजोरी | His tragic flaw is pride, which leads him to ignore every warning.
catharsis | विरेचन, मनोभावों की शुद्धि (कैथार्सिस) | Watching the tragedy gave the audience a powerful sense of catharsis.
eponymous | ग्रंथ के नाम पर आधारित (पात्र का नाम) | The eponymous heroine of the novel only appears in the second half.
verisimilitude | यथार्थ-आभास, विश्वसनीयता | Small everyday details give the historical novel a strong sense of verisimilitude.
parody | पैरोडी, उपहासपूर्ण नकल | The film is a clever parody of old spy stories.
deus ex machina | अचानक चमत्कारिक समाधान (अचानक संकट टलना) | A rich uncle who suddenly appears in the last chapter is a lazy deus ex machina.
