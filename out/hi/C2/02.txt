# UNIT: समानार्थक क्रियाओं की बारीकियों | देखना, बात करना, चलना, हँसना... सूक्ष्म अंतर
## देखना
gaze | टकटकी लगाकर देखना, एकटक देखना | Maria sat by the window, gazing at the rain for nearly an hour.
stare | घूरना, आँखें फाड़कर देखना | The little girl stared at the giraffe as if she couldn't believe her eyes.
peer | आँखें सिकोड़कर देखना, ध्यान से झाँकना | Diego peered into the dark cellar, trying to find the light switch.
gawk | मुँह बाकर देखना, टकटकी लगाकर बेवकूफों की तरह देखना | Drivers slowed down to gawk at the accident on the other side.
ogle | बुरी नज़र से घूरना, कामुक दृष्टि से देखना | He spent the whole evening ogling the expensive cars outside the hotel.
goggle | आँखें चौंधियाकर देखना, आश्चर्य से आँखें फाड़ना | The children goggled at the magician as the rabbit vanished from his hat.
peek | छिपकर देखना, झाँकना | The children peeked through the curtains to see if the guests had arrived.
rubberneck | उत्सुकतावश गर्दन मोड़कर देखना | Traffic slowed to a crawl as drivers rubbernecked at the burning lorry.
gape | मुँह खुला का खुला रह जाना, अवाक् होकर देखना | The tourists stood gaping at the enormous waterfall.
eyeball | घूरना, तरेरकर देखना | The two rival players eyeballed each other before the match began.
## देखना और पहचानना
glimpse | एक झलक देखना | We glimpsed the castle through the fog before it disappeared again.
discern | भेद पहचानना, स्पष्ट रूप से देखना | In the dim light, it was hard to discern any faces in the crowd.
perceive | समझना, अनुभव करना | Many patients perceive the new treatment as less painful.
detect | पता लगाना, ताड़ लेना | Yuki detected a slight note of sarcasm in his reply.
catch sight of | नज़र आना, दिखाई देना | Emre caught sight of his old teacher across the busy station.
make out | पहचान पाना, ज़रा-सा देखना | I could just make out the shape of a boat on the horizon.
scan | सरसरी निगाह डालना, खंगालना | Sofia scanned the room for a familiar face before sitting down.
skim | सरसरी तौर पर पढ़ना | I only had time to skim the report on the train this morning.
peruse | ध्यान से पढ़ना, मुआयना करना | Lukas perused the wine list for a long time before ordering water.
spy | दूर से देखना, ताक लेना | From the hilltop, Omar spied a small cottage hidden among the trees.
## फुसफुसाना और बड़बड़ाना
whisper | फुसफुसाना, कानाफूसी करना | Emre leaned over and whispered the answer to his classmate.
chatter | गपशप करना, बकबक करना | The two friends chattered away happily on the long train journey.
gabble | लड़खड़ाते हुए जल्दी-जल्दी बोलना | In her excitement, Sofia gabbled the whole story in about ten seconds.
jabber | अनाप-शनाप बकना, जल्दी-जल्दी अस्पष्ट बोलना | The tourists were jabbering in a language none of us could recognise.
stutter | हकलाना | As a child, Ali stuttered, but speech therapy helped him enormously.
babble | बड़बड़ाना, तुतलाना | The baby babbled happily while her parents cooked dinner.
blather | बकवास करना, फालतू बोलना | He blathered on about his new car while everyone else looked bored.
lisp | तोतलाना, रुक-रुक कर बोलना | The little boy lisped a little when he tried to say his sister's name.
prattle | बचकानी बातें करना, लगातार बकना | She prattled on about her holiday while we tried to watch the film.
witter | फ़िज़ूल बातें करते रहना | My neighbour wittered on about the weather for twenty minutes.
## चिल्लाना और गुर्राना
yell | चिल्लाना, पुकारना | The coach yelled instructions from the side of the pitch.
squeal | चीखना, चहकना | The tyres squealed as the car turned the corner too fast.
roar | दहाड़ना, गर्जना | The crowd roared when the winning goal went in.
shriek | चीख मारना, चिल्लाना | The children shrieked with delight as the waves splashed them.
screech | तीखी आवाज़ में चीखना | A parrot screeched from its cage every time the doorbell rang.
squawk | काँव-काँव करना, चिल्लाकर शिकायत करना | The passengers squawked about the delay, but nothing could be done.
howl | चिल्लाना, चीखना | The wind howled around the house all night long.
hiss | फुफकारना, फुसफुसाकर कहना | "Be quiet," she hissed. "They'll hear us!"
growl | गुर्राना, रूखेपन से बोलना | "Get out of my office," the director growled without looking up.
snarl | गुस्से से दाँत दिखाना, तराना | The tired waiter snarled at a customer who complained about the soup.
## चलना
stroll | टहलना, सैर करना | On Sunday evenings, couples stroll along the seafront.
wander | भटकना, इधर-उधर घूमना | We wandered through the old town with no particular plan.
roam | विचरण करना, घूमना-फिरना | Herds of wild ponies roam freely across the moors.
stride | लंबे कदम भरकर चलना | Camille strode confidently across the stage to accept the award.
lumber | भारी कदमों से चलना, लड़खड़ाना | A huge bear lumbered out of the forest towards the river.
pace | चक्कर काटना, टहलना | Omar paced up and down the corridor while waiting for news.
tramp | भारी कदमों से पैदल चलना | They tramped across the muddy fields for hours in the rain.
wade | पानी में चलकर पार करना | The fishermen waded into the river up to their knees.
limp | लंगड़ाना | Emre limped off the pitch after twisting his ankle.
dawdle | सुस्ती से चलना, देर करना | Stop dawdling or we'll miss the start of the film!
## दौड़ना और तेज़ी से जाना
dash | दौड़ पड़ना, झपट्टा मारना | Maria dashed out of the house when she heard the bus coming.
bolt | भाग निकलना, चंपत हो जाना | The horse bolted when a car horn sounded behind it.
race | दौड़ लगाना | Anna raced up the stairs to answer the phone.
streak | तेज़ी से निकल जाना | A fox streaked across the road in front of our car.
hurtle | तेज़ी से और अनियंत्रित रूप से आगे बढ़ना | A huge rock hurtled down the mountain towards the road.
dart | तीर की तरह तेज़ी से भागना | A small fish darted out from behind the coral.
scoot | झटपट जाना, फुर्र हो जाना | I'll just scoot down to the shop before it closes.
hasten | जल्दी करना, फुर्ती दिखाना | The waiter hastened to apologise when he saw the broken glass.
zoom | तेज़ी से गुज़रना, रफ़्तार से निकलना | Delivery scooters zoomed past us on the narrow street.
gallop | सरपट दौड़ना | The riders galloped across the open field at sunrise.
## हँसना और मुस्कुराना
giggle | खिलखिलाकर हँसना | The girls giggled at the back of the classroom during the lesson.
chuckle | मन ही मन हँसना, मंद-मंद मुस्कुराना | Omar chuckled quietly as he read the funny message.
grin | दाँत निपोरकर हँसना, मुस्कुराना | Ali grinned from ear to ear when he heard he had passed.
chortle | आनंद से हँसना | Grandpa chortled at his own joke before he even finished it.
hoot | ठहाका मारना | The audience hooted with laughter at the comedian's first joke.
cackle | कर्कश आवाज़ में हँसना | The witch in the play cackled as she stirred her pot.
crease up | हँसते-हँसते लोटपोट होना | His impression of the headteacher had us all creasing up.
be in stitches | हँसते-हँसते पेट में बल पड़ना | The film was so funny that we were in stitches the whole time.
split one's sides | हँसते-हँसते पागल हो जाना | The children split their sides laughing at the clown's tricks.
fall about laughing | ठहाके मारकर हँसना | When Lukas fell into the pool fully dressed, we fell about laughing.
## रोना
sob | सिसक-सिसक कर रोना | Sofia sobbed on her friend's shoulder after hearing the news.
weep (wept, wept) | आँसू बहाना, रोना | The old man wept silently at his wife's grave.
tear up | आँखें नम होना | Camille teared up when her students sang her a goodbye song.
keen | विलाप करना, शोक मनाना | In the old story, the women keened over the fallen hero all night.
bawl | ज़ोर-ज़ोर से चिल्लाकर रोना | The toddler bawled because his ice cream had fallen on the ground.
blubber | फफक-फफक कर रोना | Stop blubbering and tell me what actually happened.
mewl | मचलना, मद्धम आवाज़ में रोना | A hungry kitten mewled in the box by the door.
snivel | सिसकना, नाक सिकोड़कर रोना | He snivelled about losing the game instead of congratulating the winner.
cry one's eyes out | फूट-फूटकर रोना | Maria cried her eyes out when her old dog died.
choke back tears | आँसू रोकना | Diego choked back tears as he said goodbye to his team.
## पकड़ना और खींचना
grab | झपट लेना, पकड़ना | Diego grabbed his coat and ran out to catch the train.
seize | दबोच लेना, कब्ज़ा करना | The guard seized the thief by the arm before he could escape.
snatch | छीन लेना | A seagull snatched the sandwich right out of my hand.
grasp | मुट्ठी में भींचना, समझना | She grasped the rope with both hands and started to climb.
grip | कसकर पकड़ना | The nervous passenger gripped the armrest during the landing.
clasp | मजबूती से थामना, गले लगाना | The two old friends clasped hands warmly at the airport.
cling (clung, clung) | लिपट जाना, चिपके रहना | The frightened child clung to her mother's leg in the crowd.
yank | झटके से खींचना | Omar yanked the door open and shouted for help.
tug | हल्का सा खींचना | The little boy tugged at his father's sleeve to get his attention.
haul | खींचना, घसीटना | The fishermen hauled their heavy nets onto the deck.
## खाना और पीना
gobble | ठूंस-ठूंस कर खाना, झटपट खाना | The kids gobbled their dinner so they could go back outside.
devour | चट कर जाना, भूखों की तरह खाना | The hungry hikers devoured every piece of bread on the table.
nibble | कुतरना, थोड़ा-थोड़ा खाना | Camille nibbled a biscuit while she waited for the results.
munch | चबा-चबाकर खाना | He sat on the sofa munching crisps during the whole film.
wolf down | गटक जाना, भूखे भेड़िये की तरह खाना | Ali wolfed down his breakfast and rushed to the office.
gulp | गटकना, एक बार में पीना | She gulped her water after the long run in the heat.
sip | घूंट-घूंट करके पीना | Anna sipped her tea slowly on the balcony.
slurp | आवाज़ करके पीना | It's considered rude to slurp your soup in many countries.
swig | बोतल से सीधे घटकना | The cyclist swigged from his bottle without stopping.
guzzle | धड़ल्ले से पीना, बहुत ज़्यादा गटकना | Old cars guzzle fuel, so many people are switching to electric ones.
