# UNIT: फ्रेज़ल वर्ब्स: get, take, put, come | मुहावरे और वाक्यांश
## get के साथ मुहावरे: समझना और पहचानना
get the drift | बात समझ जाना | I won't explain every detail, but I think you get the drift.
get the gist | मुख्य बात समझना | I didn't catch every word, but I got the gist of his argument.
get the picture | माजरा समझ जाना | Nobody called her back for a week, so she finally got the picture.
get to the bottom of | तह तक पहुँचना | The detective swore she would get to the bottom of the missing paintings.
get wise to | भेद भाप लेना | Customers eventually got wise to the company's hidden fees and left.
get one's wires crossed | ग़लतफ़हमी होना | We must have got our wires crossed, because I waited at the wrong café.
get the message | इशारा समझ जाना | When he kept yawning loudly, the guests finally got the message and left.
get a word in edgeways | बीच में कुछ न बोल पाना | Omar talked so much at dinner that nobody could get a word in edgeways.
get one's bearings | दिशा ढूँढना | It took Yuki a few weeks to get her bearings in the new department.
get ahead of oneself | बहुत आगे की सोचना | Let's not get ahead of ourselves; we haven't even signed the contract yet.
## get के साथ मुहावरे: बदला और भाग्य
get even with | बदला लेना | He spent years plotting to get even with the partner who had cheated him.
get one's own back | हिसाब चुकाना | After the prank, Sofia got her own back by hiding his car keys.
get a kick out of | मज़ा आना | My grandfather gets a real kick out of beating us at chess.
get the short end of the stick | नुक़सान उठाना | In every group project, the quietest student gets the short end of the stick.
get away with murder | बच निकलना | The youngest child gets away with murder because everyone spoils him.
get a raw deal | अन्याय सहना | The workers felt they had got a raw deal from the new management.
get the better of | हावी होना | In the end, curiosity got the better of her and she opened the letter.
get a rise out of | चिढ़ाना | He only says those things to get a rise out of you, so ignore him.
get off lightly | कम सज़ा पाना | Considering the damage, you got off lightly with just a small fine.
get one's comeuppance | करनी की सज़ा पाना | The arrogant villain gets his comeuppance in the final chapter.
## get के साथ मुहावरे: कार्रवाई करना
get one's act together | सुधार करना | If you don't get your act together, you'll fail the final exams.
get a grip | संयम रखना | Get a grip, Lukas; it's only a job interview, not the end of the world.
get carried away | बहक जाना | I got a bit carried away at the market and bought far too much cheese.
get a move on | जल्दी करना | We'd better get a move on or we'll miss the last ferry to the island.
get cracking | तुरंत शुरू करना | The deadline is on Friday, so let's get cracking on the report.
get round to | समय निकालना | I finally got round to fixing the leaking tap after three months.
get a second wind | फिर से ऊर्जा पाना | Halfway through the marathon, Diego got a second wind and sped up.
get the green light | हरी झंडी मिलना | The architects finally got the green light to start building the bridge.
get nowhere fast | प्रगति न होना | Arguing with the ticket machine, we were clearly getting nowhere fast.
get one's skates on | जल्दी करना (ब्रितानी) | Get your skates on, Anna, the taxi has been waiting for ten minutes.
## take के साथ मुहावरे: रवैया और प्रतिक्रिया
take something in one's stride | सहजता से लेना | She took the sudden promotion in her stride and calmly reorganised the team.
take umbrage at | बुरा मानना | The chef took umbrage at the critic's comments about his signature dish.
take exception to | आपत्ति करना | Several members took exception to the chairman's dismissive tone.
take a dim view of | नापसंद करना | The headmistress takes a dim view of students using phones in class.
take heed of | ध्यान देना | Climbers who fail to take heed of the weather warnings put lives at risk.
take a hard line | कड़ा रुख अपनाना | The government has decided to take a hard line on tax evasion this year.
take the high road | गरिमा बनाए रखना | Instead of replying to the insults, Emre chose to take the high road.
take it or leave it | मंजूर हो या न हो | This is my final offer for the car, so take it or leave it.
take the heat | आँच सहना | When the launch failed, the project manager had to take the heat alone.
take someone to task | डाँटना | The editor took the young reporter to task for not checking his sources.
## take के साथ मुहावरे: पहल और बदलाव
take the reins | कमान संभालना | When her father retired, Sofia took the reins of the family hotel.
take stock of | जायजा लेना | After the crisis, the company paused to take stock of its finances.
take a toll on | असर डालना | Years of night shifts had taken a heavy toll on his health.
take the edge off | असर कम करना | A cup of strong tea took the edge off the cold winter morning.
take a back seat | पीछे हट जाना | After retiring, she was happy to take a back seat and let her son run the farm.
take a stab at | हाथ आजमाना | I've never baked bread before, but I'll take a stab at it this weekend.
take root | जड़ पकड़ना | The idea of a four-day working week has slowly taken root in many firms.
take shape | रूप लेना | As the weeks passed, the plans for the new museum began to take shape.
take a turn for the worse | बदतर होना | The patient's condition took a turn for the worse during the night.
take the floor | बोलना शुरू करना | When Maria took the floor, the noisy conference hall fell silent.
## take के साथ मुहावरे: सामाजिक संबंध
take a pew | बैठ जाना (बोलचाल) | Come in and take a pew; the kettle has just boiled.
take a leaf out of someone's book | अनुकरण करना | You should take a leaf out of Ali's book and save a little every month.
take pride of place | प्रमुख स्थान पाना | Her grandmother's old clock takes pride of place in the living room.
take the liberty of | धृष्टता करना | I took the liberty of booking a table for us at the seafood restaurant.
take the rap for | इल्जाम अपने सिर लेना | He refused to take the rap for a mistake his manager had made.
take it upon oneself | ज़िम्मेदारी लेना | Camille took it upon herself to organise the neighbourhood clean-up.
take the bait | झांसे में आना | He tried to provoke me with a rude comment, but I didn't take the bait.
take someone's word for it | भरोसा करना | I haven't seen the film, but I'll take your word for it that it's brilliant.
take the easy way out | आसान रास्ता चुनना | Quitting would be taking the easy way out, so she decided to keep trying.
take one's leave | विदा लेना (औपचारिक) | The ambassador bowed politely and took his leave before midnight.
## put के साथ मुहावरे: बाधा डालना और समाप्त करना
put a damper on | पानी फेरना | The sudden storm put a damper on the village summer festival.
put paid to | उम्मीदों पर पानी फेरना | A serious knee injury put paid to his dreams of playing professionally.
put a spanner in the works | अड़ंगा डालना | The strike at the port has put a spanner in the works for our delivery.
put something on the back burner | ठंडे बस्ते में डालना | We've had to put the kitchen renovation on the back burner this year.
put something to bed | निपटा देना | Let's put this issue to bed before we move on to the budget.
put up a fight | मुकाबला करना | The defending champions put up a good fight but lost in the final minutes.
put someone on the spot | मुश्किल में डालना | The journalist put the minister on the spot with an unexpected question.
put someone in their place | औकात याद दिलाना | When he interrupted her again, she calmly put him in his place.
put one's oar in | दखल देना | Nobody asked for his opinion, but he still had to put his oar in.
put someone through their paces | परीक्षा लेना | The coach put the new players through their paces on the first day.
## put के साथ मुहावरे: विचार और कथन
put two and two together | जोड़ना | When I saw the empty cake box and his guilty smile, I put two and two together.
put the record straight | सफ़ाई देना | The author gave an interview to put the record straight about her past.
to put it mildly | सीधे शब्दों में कहें तो | The hotel was not exactly luxurious, to put it mildly.
put out feelers | टटोलना | The company has put out feelers to see if anyone wants to buy the brand.
put pen to paper | लिखना शुरू करना | It took him ten years to finally put pen to paper and write his memoirs.
put a premium on | को प्राथमिकता देना | Our school puts a premium on creativity rather than memorisation.
put stock in | विश्वास करना | I don't put much stock in horoscopes or fortune tellers.
put in a good word for | सिफ़ारिश करना | Could you put in a good word for me with the head of marketing?
put one's cards on the table | पत्ते खोलना | Let me put my cards on the table: we can't afford to pay more than this.
put something into perspective | सही परिप्रेक्ष्य में देखना | Visiting the hospital put my small problems into perspective.
## come के साथ मुहावरे: स्वीकार करना और प्रकट होना
come to terms with | स्वीकार करना | It took him a long time to come to terms with his father's death.
come clean | सच उगलना | Eventually she came clean and admitted she had broken the vase.
come to light | सामने आना | New evidence came to light that proved the prisoner was innocent.
come to grips with | समझ बनाना | Older staff are still coming to grips with the new software.
come of age | बालिग होना | When the twins came of age, they inherited the family vineyard.
come into one's own | चमक उठना | The quiet student really came into her own in the debating club.
come to the fore | आगे आना | During the flood, the town's sense of solidarity came to the fore.
come to pass | पूरा होना (साहित्यिक) | Everything the old woman had predicted eventually came to pass.
come full circle | घूम-फिरकर वहीं आना | After decades abroad, he came full circle and returned to his home village.
come out of one's shell | संकोच छोड़ना | Since joining the theatre group, our shy son has come out of his shell.
## come के साथ मुहावरे: परिणाम और शर्तें
come to the rescue | मदद के लिए आना | When our car broke down, a friendly farmer came to the rescue with his tractor.
come to blows | हाथापाई होना | The two neighbours almost came to blows over a parking space.
come what may | चाहे जो हो जाए | Come what may, I'll be there for your wedding next spring.
come as no surprise | हैरानी की बात न होना | It will come as no surprise that the trains are late again.
come down to earth | हकीकत का सामना करना | After the holiday, it was hard to come down to earth and face my inbox.
come apart at the seams | चरमरा जाना | Without a strong leader, the coalition began to come apart at the seams.
come under fire | आलोचना का शिकार होना | The new rules have come under fire from teachers and parents alike.
come off second best | हार जाना | Whenever my brother argues with our grandmother, he comes off second best.
come in handy | काम आना | Keep that old rope in the car; it might come in handy someday.
come to nothing | व्यर्थ हो जाना | All their careful plans for the expedition came to nothing in the end.
