# UNIT: Phrasal verbs III | उन्नत phrasal verbs
## Çatışma ve geri adım
square up to | मुकाबला करना, भिड़ना | The two players squared up to each other before the referee stepped in.
round on | अचानक हमला करना, झपट्टा मारना | When I asked about the money, Lukas suddenly rounded on me in front of everyone.
shout down | चिल्लाकर चुप कराना, दबाना | Every time the speaker tried to answer, the angry crowd shouted him down.
back down | पीछे हटना, अपनी बात से मुकरना | Neither side was willing to back down, so the talks went on all night.
climb down | झुकना, अपनी जिद छोड़ना | After a week of protests, the management had to climb down and cancel the new rules.
have it out with (had, had) | साफ़-साफ़ बात करके हिसाब चुकाना | I finally had it out with my flatmate about the dirty dishes.
flare up | भड़क उठना, अचानक उग्र होना | Old tensions between the two families flared up again at the wedding.
stir up | भड़काना, आग लगाना | Someone has been stirring up trouble between the night shift and the day shift.
rub in | बार-बार याद दिलाकर चिढ़ाना | I know I lost the bet, Omar, there's no need to rub it in.
gang up on | एकजुट होकर परेशान करना | The older boys used to gang up on Diego after school.
## Aldatma ve hile
take in (took, taken) | धोखा देना, बेवकूफ बनाना | Even experienced collectors were taken in by the clever fake painting.
string along (strung, strung) | झूठी उम्मीद देना, लटकाए रखना | He strung her along for two years without ever planning to marry her.
palm off on | थमाना, चिपकाना | The dealer tried to palm off a broken watch on an unsuspecting tourist.
rip off | ठगना, चूना लगाना | Tourists often get ripped off by taxi drivers near the station.
cook up | मनगढ़ंत कहानी बनाना, गढ़ना | Ali cooked up a story about a flat tyre to explain why he was late.
trump up | झूठा इल्ज़ाम मढ़ना | The journalist was arrested on trumped-up charges and released a month later.
cover up | पर्दा डालना, छुपाना | The company tried to cover up the accident, but a worker told the press.
sell out (sold, sold) | अपने उसूल बेचना, बिक जाना | Fans accused the band of selling out when they started writing advertising songs.
put one over on (put, put) | मूर्ख बनाना, मात देना | Nobody puts one over on my grandmother, she checks every receipt twice.
play along | हाँ में हाँ मिलाना, नाटक करना | I knew it was a surprise party, but I played along and acted shocked.
## İkna ve baskı
goad into | उकसाना, मजबूर करना | His friends goaded him into jumping from the bridge into the cold river.
talk out of | समझा-बुझाकर रोकना | Her friends tried to talk her out of quitting her job, but she had decided.
railroad into | दबाव डालकर मनवाना | Don't let the salesman railroad you into signing the contract today.
bully into | डरा-धमकाकर मजबूर करना | The workers were bullied into accepting longer hours without extra pay.
coax out of | प्यार से मनाकर निकालना | Yuki gently coaxed the frightened kitten out of its hiding place under the sofa.
butter up | मक्खन लगाना, चापलूसी करना | Emre has been buttering up his boss all week because he wants Friday off.
prevail upon | राजी करना (औपचारिक) | We prevailed upon the professor to give one more lecture before retiring.
hold out for (held, held) | बेहतर चीज़ के लिए अड़े रहना | The union is holding out for a ten percent pay rise.
jolly along | हँसा-फुसलाकर हौसला बढ़ाना | The guide jollied the tired hikers along with jokes and promises of hot soup.
push for | ज़ोरदार माँग करना | Local residents are pushing for a new crossing near the primary school.
## Duygular ve ruh hali
choke up | गला रुँध जाना | Lukas choked up halfway through his speech at his sister's wedding.
pine for | तरसना, तड़पना | Living abroad, Omar often pined for his mother's cooking and the noise of home.
perk up | तरोताज़ा होना, खिल उठना | Emre perked up immediately when he heard there was cake in the kitchen.
buck up | हिम्मत रखना, सँभल जाना | Buck up, Sofia, one bad exam result isn't the end of the world.
hole up | दुबक जाना, छिप बैठना | After the break-up, he holed up in his flat and refused to see anyone.
simmer down | शांत होना | Simmer down, everyone, we can discuss this calmly after lunch.
lighten up | तनावमुक्त होना, हल्का-फुल्का होना | Lighten up, it was only a joke and nobody meant to upset you.
psych up | मानसिक रूप से तैयार करना, जोश भरना | The team psyched themselves up with loud music before the big match.
boil over | गुस्सा फूट पड़ना | Months of frustration finally boiled over into a furious argument.
tense up | तनाव में आ जाना, अकड़ जाना | Ali tenses up whenever someone asks him about his exam results.
## Para ve hesap
stump up | अनिच्छा से पैसे चुकाना | The parents had to stump up another hundred euros for the school trip.
cough up | ना चाहते हुए भी पैसे देना | Come on, Lukas, cough up, you still owe me for the concert tickets.
tot up | कुल जोड़ना | Camille totted up the bill and was shocked by how much we had spent.
rack up | इकट्ठा करना, बढ़ाना | Within a year he had racked up huge debts on three different credit cards.
siphon off | गबन करना, चुपके से निकालना | The manager had been siphoning off company funds into a private account.
salt away | पैसे बचाकर रखना | Over the years she had salted away enough money to buy a small cottage.
squirrel away | छुपाकर रखना, जमा करना | My grandfather squirrelled away cash in old biscuit tins all over the house.
buy out (bought, bought) | हिस्सा खरीद लेना | Anna bought out her business partner and now runs the bakery alone.
buy up (bought, bought) | भारी मात्रा में खरीद लेना | Investors are buying up old houses in the village and turning them into hotels.
mark down | दाम घटाना | All the winter coats have been marked down by forty percent.
## Anlama ve öğrenme
puzzle out | माथापच्ची करके हल करना | It took us an hour to puzzle out the instructions for the new printer.
chew over | अच्छी तरह विचार करना | Let me chew it over this weekend and I'll give you an answer on Monday.
sink in | बात समझ में आना, दिमाग में बैठना | It took a few days for the news of her promotion to sink in.
hit upon (hit, hit) | अचानक सूझना, विचार आना | While washing the dishes, Omar hit upon a brilliant name for the café.
latch onto | चिपक जाना, पकड़ बनाना | The media latched onto one careless remark and ignored the rest of the speech.
cotton on | देर से समझ में आना | It took me ages to cotton on that they were planning a surprise for me.
catch on (caught, caught) | लोकप्रिय होना, चलन में आना | The new dance caught on quickly and soon everyone at school was doing it.
bone up on | सरसरी तौर पर पढ़ना, तैयारी करना | Before the interview, Diego boned up on the company's recent projects.
swot up on | रट्टा मारना, घुलकर पढ़ना | Yuki spent the whole weekend swotting up on chemistry formulas.
wise up | अक्ल ठिकाने आना, समझदार होना | It's time you wised up and realised that he's never going to change.
## Konuşma tarzları
rattle off | फटाफट बोल जाना | The waiter rattled off a list of twelve desserts without taking a breath.
trail off | आवाज़ धीमी पड़ जाना | Her voice trailed off as she noticed everyone was staring at her.
pipe up | बीच में बोल पड़ना | A small boy at the back piped up with a surprisingly clever question.
harp on | एक ही बात बार-बार दोहराना | My uncle keeps harping on about how cheap everything was in his day.
drone on | उबाऊ आवाज़ में बोलते जाना | The lecturer droned on for two hours and half the students fell asleep.
rabbit on | बकवास करना, बिना रुके बोलना | Sofia was rabbiting on about her holiday and didn't notice I was bored.
clam up | चुप हो जाना, मुँह न खोलना | Whenever we ask about his past, Lukas clams up and changes the subject.
sound off | बढ़-चढ़कर बोलना, डींग हाँकना | He loves sounding off about politics at family dinners.
mouth off | बद्तमीज़ी से बोलना, मुँह चलाना | The player was sent off for mouthing off at the referee.
gush over | अत्यधिक प्रशंसा करना | All the relatives gushed over the new baby's tiny hands and feet.
## Kaçınma ve sorumluluktan kaçma
duck out of | ज़िम्मेदारी से बचना, कतराना | Diego always manages to duck out of washing up after dinner.
cop out | ज़िम्मेदारी से पीछे हटना | Saying you're too busy is just a way of copping out of the decision.
chicken out | डरकर पीछे हट जाना | Anna was going to do a bungee jump, but she chickened out at the last minute.
shy away from | कतराना, दूर रहना | Good leaders don't shy away from difficult conversations with their teams.
shrink from (shrank, shrunk) | घबराना, हिचकिचाना | She never shrank from telling the truth, even when it was painful.
steer clear of | दूर रहना, बचकर रहना | If I were you, I'd steer clear of that restaurant, the fish isn't fresh.
weasel out of | चालाकी से बचना | He always tries to weasel out of paying his share of the bill.
beg off | बहाना बनाकर छुट्टी लेना | Camille begged off the dinner, saying she had a terrible headache.
pull out | पीछे हट जाना | The sponsor pulled out of the festival just two weeks before it opened.
back away from | मुकर जाना, वादा तोड़ना | The government has backed away from its promise to build new hospitals.
## Azim ve çaba
plough on | डटे रहना, संघर्ष जारी रखना | Despite the rain, the volunteers ploughed on until the whole beach was clean.
press ahead | दृढ़ता से आगे बढ़ना | The city is pressing ahead with plans for a new tram line.
soldier on | हिम्मत न हारना, जुटे रहना | Although she had a cold, Maria soldiered on and finished the report.
plug away at | लगातार मेहनत करना | Emre has been plugging away at his novel every evening for three years.
slog away | कड़ी मेहनत करना, पसीना बहाना | We slogged away in the hot kitchen from early morning until midnight.
buckle down | कमर कस लेना, मन लगाकर जुटना | If you want to pass, you'll need to buckle down and start revising now.
forge ahead | तेज़ी से आगे निकलना | The small start-up forged ahead and soon overtook its bigger rivals.
pitch in | सहयोग करना, हाथ बँटााना | When the storm damaged the school, the whole village pitched in to repair it.
step up | ज़िम्मेदारी संभालना, आगे आना | When the captain was injured, the youngest player stepped up and scored twice.
see through (saw, seen) | अंजाम तक पहुँचाना, पूरा करना | It's a difficult project, but Yuki is determined to see it through.
## Ortaya çıkma ve sonuçlanma
spring up (sprang, sprung) | अचानक पैदा होना, उग आना | New coffee shops have sprung up all over the neighbourhood this year.
fall through (fell, fallen) | रद्द होना, खटाई में पड़ना | Our plans to buy the flat fell through when the bank refused the loan.
dry up | सूख जाना, समाप्त होना | After the factory closed, work in the town completely dried up.
wear off (wore, worn) | असर कम होना | The painkillers started to wear off just as the film reached its climax.
boil down to | सार निकलना, बात यहीं आना | In the end, the whole argument boils down to a question of money.
pan out | परिणाम निकलना, सफल होना | Let's wait and see how things pan out before we make any decisions.
play out | घटित होना, सामने आना | The drama played out in front of millions of viewers on live television.
round off | शानदार अंत करना | We rounded off the evening with a walk along the harbour.
polish off | चट कर जाना, सफ़ाया करना | The kids polished off the whole pizza in less than five minutes.
shoot up (shot, shot) | तेज़ी से बढ़ना, उछाल आना | Rents in the city centre have shot up since the new metro line opened.
