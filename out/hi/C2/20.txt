# UNIT: वक्तृत्व और भाषण कला | भाषण कला, अलंकार, वाद-विवाद, भ्रामक तर्क, प्रस्तुति भाषा
## भाषण कला
oratory | वक्तृत्व, भाषण कला | Her oratory moved the crowd to tears on the final night of the campaign.
orator | वक्ता, भाषणकर्ता | Omar was a gifted orator who never needed notes on stage.
rhetorician | अलंकारशास्त्री, वक्तृत्व विशेषज्ञ | The ancient rhetorician taught his pupils to argue both sides of any case.
eloquence | वाग्मिता, बोलने की कला | Sofia spoke with such eloquence that even her rivals applauded.
declamation | सस्वर पाठ, उच्च स्वर में भाषण | The students practised declamation by reciting famous speeches in the hall.
peroration | भाषण का उपसंहार | His peroration built slowly to a thunderous call for unity.
exordium | भाषण की प्रस्तावना | A good exordium wins the goodwill of the audience before any argument begins.
ethos | वक्ता की विश्वसनीयता | Her years as a nurse gave her ethos when she spoke about hospital funding.
pathos | भावनात्मक अपील, पाथोस | The speech relied heavily on pathos, with stories of lost children and broken homes.
logos | तार्किक प्रमाण, लोगोज़ | Lukas preferred logos to emotion, filling his talk with data and careful reasoning.
## अलंकार I
anaphora | शब्दों की पुनरावृत्ति (आरंभ में) | The poet used anaphora, starting every line with the words "we shall".
antithesis | विरोधाभास | The line "it was the best of times, it was the worst of times" is a classic antithesis.
chiasmus | विपरीत क्रमीय विन्यास (चियास्मस) | "Ask not what you can do for words but what words can do for you" is a playful chiasmus.
asyndeton | संयोजक-लोप (असिंडेटन) | "I came, I saw, I conquered" is the most famous example of asyndeton.
litotes | न्यूनोक्ति, अल्पोक्ति | Calling the disaster "not a total success" is a typical example of litotes.
synecdoche | लक्षणा (अंश के लिए पूर्ण का प्रयोग) | In the phrase "all hands on deck", "hands" is a synecdoche for sailors.
metonymy | लक्षणा, नाम-परिवर्तन | Using "the crown" to mean the monarchy is a common metonymy.
oxymoron | विरोधाभास (परस्पर विरोधी शब्द) | "Deafening silence" is a well-known oxymoron that writers love.
alliteration | अनुप्रास | The slogan used alliteration to make the brand name easy to remember.
prosopopoeia | मानवीकरण | Through prosopopoeia, the poet let the old river speak about its own past.
## अलंकार II
tricolon | त्रय-अलंकार, तीन पदों का क्रम | Her speech ended with a tricolon: we came, we listened, we acted.
epistrophe | अंत्य-पुनरावृत्ति | The writer used epistrophe, ending each sentence with the same phrase for effect.
zeugma | युग्म-अलंकार (ज़ेग्मा) | "She lost her keys and her temper" is a neat example of zeugma.
paralipsis | उपेक्षा-अलंकार (कहकर छोड़ देना) | "I will not even mention his debts" was a sly piece of paralipsis.
aposiopesis | अधूरा छोड़ देना (कथन रोकना) | "If you ever do that again, I will..." she said, using aposiposises to sound threatening.
polysyndeton | बहुसंयोजक प्रयोग | The story used polysyndeton: "and it rained and it thundered and nobody slept".
dysphemism | अपशब्द, अमंगल प्रयोग | Calling the hospital "a meat factory" was a deliberate dysphemism.
understatement | बात को हल्का करके कहना | Saying the storm was "a bit windy" was quite an understatement.
periphrasis | घुमा-फिरा कर कहना, पर्याय | The novelist loved periphrasis and never simply wrote the word "died".
rhetorical question | अलंकारिक प्रश्न, उत्तरहीन प्रश्न | "Who among us has never made a mistake?" was clearly a rhetorical question.
## वाद-विवाद और चर्चा
interlocutor | वार्ताकार, संवाद-साथी | Yuki listened closely to her interlocutor before offering a counterpoint.
gainsay | खंडन करना, विरोध करना | Nobody could gainsay the facts that Yuki laid out so calmly.
controvert | वाद-विवाद करना, प्रतिवाद करना | The young researcher dared to controvert the views of her famous supervisor.
rejoinder | करारा जवाब, तीखा प्रतिउत्तर | Her witty rejoinder left the whole panel laughing.
riposte | हाजिरजवाबी, तुरंत जवाब | He delivered a quick riposte before the moderator could intervene.
polemic | तीखी बहस, विवाद लेख | Diego wrote a fierce polemic against the plan to close the village school.
devil's advocate | विरोधी का पक्ष लेने वाला (डेविल्स एडवोकेट) | Let me play devil's advocate and argue that the old system worked better.
have the floor | बोलने का अधिकार होना | Please wait your turn; Camille has the floor at the moment.
pick holes in | नुक्ताचीनी करना, खामी निकालना | The judges picked holes in every argument the team presented.
dialectic | द्वंद्ववाद, तर्क-वितर्क पद्धति | The seminar followed a dialectic in which every thesis met its opposite.
## भ्रामक तर्क
tu quoque | "तुम भी तो वही हो" तर्क (तू क्वोके) | Answering criticism by saying "you do it too" is the tu quoque fallacy.
straw man | मनगढ़ंत कमज़ोर तर्क (स्ट्रॉ मैन) | Instead of answering me, you attacked a straw man I never built.
ad hominem | व्यक्तिपरक हमला (ऐड होमिनम) | Calling him lazy instead of discussing his idea was pure ad hominem.
moving the goalposts | नियम बदलना, शर्तें बदलना | Every time we met his demands, he added new ones; that is moving the goalposts.
slippery slope | ढलान वाला भ्रामक तर्क (स्लिपररी स्लोप) | Saying one dessert will lead to total ruin is a slippery slope argument.
false dilemma | झूठा विकल्प, द्विविधा | "Either you agree with me or you hate progress" is a false dilemma.
circular reasoning | चक्राकार तर्क | "He is honest because he says he is honest" is a case of circular reasoning.
non sequitur | असंगत निष्कर्ष (नॉन सेक्विटुर) | Concluding that she is a great cook because she owns many pans is a non sequitur.
sophistry | कुतर्क, छल-प्रपंच | His clever sophistry could not hide the weakness of his position.
specious | ऊपरी तौर पर सही दिखने वाला | The salesman offered a specious argument that sounded convincing at first.
## मनाने की तकनीकें
cajole | फुसलाना, मीठी बातें करना | Ali cajoled his little sister into tidying the whole room.
exhort | प्रोत्साहित करना, प्रेरणा देना | The coach exhorted the players to give everything in the final minutes.
wheedle | चापलूसी से मनाना | The children wheedled an extra hour of television out of their grandmother.
inveigle | बहलाना-फुसलाना, जाल में फँसाना | She inveigled her way into the private meeting by posing as a reporter.
importune | बार-बार जिद्द करना, तंग करना | The street vendors importuned every tourist who walked past the harbour.
adjure | कसम दिलाना, गंभीर आग्रह करना | Her mother adjured her to tell the whole truth before it was too late.
browbeat | धमकाना, डराना-धमकाना | The manager tried to browbeat the staff into working unpaid overtime.
blandishment | चापलूसी, लुभावनी बातें | Despite all his blandishments, she refused to sign the contract.
appeal to authority | अधिकार पर भरोसा करना (अपील टू अथॉरिटी) | Quoting a famous professor is not proof; it is merely an appeal to authority.
rabble-rouser | भीड़ भड़काने वाला, दंगाई | The rabble-rouser shouted slogans until the crowd began pushing forward.
## वक्ता की शैली
grandiloquent | आडंबरपूर्ण, बड़ी-बड़ी बातें करने वाला | His grandiloquent toast lasted twenty minutes and said almost nothing.
bombastic | भड़काऊ, अतिशयोक्तिपूर्ण | Critics mocked the bombastic language of the new mayor's speech.
orotund | गंभीर और गूंजती हुई आवाज़ | The actor's orotund voice filled every corner of the old theatre.
mellifluous | सुरीला, मधुर (आवाज़) | The narrator's mellifluous voice made the audiobook a pleasure.
pithy | सारगर्भित, संक्षिप्त और अर्थपूर्ण | Emre gave a pithy summary that everyone could remember.
laconic | मितभाषी, कम बोलने वाला | The laconic farmer answered every question with a single word.
prolix | अत्यधिक लंबा, ज़रूरत से ज़्यादा विस्तृत | The prolix report could have been half as long without losing anything.
fustian | ढकोसला, दिखावटी और खोखली भाषा | Beneath the fustian of his speech there was hardly a single idea.
silver-tongued | वाक्पटु, बातों में माहिर | A silver-tongued lawyer can make a weak case sound almost reasonable.
stentorian | अत्यंत तेज़ और गूंजती हुई आवाज़ | The guide's stentorian voice reached us at the back of the crowd.
## प्रस्तुति भाषा
segue | सहज बदलाव, अगला विषय | That joke made a perfect segue into the next part of her talk.
signpost | रूपरेखा स्पष्ट करना (संकेत देना) | Good speakers signpost each section so the audience never feels lost.
elevator pitch | संक्षिप्त परिचय भाषण (एलिवेटर पिच) | Lukas practised a thirty-second elevator pitch for the startup fair.
cue card | बोलने का संकेत कार्ड | She glanced at a cue card only once during the entire speech.
teleprompter | प्रोम्प्टर स्क्रीन (टेलीप्रॉम्प्टर) | When the teleprompter failed, the host had to speak from memory.
keynote | मुख्य भाषण, उद्घाटन भाषण | Maria was invited to give the keynote at the engineering conference.
Q and A | प्रश्नोत्तर सत्र | We will leave fifteen minutes at the end for a Q and A.
ad-lib | तात्कालिक बोलना, बिना तैयारी बोलना | When the slides froze, Anna had to ad-lib for several minutes.
off the cuff | बिना तैयारी के, तुरंत | His best remarks were made off the cuff, not from the script.
icebreaker | बर्फ़ पिघलाना (आरंभिक अनौपचारिक बातचीत) | She opened the workshop with a short icebreaker about favourite books.
## वक्तृत्व क्रियाएँ
explicate | व्याख्या करना, विस्तार से समझाना | In her essay, Anna explicates a difficult poem line by line.
hold forth | लंबा भाषण देना | Uncle Omar held forth on politics while the soup went cold.
expatiate | विस्तार से चर्चा करना | The guide expatiated on the history of every single stone in the castle.
harangue | लंबा भाषण देना, डाँटना | The captain harangued the crew for an hour about discipline.
declaim | ओजस्वी स्वर में पढ़ना | The actor declaimed the poem from the top of the stairs.
extol | प्रशंसा करना, गुणगान करना | The review extolled the chef's inventive use of local herbs.
inveigh | कड़ी आलोचना करना | The columnist inveighed against the destruction of the old harbour.
expostulate | समझाना, विरोध जताना | Her friends expostulated with her about quitting her job so suddenly.
equivocate | गोलमोल बातें करना, टालमटोल करना | When asked about the budget, the director began to equivocate.
obfuscate | उलझन में डालना, अस्पष्ट करना | The jargon in the letter seemed designed to obfuscate the real problem.
## भाषण के प्रकार और रूप
eulogy | श्रद्धांजलि, प्रशस्ति पाठ | Her granddaughter gave a warm and funny eulogy at the service.
panegyric | प्रशंसात्मक भाषण | The retirement dinner turned into a long panegyric to the old headmaster.
diatribe | तीखी आलोचना, भर्त्सना भाषण | His diatribe against modern music went on for most of the evening.
philippic | तीव्र निंदात्मक भाषण | The senator's philippic against corruption was quoted for years.
homily | उपदेश, नैतिक सीख | Grandpa delivered a homily about saving money every time we visited.
valediction | विदाई भाषण, विदाई संदेश | The class president's valediction was short, honest and moving.
soliloquy | स्वगत कथन, आत्मभाषण | In her final soliloquy, the heroine reveals her secret to the audience.
jeremiad | विलाप, लंबी शिकायत | The article was a gloomy jeremiad about the decline of handwriting.
stump speech | चुनावी भाषण | The candidate repeated the same stump speech in every small town.
call to action | कार्रवाई का आह्वान | A strong presentation should always end with a clear call to action.
