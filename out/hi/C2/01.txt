# UNIT: पुराने और साहित्यिक शब्द | कविता और शास्त्रीय साहित्य की पुरानी भाषा
## शरीर और रूप-रंग (पुरानी भाषा)
visage | चेहरा, मुखमंडल (साहित्यिक) | Her pale visage appeared at the window like a ghost in the moonlight.
countenance | चेहरे का भाव, मुखमुद्रा | The old king's countenance darkened when he heard the news.
brow | माथा, भृकुटी (साहित्यिक) | A deep frown furrowed the scholar's brow as he read the letter.
bosom | छाती, वक्षस्थल (साहित्यिक) | She held the child close to her bosom through the cold night.
tresses | लंबे बाल, लटें (काव्यत्मक) | Her golden tresses tumbled over her shoulders in the morning wind.
sinew | नस, मांसपेशियों की ताकत (साहित्यिक) | Every sinew in his body strained as he pulled the boat ashore.
orb | आँख; गोला (काव्यत्मक) | Her bright blue orbs were filled with tears of joy.
mien | तौर-तरीक़ा, मुद्रा, शान | The stranger had the proud mien of a soldier who had seen many wars.
raiment | पोशाक, वस्त्र (पुराना) | The priestess appeared in white raiment embroidered with silver thread.
apparel | पहनावा, परिधान (औपचारिक, पुराना) | The nobles arrived at the feast in their finest apparel.
## प्रकृति (काव्यत्मक)
vale | घाटी (काव्यत्मक) | A gentle mist lay over the quiet vale at dawn.
dale | छोटी घाटी, द्रोणी (काव्यत्मक) | The shepherd led his flock over hill and dale all summer.
brook | छोटी नदी, झरना | We sat beside a babbling brook and listened to the birds.
mere | झील, जलाशय (काव्यत्मक) | Swans glided silently across the still surface of the mere.
firmament | आकाश, गगन | Countless stars glittered across the dark firmament above the sea.
zephyr | हल्की बयार, मंद पवन | A warm zephyr carried the scent of roses into the garden.
gloaming | गोधूलि, सांझ (काव्यत्मक) | They walked home slowly in the soft gloaming of a summer evening.
bower | कुंज, लतामण्डप | The lovers met secretly in a shady bower of roses.
sward | घास का मैदान, चरागाह (काव्यत्मक) | Children danced barefoot on the green sward by the river.
brine | समुद्र, खारा पानी | The old sailor had spent forty years upon the brine.
## लोग (पुराना)
maiden | कुँवारी कन्या, युवती (पुराना) | A young maiden stood waiting by the castle gate.
damsel | राजकुमारी, कुलीन बाला (पुराना) | In the old tales, a brave knight always rescues the damsel.
knave | धूर्त, दुष्ट व्यक्ति (पुराना) | That lying knave has stolen the bishop's silver cup!
swain | गड़रिया; प्रेमी युवक (काव्यत्मक) | A lovesick swain sang beneath her window every night.
liege | सामंती प्रभु, स्वामी | I swear to serve you faithfully, my liege, until my death.
kinsman | रिश्तेदार, स्वजन (पुरुष, पुराना) | He was welcomed at the castle as a kinsman of the lord.
churl | असभ्य व्यक्ति, गँवार (पुराना) | Only a churl would refuse bread to a hungry traveller.
crone | बुढ़िया, डायरनुमा वृद्ध महिला (कथा) | An old crone by the fire told the children strange stories.
sire | महाराज, स्वामी (संबोधन) | The enemy army is at the gates, sire. What shall we do?
minstrel | चारण, घुमक्कड़ गायक | A wandering minstrel entertained the guests with songs of old battles.
## पुराने सर्वनाम और क्रिया रूप
thou | तू (पुराना, कर्ता) | Thou shalt not pass this bridge without paying the toll.
thee | तुझे, तुझको (पुराना) | I have loved thee since the first day we met.
thy | तेरा, तुम्हारी (पुराना) | Speak softly, for thy words may wake the sleeping child.
thine | तेरा; तेरी (स्वर से पहले, पुराना) | This land was once thine, but now it belongs to another.
ye | तुम, आप लोग (पुराना) | Hear me, ye people of the village, for I bring news.
thou art | तू है (पुराना) | Thou art the fairest flower in all the kingdom.
hath | रखता है, चुका है (has, पुराना) | The storm hath destroyed the harvest of the whole valley.
doth | करता है (does, पुराना) | The lady doth protest too much, methinks.
shalt | -ेगा, -ोगी (shall, पुराना) | Thou shalt honour thy father and thy mother all thy days.
methinks | मुझे ऐसा लगता है (पुराना) | Methinks the stranger knows more than he is telling us.
## पुराने क्रिया-विशेषण
hither | इधर, यहाँ (पुराना) | Come hither, child, and let me look at your face.
thither | उधर, वहाँ (पुराना) | The pilgrims travelled thither on foot from distant lands.
whither | किधर, कहाँ (पुराना) | Whither are you going so late at night, good sir?
thence | वहाँ से, तदुपरांत (पुराना) | They sailed to Lisbon and thence across the ocean to the New World.
yonder | वह सामने का, उधर का | Do you see that light shining from yonder window?
anon | शीघ्र ही, थोड़ी देर में (पुराना) | I must leave you now, but I shall return anon.
ere | से पहले (पुराना) | We must reach the castle ere the sun goes down.
oft | अक्सर, बार-बार (काव्यत्मक) | The old songs are oft forgotten by the young.
betwixt | के बीच (पुराना) | The little cottage stood betwixt the forest and the sea.
wherefore | किस लिए, क्यों (पुराना) | Wherefore do you weep, my friend, when the battle is won?
## पुरानी क्रियाएँ
behold (beheld, beheld) | देखना, निहारना | The travellers stopped to behold the sunrise over the mountains.
beseech (besought, besought) | विनती करना, गिड़गिड़ाना | I beseech you, my lord, to spare my son's life.
smite (smote, smitten) | जोरदार प्रहार करना, मारना (पुराना) | The hero smote the giant with a single blow of his sword.
slay (slew, slain) | मार डालना, वध करना (साहित्यिक) | According to legend, the young prince slew the dragon alone.
hearken | कान लगाकर सुनना, ध्यान देना (पुराना) | Hearken to the words of the wise, and you will prosper.
tarry | देर लगाना, ठहरना (पुराना) | Do not tarry in the forest after dark, for wolves are near.
wend | आगे बढ़ना, चलना (पुराना) | The weary soldiers wended their way home after the long war.
quoth | कहा (पुराना) | "I shall not yield," quoth the knight, raising his shield.
forsake (forsook, forsaken) | त्याग देना, अकेला छोड़ देना | She swore she would never forsake her homeland.
cleave (clove, cloven) | चीरना, दो टुकड़े करना | With one stroke of his axe he clove the log in two.
## उच्च शैली की क्रियाएँ
beget (begot, begotten) | उत्पन्न करना, जन्म देना | Violence, as the old saying goes, only begets more violence.
espy | दूर से देखना, ताक लेना | From the tower the guard espied a rider approaching the town.
bewail | विलाप करना, शोक मनाना | The villagers bewailed the loss of their fishing boats in the storm.
rue | पछताना, भुगतना | You will rue the day you insulted the queen's champion.
vanquish | हराना, परास्त करना | The small army managed to vanquish a far greater enemy.
succour | सहायता करना, संकट में बचाना | The monks succoured the sick and the poor throughout the winter.
beguile | मोह लेना; धोखा देना | The sailors were beguiled by the strange song coming from the rocks.
betroth | सगाई करना (पुराना) | The princess was betrothed to a prince from a distant kingdom.
sunder | अलग करना, तोड़ डालना (साहित्यिक) | No power on earth shall sunder these two loving hearts.
rend (rent, rent) | फाड़ना, चीरना (साहित्यिक) | In her grief she rent her garments and wept aloud.
## पुराना समाज
alms | भिक्षा, खैरात | Every Friday the merchant gave alms to the beggars at the gate.
tithe | दशमांश कर, धार्मिक कर | Peasants had to pay a tithe of their harvest to the church.
fealty | निष्ठा की शपथ (सामंती) | The knights swore fealty to the new king on their knees.
serf | कृषक दास, भू-दास | A medieval serf could not leave the land without his lord's permission.
squire | सामंत का सेवक, हथियारबंद सिपाही | The young squire carried his master's shield into battle.
steed | घोड़ा, अश्व (साहित्यिक) | The knight mounted his white steed and rode towards the castle.
troth | वचन, प्रतिज्ञा (पुराना) | At the altar, the young couple plighted their troth.
coffer | संदूक, खजाना | The king's coffers were empty after years of war.
bard | भाट, चारण, चरण कवि | The bard sang of heroes and lost kingdoms by the fire.
scabbard | म्यान, तलवार का खोल | He slowly drew his sword from its leather scabbard.
## काव्यत्मक संज्ञाएँ और विशेषण
woe | दुःख, विपत्ति, शोक | The poem tells a tale of love, loss and endless woe.
mirth | उल्लास, हँसी-ख़ुशी | The hall was filled with music, laughter and mirth.
bane | अभिशाप, जी का जंजाल | That noisy rooster is the bane of my mornings.
guile | छल, कपट, धूर्तता | The fox won not by strength but by guile.
wondrous | अद्भुत, आश्चर्यजनक (साहित्यिक) | The travellers told wondrous tales of distant islands.
doughty | वीर, पराक्रमी (पुराना) | The doughty old captain refused to abandon his ship.
hoary | सफेद बालों वाला; अतिप्राचीन | A hoary old man sat alone at the end of the bridge.
wan | पीला पड़ा हुआ, म्लान | After weeks of illness, her face looked thin and wan.
comely | सुंदर, आकर्षक (पुराना) | The miller had a comely daughter whom everyone admired.
blithe | प्रसन्नचित्त, लापरवाह | She went on her way with a blithe and carefree heart.
## पुराने विस्मयादिबोधक और स्वीकृति शब्द
alas | हाय, अरेरे (दुःख व्यक्त करने के लिए) | Alas, the ship sank before it could reach the harbour.
prithee | कृपया, अनुरोध है (पुराना) | Prithee, tell me the name of this fair village.
forsooth | सचमुच, वास्तव में (पुराना, व्यंग्यात्मक) | A great hero, forsooth, who runs at the sight of a mouse!
verily | सत्य ही, वास्तव में (पुराना) | Verily, I tell you, this man speaks the truth.
lo | देखो, अरे (पुराना) | And lo, a bright star appeared in the eastern sky.
fie | धिक्कार है, थू (पुराना) | Fie upon you for breaking your promise to the poor widow!
hark | सुनो, ध्यान दो (पुराना) | Hark, I hear the sound of horses on the road!
nay | नहीं; बल्कि (पुराना) | It was a difficult, nay, an impossible journey through the mountains.
yea | हाँ (पुराना) | Those who agree shall say yea, and those who disagree shall say nay.
perchance | शायद, संभवतः (पुराना) | To sleep, perchance to dream, was all the weary traveller wished.
