## Аэропорт жана чек ара

arrivals hall | келип түшүү залы | Maria's brother was waiting for her in the arrivals hall with flowers.
duty-free | бажы төлөмсүз | Sofia bought some perfume at the duty-free shop before her flight.
immigration | паспорттук көзөмөл (өлкөгө кирүү) | The queue at immigration was long because three flights landed together.

excess baggage | ашыкча жүк | Diego paid sixty euros for excess baggage on the way home.

## Маданий айырмачылыктар

etiquette | этикет, жүрүм-турум эрежелери | Table etiquette can be very different from one country to another.
culture shock | маданий шок | Emre experienced culture shock during his first month in Canada.
stereotype | стереотип, калыптанып калган түшүнүк | Not every British person drinks tea all day; that is just a stereotype.
diversity | ар түрдүүлүк | London is famous for its cultural diversity and many languages.
taboo | табу, тыюу салынган нерсе | In some cultures, talking about salary is still a taboo.
offend | таарыдыруу, көңүлүн оорутуу | I didn't want to offend our hosts, so I tried every dish.
appropriate | ылайыктуу, туура | Shorts are not appropriate clothing when you visit a temple.
gesture | жест, кол кыймылы | A thumbs-up is a friendly gesture in some countries but rude in others.
adapt | көнүү, ыңгайлашуу | Children usually adapt to a new culture faster than adults.
