# UNIT: Сүйлөшүү тили | Бөлүкчөлөр, илептер, күнүмдүк жаргон
## Толтуруучу сөздөр
you know | билесиң го, мисалы | It was one of those days when, you know, nothing really works.
I mean | яғни, айтайын дегеним | The film was fine, I mean, it just wasn't what I expected.
kind of | бир аз, сыяктуу | I'm kind of tired after the trip, so let's stay in tonight.
sort of | бир түрү, дээрлик | Emre sort of agreed, but he didn't sound very convinced.
basically | негизинен, кыскасы | Basically, we need more time and a bit more money to finish.
literally | сөзмө-сөз, тагыраак айтканда | I was literally the only person in the whole cinema last night.
or something | же ушуга окшогон нерсе | Let's grab a coffee or something before the meeting starts.
and stuff | жана ушул сыяктуулар | We talked about work and stuff, nothing too serious really.
if you like | кааласаң, туура келсе | The village was, if you like, a little world of its own.
you see | байкасаң, көргөнүңдөй | You see, I never learned to drive, so I always take the bus.
## Илептер

gosh | турсунчу, ой кудай | Gosh, I didn't realise it was already so late in the evening.
blimey | кайдагыны айтпа, урумдук (Брит.) | Blimey, have you seen how much they charge for parking here?
yikes | уку, тобо | Yikes, I just noticed I sent that email to the wrong person.
whoops | ой, кечиресиз | Whoops, I think I've just spilled coffee all over your notes.
phew | шүгүр, кутулдук | Phew, we caught the last train just before the doors closed.
ugh | уф, түйшүк | Ugh, it's raining again and I forgot my umbrella at home.
eww | иий, жийиркеничтүү | Eww, there's something sticky all over the handle of this door.
aha | аха, түшүндүм | Aha, so that's why the printer kept jamming all morning!
huh | эми, ошондойбу | Huh, I never thought Omar would actually move abroad one day.
## Жооп кайтаруу сөздөрү

you bet | албетте, шексиз | Are you coming to the party on Saturday? You bet I am!
fair enough | макул, түшүнүктүү | You need more time to decide? Fair enough, let's talk next week.
tell me about it | мен деле айтып жатам, өзүм билем | This traffic is unbearable today. Tell me about it, I've been stuck for an hour.
you don't say | ошондой де, ооба ya | The shop is closed on Sunday? You don't say, what a surprise.
big deal | чоң иш, мааниси жок | So he has a new car, big deal, it doesn't make him a better person.
so what | эмне болуптур, анын эмнеси бар | So what if we're a little late, the party goes on until midnight.

no kidding | тамаша эмес, чын эле | No kidding, Yuki really ran the whole marathon without any training.
go figure | түшүнүп көр, табышмак | He hates travelling but became a pilot, go figure.

dude | жон, доске (АМР.) | Dude, you have to try the burgers at that new place downtown.
folks | элдер, калк | Okay folks, the bus leaves in ten minutes, so please don't be late.
cheers | рахмат, саламатта бол | Cheers for holding the door, that bag was heavier than I thought.
ta | рахмат (Брит. бейрасмий) | Ta for the tea, love, it's exactly what I needed after work.
howdy | салам (АМР. айылдык) | Howdy, neighbour, are you coming to the barbecue this weekend?

catch you later | кийин көрүшөбүз | I've got to run to my lecture now, catch you later at lunch.

wicked | сонун, укмуш (жарг.) | That concert last night was wicked, the crowd was amazing.

## Сүйлөшүү кыскартмалары
gonna | -макчы, бара жаткан (going to) | I'm gonna call my mum after work and tell her the news.
wanna | кылгысы келүү (want to) | Do you wanna come with us to the beach this afternoon?
gotta | керек (got to) | Sorry, I've gotta go, my train leaves in fifteen minutes.
dunno | билбейм (don't know) | I dunno where Omar put the keys, ask him when he gets back.
lemme | уруксат эт (let me) | Lemme check my calendar before I promise anything for next week.
gimme | бер мага (give me) | Gimme a minute, I'm just finishing this email to the client.
y'all | баарыңыз (Амр. түштүк) | Are y'all coming to the family dinner on Sunday evening?
ain't | эмес (жарг., isn't/am not) | That ain't the way we do things around here, my friend.
innit | ошондой эмеспи (Брит. жарг.) | It's freezing outside today, innit? Better take a scarf.
cos | анткени (because) | I stayed home cos I wasn't feeling great this morning.
