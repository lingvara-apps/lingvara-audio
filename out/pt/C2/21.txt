# UNIT: Movimento, gestos e sons | Verbos de marcha, queda, mímica e som na narrativa literária
## Marcha I: relaxada e vadia
mosey | andar sem rumo, caminhar vagarosamente | After lunch we moseyed down to the harbour to watch the fishing boats.
perambulate | caminhar, passear (formal) | Every Sunday the old couple perambulated slowly around the village green.
traipse | bater de porta em porta, andar exausto | We traipsed from shop to shop all afternoon looking for the right shoes.
pad | andar com passos silenciosos | The cat padded silently across the kitchen floor in the dark.
toddle | andar cambaleante (bebê) | Her little son toddled towards the ducks with a piece of bread in his hand.
tread (trod, trodden) | pisar, caminhar | Tread carefully on the old wooden stairs, as some of them are loose.
promenade | passear, desfilar | In summer, families promenade along the seafront after sunset.
potter | entreter-se com tarefas leves | Grandpa spends his mornings pottering around the garden shed.
lollygag | vadiar, perder tempo | If you lollygag on the beach much longer, we'll miss the last boat back.
rove | vagar, percorrer | As students, Lukas and Emre roved across the islands with only a tent.
## Marcha II: pesada, manca e pomposa
hobble | mancar, coxear | After twisting her ankle, Sofia hobbled back to the car.
waddle | andar gingando, andar como pato | A family of ducks waddled across the road while the traffic waited.
mince | andar com passos curtos e afetados | She minced along the icy pavement in her new shoes, afraid of slipping.
sashay | desfilar rebolando | She sashayed down the corridor as if it were a fashion runway.
strut | andar empinado, pavonear-se | The young champion strutted around the gym, showing off his new medal.
flounce | sair pisando duro, retirar-se encolerizado | Annoyed by the joke, Camille flounced out of the room without a word.
stomp | bater o pé, caminhar pesadamente | The child stomped upstairs after being told to turn off the game.
galumph | caminhar desajeitado e barulhento | The puppy galumphed through the puddles and covered us all in mud.
trudge | caminhar com esforço, arrastar-se | After the long hike, we trudged back to the village through the wet snow.
prance | trotar, marchar com garbo | The horses pranced proudly around the ring before the show began.
## Movimento furtivo e ágil
prowl | rondar, espreitar | A fox prowled around the henhouse long after midnight.
skulk | esgueirar-se, andar à espreita | Two suspicious men were skulking in the shadows behind the warehouse.
slink (slunk, slunk) | escafafundir-se, sumir de fininho | The dog slunk away with its tail between its legs after the scolding.
sidle | aproximar-se de lado, achegar-se | Omar sidled up to the host and asked quietly whether there was more cake.
stalk | perseguir furtivamente; caminhar altivo | The leopard stalked its prey through the tall grass without a sound.
careen | desgovernar-se, disparar descontrolado | The runaway cart careened down the hill and crashed into a hedge.
creep (crept, crept) | rastejar, andar furtivamente | We crept towards the kitchen at midnight, hoping to find some cake.
flit | voar levemente, revoar | Butterflies flitted from flower to flower in the warm afternoon.
clamber | trepar, escalar desajeitadamente | The children clambered over the rocks to reach the hidden beach.
skitter | deslizar rapidamente, corelar | A lizard skittered across the hot stones and vanished into a crack.
## Trpeço e queda
stumble | tropeçar | Yuki stumbled on a loose stone but managed not to fall.
lurch | dar um tranco, guinar | The bus lurched forward and several passengers grabbed the rails.
totter | cambalear, andar vacilante | The toddler tottered towards his mother on unsteady legs.
teeter | equilibrar-se na corda bamba, oscilar | A pile of books teetered on the edge of the desk before finally falling.
reel | atordoar-se, cambalear para trás | He reeled backwards when the heavy door swung open into his face.
tumble | tombar, rolar ladeira abaixo | The boy tumbled down the grassy slope, laughing all the way.
topple | desabar, tombar | A strong gust of wind toppled the ladder onto the flower bed.
sprawl | estatelar-se, esparramar-se | Diego slipped on the wet tiles and went sprawling across the floor.
plummet | despencar, precipitar-se | The kite plummeted into the sea when the string suddenly snapped.
keel over | desmaiar, capotar (expressão) | In the heat of the parade, one of the guards keeled over.
## Contorção e tremor
flail | debater-se, agitar os braços | The swimmer flailed in the waves until the lifeguard reached him.
writhe | contorcer-se de dor | The injured player writhed on the grass, clutching his knee.
squirm | torcer-se de desconforto ou vergonha | Emre squirmed in his seat when his old school photos appeared on the screen.
wriggle | contorcer-se para escapar | The child wriggled out of her coat and ran off to play.
twitch | contrair-se, ter espasmos | His left eye twitched whenever he was nervous about an exam.
quiver | tremular, vibrar levemente | Her lower lip quivered as she tried not to cry.
shudder | estremeçer, arrepiar-se | Anna shuddered at the memory of the cold, dark cellar.
convulse | convulsinar-se, sacudir-se | The whole audience convulsed with laughter at the final scene.
jolt | sobressaltar-se, dar um solavanco | Maria jolted awake when the train braked suddenly.
wobble | balançar, bambolear | The table wobbled every time someone leaned on it.
## Expressões faciais
blanch | empalidecer, desbotar | Lukas blanched when he saw the size of the restaurant bill.
flush | corar, ruborizar-se | Sofia flushed with pleasure when the teacher praised her essay.
crinkle | enrugar-se (ao redor dos olhos) | His eyes crinkled at the corners whenever he laughed.
furrow | franzir (a testa) | Omar furrowed his brow as he studied the confusing map.
pucker | fazer bico, franzir os lábios | The baby puckered her lips at the taste of the lemon.
raise an eyebrow | erguer a sobrancelha (expressão) | Camille raised an eyebrow when I ordered a third dessert.
curl one's lip | torcer o lábio com desdém (expressão) | The waiter curled his lip when we asked for ketchup with the steak.
pull a face | fazer careta (expressão) | Ali pulled a face when he heard we were having spinach again.
poker face | cara de paisagem, expressão inexpressiva | Yuki kept a perfect poker face while everyone guessed her secret.
gawp | ficar boquiaberto, encarar pasmo | Tourists stood gawping at the huge waves crashing against the cliffs.
## Gestos e postura
shrug | dar de ombros | When I asked where the keys were, Omar just shrugged.
beckon | chamar com a mão, acenar | The old woman beckoned us closer with a bony finger.
gesticulate | gesticular | The tour guide gesticulated wildly as she described the battle.
brandish | brandir, ameaçar com arma | The angry cook brandished a wooden spoon at the cat on the counter.
lunge | dar um bote, investir | The goalkeeper lunged to his left and just managed to save the shot.
crouch | agachar-se, acocorar-se | The photographer crouched behind a bush, waiting for the deer to appear.
hunch | corcovear, encolher os ombros | Ali sat hunched over his laptop until the early hours.
slouch | curvar-se, relaxar a postura | Stop slouching and sit up straight at the table, please.
stoop | inclinar-se, curvar o corpo | She stooped to pick up a coin that had rolled under the bench.
loll | esparramar-se com preguiça | The dog lolled in the shade with its tongue hanging out.
## Verbos de som: fala
rasp | falar com voz áspera | "Get out of my garden," the old man rasped from behind the fence.
croak | falar com voz rouca | With a terrible cold, Camille could only croak a few words on the phone.
intone | entoar monologicamente | The priest intoned the ancient prayer in a deep, steady voice.
drawl | arrastar as palavras ao falar | The old fisherman drawled his stories so slowly that the children grew impatient.
gibber | balbuciar incoerentemente | Terrified by the storm, the young sailor could only gibber in the corner.
blurt | deixar escapar, falar sem pensar | Emre blurted out the surprise before anyone could stop him.
bellow | berrar, bramar | The captain bellowed orders across the deck as the wind grew stronger.
whine | choramingar, resmungar | The children whined all the way home because they were hungry.
warble | trinar, cantarolar | Grandma warbled an old love song while she washed the dishes.
sputter | falar gaguejando de raiva | Speechless with rage, he could only sputter a few broken words.
## Verbos de som: respiração e corpo
wheeze | chiar ao respirar | After climbing six floors, Lukas stood at the top wheezing heavily.
gasp | ficar sem fôlego, ofegar | The crowd gasped as the acrobat slipped on the high wire.
pant | arfar, estar com falta de ar | The dog lay panting in the shade after chasing the ball.
snort | fungar, rir pelo nariz | Anna snorted with laughter at the ridiculous idea.
grunt | grunhir, resmungar | My teenage brother only grunted when I asked about his day.
groan | gemer, lamentar-se | The whole class groaned when the teacher announced another test.
snuffle | fungar aspirando o nariz | The hedgehog snuffled through the fallen leaves looking for insects.
hiccup | soluçar | Maria hiccupped all through the meeting and couldn't stop laughing.
belch | arrotar | The baby belched loudly after drinking her milk.
puff | resfolegar, ofegar subindo | Omar puffed up the hill on his old bicycle.
## Sons ao redor
creak | ranger | The floorboards creaked as we walked across the old attic.
rustle | sussurrar, rustir | Leaves rustled in the wind outside the open window.
thud | baquear, cair com baque | A heavy book thudded onto the floor in the silent library.
squelch | estalar na lama, chafurdar | Our shoes squelched in the mud all the way back to the farm.
clang | retinir, tinir metálico | The iron gate clanged shut behind the last visitor.
whir | zumbir rotativamente | The old projector whirred softly at the back of the classroom.
rumble | estrondar, trovejar | Thunder rumbled over the hills long before the rain arrived.
crackle | estalar | Logs crackled in the fireplace while the storm raged outside.
gurgle | gorgolejar | Water gurgled down the drain after the heavy shower.
sizzle | chiar na frigideira | Onions sizzled in the pan as Yuki prepared dinner.
