# UNIT: النقل والمرور | الطرق، القيادة، المركبات، النقل العام
## الطرق
motorway | طريق سريع | The motorway was closed after a lorry lost its load.
dual carriageway | طريق مزدوج | The speed limit on this dual carriageway is seventy miles per hour.
junction | تقاطع طرق | Turn left at the next junction and park near the bakery.
roundabout | دوار، ميدان مروري | Take the second exit at the roundabout.
lane | مسارب المرور | Stay in the left lane unless you are overtaking.
hard shoulder | كتف الطريق (للطوارئ) | Only stop on the hard shoulder in an emergency.
bypass | طريق الالتفاف، طريق فرعي | The new bypass takes heavy traffic away from the town centre.
slip road | طريق الفرعي (للدخول للخروج من الطريق السريع) | Speed up on the slip road before you join the motorway.
flyover | جسر علوي | A new flyover was built to reduce queues at the junction.
toll | رسوم المرور | We paid a small toll to cross the bridge.
## القيادة
overtake (overtook, overtaken) | تجاوز المركبات | Never overtake on a bend where you can't see ahead.
accelerate | يسرع، يزيد السرعة | The car accelerated quickly as soon as the light turned green.
brake | يضغط على الفرامل | I had to brake hard when a cat ran into the road.
reverse | يرجع للخلف | She reversed slowly out of the narrow garage.
indicate | يشير بالمؤشر (الغمّاز) | Always indicate before you change lanes.
swerve | ينحرف فجأة | The driver swerved to avoid a fox on the road.
skid | يتزحلق (للسيارة) | The van skidded on the icy road and hit a fence.
merge | يندمج في المرور | Two lanes merge into one just after the tunnel.
tailgate | يقترب جدًا من السيارة الأمامية | It's dangerous to tailgate, especially in wet weather.
give way | يعطي حق الأفضلية | You must give way to traffic coming from the right.
## أجزاء السيارة
windscreen | الزجاج الأمامي | A small stone cracked the windscreen on the motorway.
bonnet | غطاء المحرك | The mechanic lifted the bonnet and checked the engine.
boot | صندوق السيارة الخلفي | Put the suitcases in the boot, please.
steering wheel | عجلة القيادة، المقود | Keep both hands on the steering wheel while you drive.
dashboard | لوحة القيادة | Suddenly, a red warning light on the dashboard came on.
bumper | مصدّ السيارة، الصدام | Someone scratched the back bumper in the car park.
exhaust | عادم السيارة | Black smoke was coming out of the old bus's exhaust.
tyre | إطار السيارة، الكاوتشوك | Check your tyres before a long journey.
headlight | مصباح أمامي | One of my headlights isn't working.
gearbox | علبة التروس، ناقل الحركة | Repairing the gearbox will cost more than the car is worth.
## قواعد المرور
speed limit | السرعة القصوى المحددة | The speed limit near schools is twenty miles per hour.
learner driver | سائق متدرب | Learner drivers must be accompanied by an experienced driver.
speeding ticket | مخالفة سرعة | Emre got a speeding ticket on his way to the airport.
drink-driving | القيادة تحت تأثير الكحول | Drink-driving is one of the main causes of serious accidents.
traffic warden | شرطي المرور، مراقب المواقف | A traffic warden was checking cars in the high street.
number plate | لوحة السيارة | The camera recorded the car's number plate.
right of way | حق الأفضلية في المرور | Buses have right of way when leaving a bus stop here.
pedestrian crossing | معبر المشاة | Slow down when you see a pedestrian crossing ahead.
penalty points | نقاط الجزاء المرورية | If you get twelve penalty points, you lose your licence.
driving test | امتحان القيادة | Maria passed her driving test on the third attempt.
## مشاكل المرور
congestion | ازدحام مروري | Congestion in the city centre gets worse every year.
gridlock | شلل مروري تام | The storm caused total gridlock on the roads out of town.
tailback | طابور طويل من السيارات | There is a five-mile tailback after the accident.
roadworks | أعمال الطريق | Roadworks on the main road will continue until March.
diversion | تحويلة مرورية | Follow the yellow signs for the diversion.
bottleneck | عنق الزجاجة (اختناق مروري) | The old bridge is a bottleneck for traffic every morning.
collision | تصادم | Two cyclists were injured in a collision with a van.
pile-up | حادث اصطدام متسلسل | Fog led to a huge pile-up involving twenty cars.
standstill | توقف تام | Traffic came to a complete standstill near the stadium.
road rage | غضب الطريق (شجار مروري) | Road rage often starts with a small mistake by another driver.
## النقل العام
commuter | مسافر يومي للعمل | The early train is always full of tired commuters.
fare | أجرة السفر | Bus fares will rise by ten percent next year.
season ticket | تذاكر موسمية، اشتراك دوري | A season ticket is much cheaper if you travel every day.
ticket barrier | بوابة التذاكر (الترنستيل) | Insert your ticket at the ticket barrier to get onto the platform.
terminus | محطة أخيرة، نهاية الخط | This bus doesn't stop until it reaches the terminus.
interchange | محطة تبديل المواصلات | The station is a major interchange for buses and trains.
shuttle | حافلة مكوكية | A free shuttle runs between the hotel and the airport.
off-peak | وقت خارج ذروة الازدحام | Off-peak tickets are only valid after half past nine.
overcrowded | مكتظ، شديد الازدحام | The trains are overcrowded during the summer festival.
fare dodger | متهرب من دفع الأجرة | The inspector caught three fare dodgers on the evening train.
## أنواع المركبات

motorhome | منزل متنقل، كرفان | Lukas and Anna travelled around Scotland in a motorhome.
tow truck | شاحنة سحب السيارات | The tow truck took two hours to arrive.
hybrid | سيارة هجينة | Our new car is a hybrid, so it uses much less fuel.
minicab | تاكسي الطلب الخاص | It was late, so we ordered a minicab to get home.

unleaded | بنزين بدون رصاص | This car only takes unleaded petrol.

## النقل والمستقبل
