# UNIT: Filosofía y ética | Ramas de la filosofía, teorías morales, debates éticos
## Ramas de la filosofía
philosophy of mind | filosofía de la mente | Philosophy of mind asks how thoughts and feelings relate to the brain.
aesthetics | estética | Her lecture on aesthetics explored why we find certain landscapes beautiful.
applied ethics | ética aplicada | Applied ethics deals with real issues such as medicine, business and the environment.
philosophy of language | filosofía del lenguaje | Philosophy of language asks how words manage to refer to things in the world.
philosophy of science | filosofía de la ciencia | Philosophy of science examines what makes a theory truly scientific.
axiology | axiología | Axiology deals with the nature of value, both moral and aesthetic.
political philosophy | filosofía política | Political philosophy asks what makes a government legitimate in the first place.
metaethics | metaeética | Metaethics asks whether moral statements can be true or false at all.
ethicist | especialista en ética, ético | The hospital consulted an ethicist before making the difficult decision.
ontological | ontológico | The debate raises an ontological question about whether numbers really exist.
## Corrientes de pensamiento
humanism | humanismo | Humanism places human reason and dignity at the centre of moral thinking.
rationalism | racionalismo | Rationalism argues that reason, not experience, is the main source of knowledge.
positivism | positivismo | Positivism claimed that only statements tested by observation have real meaning.
stoicism | estoicismo | Emre turned to stoicism to cope with the stress of his new job.
pragmatism | pragmatismo | Pragmatism judges ideas by their practical results rather than by abstract truth.
nihilism | nihilismo | The novel's hero slides into nihilism after losing everything he believed in.
idealism | idealismo | In philosophy, idealism claims that reality is fundamentally mental.
materialism | materialismo | Materialism holds that everything, including thought, is made of matter.
determinism | determinismo | If determinism is true, every choice we make was fixed long before we were born.
scepticism | escepticismo | Philosophical scepticism questions whether we can know anything at all.
## Teorías morales
utilitarianism | utilitarismo | Utilitarianism judges an action by how much happiness it produces for everyone affected.
deontology | deontología, ética deontológica | According to deontology, some acts are wrong even if they lead to good results.
virtue ethics | ética de las virtudes | Virtue ethics asks what a person of good character would do in your place.
consequentialism | consecuencialismo | Consequentialism says that only the outcomes of an action matter morally.
categorical imperative | imperativo categórico | The categorical imperative tells us to act only in ways we could wish everyone to act.
social contract | contrato social | Social contract theory explains why free individuals agree to obey a government.
hedonism | hedonismo | Ancient hedonism was less about parties and more about avoiding pain.
ethical egoism | egoísmo ético | Ethical egoism claims that we ought to act in our own long-term interest.
natural law | ley natural, derecho natural | Natural law theorists believe moral rules can be discovered through reason alone.
greater good | bien común, bien mayor | She agreed to give up her land for the greater good of the village.
## Conceptos filosóficos básicos
a priori | a priori | Mathematical truths are often described as a priori knowledge.
a posteriori | a posteriori | That water boils at a hundred degrees is an a posteriori truth.
dualism | dualismo | Dualism treats the mind and the body as two completely different substances.
monism | monismo | Monism holds that mind and matter are ultimately made of one single substance.
transcendent | trascendente | Many traditions speak of a transcendent reality beyond the physical world.
causality | causalidad | Some physicists now question whether causality works the same way at tiny scales.
moral agent | agente moral | Only a moral agent, able to understand right and wrong, can be held responsible.
sentience | sintiencia, capacidad de sentir | The question of animal sentience is central to modern debates about farming.
personhood | condición de persona | Lawyers and philosophers disagree about when personhood begins.
intrinsic | intrínseco | She believes that nature has intrinsic value, not just value for humans.
## Métodos de razonamiento
thought experiment | experimento mental | The famous thought experiment asks whether you would pull a lever to save five lives.
reductio ad absurdum | reducción al absurdo | He used a reductio ad absurdum to show that the rule would forbid all eating.
sound argument | argumento válido, argumento sólido | A sound argument has true premises and a conclusion that follows from them.
principle of charity | principio de caridad | The principle of charity means interpreting your opponent's view in its strongest form.
first principles | primeros principios | Instead of copying others, she reasoned from first principles.
counterexample | contraejemplo | A single counterexample is enough to disprove a general rule.
Occam's razor | navaja de Ockham | By Occam's razor, the simplest explanation is usually the one to prefer.
proposition | proposición | Every proposition in logic must be either true or false.
necessary condition | condición necesaria | Oxygen is a necessary condition for fire, but it is not enough on its own.
sufficient condition | condición suficiente | Being a square is a sufficient condition for being a rectangle.
## Adjetivos de juicio moral
permissible | permisible, admisible | Is it ever morally permissible to lie in order to protect someone?
blameworthy | censurable, culpable | He was careless, but not blameworthy, since nobody had warned him.
praiseworthy | loable, elogiable | Her decision to return the lost wallet was truly praiseworthy.
reprehensible | reprehensible | Taking advantage of elderly customers is completely reprehensible.
unconscionable | inaceptable, desmesurado | It is unconscionable to raise the price of medicine during a crisis.
unscrupulous | sin escrúpulos, deshonesto | An unscrupulous landlord tried to keep the students' deposits.
amoral | amoral | The main character is not evil, just completely amoral.
high-minded | idealista, elevado | Behind all the high-minded speeches, the plan was really about money.
venal | venal, corrupto | The story describes a venal official who sells permits to the highest bidder.
commendable | encomiable | The team showed commendable patience with the difficult client.
## Verbos éticos

rationalise | racionalizar, buscar justificaciones | He tried to rationalise his rudeness by blaming the stress at work.
absolve | absolver | Apologising does not absolve you of responsibility for the damage.
transgress | transgredir, quebrantar | Artists sometimes transgress social norms to make people think.
recant | retractarse | Under heavy pressure, the scholar was forced to recant his theory in public.
disavow | desautorizar, rechazar | The company quickly disavowed the offensive comments made by one of its managers.
deplore | deplorar | We deplore any use of violence, whatever the cause.
censure | censurar, reprobar | The board voted to censure the director for misleading investors.

euthanasia | eutanasia | Euthanasia remains illegal in many countries but is debated more openly than before.

sanctity of life | santidad de la vida | Doctors are trained to respect the sanctity of life above all else.

## Filosofía de la justicia

retributive justice | justicia retributiva | Retributive justice holds that wrongdoers deserve punishment in proportion to their crime.
egalitarian | igualitario | The island's egalitarian traditions meant that land was shared equally among families.
common good | bien común | Good leaders put the common good before their own ambitions.
veil of ignorance | velo de la ignorancia | Behind a veil of ignorance, you would not know whether you would be born rich or poor.
natural rights | derechos naturales | The document claims that life and liberty are natural rights that no ruler can remove.

state of nature | estado de naturaleza | In the state of nature, before any government, life might have been short and harsh.
