# UNIT: Rhetoric and debate | Debate, persuasive language, media analysis
## Debate basics
motion | taklif, bahs mavzusi | Tonight's debate motion proposes banning cars from the city centre.
rebuttal | eʼtiroz, rad etish | Her rebuttal exposed several weaknesses in the other team's case.
proponent | tarafdor, himoyachi | Proponents of the plan say it will reduce traffic considerably.
moderator | moderator, boshlovchi | The moderator reminded both speakers to respect the time limit.
concede | tan olish, yon berish | Diego conceded that his opponent had made a fair point.
counterargument | qarshi dalil | A strong essay should address at least one counterargument.
premise | asos, dastlabki shart | The whole argument rests on a false premise.
stance | pozitsiya, qarash | What is your stance on working from home?
contend | daʼvo qilmoq, taʼkidlamoq | Critics contend that the new law will hurt small businesses.
refute | rad etmoq, yoʻqqa chiqarmoq | Camille refuted every claim with clear evidence and calm logic.
## Building an argument
assertion | daʼvo, tasdiq | His assertion was not supported by any reliable data.
substantiate | asoslamoq, dalillamoq | Can you substantiate that claim with real figures?
validity | asoslilik, haqiqiylik | Several experts questioned the validity of the survey results.
coherent | izchil, mantiqli | Sofia presented a coherent argument from start to finish.
compelling | ishontirarli, kuchli | The lawyer made a compelling case for her client's innocence.
flawed | nuqsonli, xato | The study was flawed because the sample was too small.
underpin | asos boʻlmoq, qoʻllab-quvvatlamoq | Solid research underpins every claim in her speech.
contention | bahsli fikr, daʼvo | It is my contention that schools should start later in the morning.
deduction | xulosa, mantiqiy chiqarma | His deduction seemed logical, but it ignored one important fact.
plausible | ishonchli, mantiqqa muvofiq | Her explanation sounds plausible, but we still need proof.
## Persuasive language
appeal | taʼsir qilmoq, jalb qilmoq | The advert appeals to parents who worry about their children's health.
emotive | taʼsirchan, hissiyotga boy | Politicians often use emotive language to win support.
sway | oʻzgartirmoq, koʻndirmoq | Nothing he said could sway the undecided voters.
rhetorical | ritorik, taʼsir uchun aytilgan | That was a rhetorical question, so you don't need to answer it.
hyperbole | mubolagʻa | Saying the queue was a mile long was pure hyperbole.
underscore | taʼkidlamoq, ostiga chizmoq | The speaker underscored the need for urgent action on pollution.
credibility | ishonchlilik, obroʻ | One factual error can damage a speaker's credibility.
win over | oʻz tomoniga ogʻdirmoq | Her honest answers won over even the sceptical audience.
manipulate | manipulyatsiya qilmoq, aldab boshqarmoq | Some advertisers manipulate our fears to sell their products.
catchphrase | shior, yodda qoluvchi ibora | The campaign's catchphrase was repeated on every billboard in town.
## Speech and presentation
lectern | minbar (nutq soʻzlash uchun) | Sofia placed her notes on the lectern and smiled at the audience.
rapport | oʻzaro tushunish, yaqinlik | A good speaker builds rapport with the audience in the first minute.
anecdote | latifa, qisqa voqea | He opened his talk with a funny anecdote about his first job.
podium | sahna, podyum | The mayor stepped up to the podium and thanked everyone.
digress | mavzudan chetga chiqmoq | Sorry, I digress; let's return to the main question.
concise | qisqa va loʻnda | Keep your answers concise because each speaker has two minutes.
recap | qisqacha takrorlamoq, xulosa qilmoq | Let me briefly recap the three main points of today's talk.
ramble | choʻzib yubormoq, mavzudan adashmoq | When he gets nervous, he tends to ramble for ages.
captivate | rom qilmoq, mahliyo etmoq | Her story captivated the audience from the very first minute.
heckle | gap otmoq, xalaqit bermoq | A man at the back began to heckle the speaker.
## Media analysis
slant | qiya yondashuv, tarafgirlik | The article had a clear slant against the new mayor.
coverage | yoritish, axborot berish | The coverage of the floods was detailed and fair.
sensationalism | shov-shuvbozlik | Readers are tired of sensationalism and want calm, accurate reporting.
misleading | chalgʻituvchi, aldovchi | The headline was misleading and caused unnecessary panic.
editorial | bosh maqola | Today's editorial criticises the government's housing policy.
spin | boʻyab koʻrsatish, talqin qilish | The minister's team put a positive spin on the bad figures.
scrutiny | diqqat bilan tekshirish, nazorat | The company's accounts are now under close scrutiny.
fact-check | faktlarni tekshirib chiqmoq | Journalists should fact-check every quote before publishing it.
objectivity | xolislik, obyektivlik | A good reporter tries to maintain objectivity at all times.
sound bite | qisqa parcha, iqtibos | The evening news showed only a short sound bite from the interview.
## Agreement and dissent
concur | qoʻshilmoq, fikri bir boʻlmoq | I concur with my colleague on this particular point.
dissent | eʼtiroz, norozilik | There was strong dissent among the members about the new rules.
misgiving | shubha, xavotir | Many residents expressed serious misgivings about the new parking plan.
contradict | ziddiyatli boʻlmoq, inkor etmoq | His latest statement contradicts what he said last week.
dispute | bahslashmoq, shubha ostiga qoʻymoq | Nobody seriously disputes that the climate is changing.
acknowledge | tan olmoq, eʼtirof etmoq | She acknowledged that the plan had some weaknesses.
side with | tarafiga oʻtmoq, qoʻllab-quvvatlamoq | In the end, most of the class sided with Omar on this issue.
dismiss | pisand qilmasman, rad etmoq | He dismissed the criticism as unfair and badly informed.
take issue with | norozilik bildirmoq, qarshi chiqmoq | I take issue with your claim that young people never read.
common ground | umumiy til, mushtarak nuqta | Despite their differences, the two sides found some common ground.
## Evidence and logic
anecdotal | shaxsiy tajribaga asoslangan | The evidence is only anecdotal and needs proper research.
generalization | umumlashtirish, xulosa chiqarish | Saying all teenagers are lazy is an unfair generalization.
assumption | taxmin, faraz | Your plan is based on the assumption that prices will fall.
conclusive | uzil-kesil, aniq | There is no conclusive proof that the new drug works.
irrelevant | aloqador boʻlmagan, mavzudan tashqari | Her age is irrelevant to the question we are discussing.
inconsistency | zidlik, nomutanosiblik | The reporter noticed an inconsistency in the witness's story.
circular | doiraviy, qismatli (mantiq) | His reasoning is circular because the conclusion simply repeats the premise.
verify | tasdiqlamoq, tekshirib koʻrmoq | We could not verify the information from any other source.
rationale | mantiqiy asos, sabab | Can you explain the rationale behind this decision?
correlation | oʻzaro bogʻliqlik | Correlation does not always mean that one thing causes another.
## Tone of debate
heated | qizgʻin, keskin | The meeting turned into a heated argument about money.
civil | odobli, madaniyatli | Let's keep this discussion civil and avoid personal comments, please.
confrontational | janjalli, ziddiyatli | His confrontational style made the negotiations even harder.
cut in | gapni boʻlmoq, suhbatga aralashmoq | Sorry to cut in, but I have a quick question.
backtrack | ortga qaytmoq, soʻzidan qaytmoq | The company was forced to backtrack after customers complained loudly.
bicker | tortishib qolmoq, yuz koʻrmas boʻlmoq | The two brothers bicker about everything, even the weather.
hostile | dushmanona, yovvoyi | The audience grew hostile when the speaker ignored their questions.
composure | vazminlik, oʻzini tuta bilish | Even under pressure, Emre never lost his composure.
talk over | gapni boʻlib gapirmoq | It is rude to talk over people who disagree with you.
patronising | kamsituvchi, tepadan qaraydigan | Please don't use that patronising tone when you explain it to me.
## Public and campaigns
petition | petitsiya, imzo toʻplash | Over ten thousand people signed the petition to save the park.
advocate | himoya qilmoq, yoqlab chiqmoq | Maria advocates more cycle lanes in the city.
lobby | lobbilik qilmoq, taʼsir oʻtkazmoq | Local farmers lobbied the council for lower water prices.
grassroots | quyi qatlamdagi, xalqona | The grassroots movement started with just a few neighbours.
rally | miting, ommaviy yigʻilish | Thousands of people attended the rally in the main square.
mobilize | safarbar qilmoq, harakatga keltirmoq | The group mobilized hundreds of volunteers through social media.
controversy | bahs-munozara, janjal | The new stadium caused controversy among local residents.
propaganda | tashviqot, propaganda | The posters were pure propaganda with no real facts.
outcry | norozilik, shovqin-suron | The plan to close the hospital caused a public outcry.
backlash | keskin norozilik, aks taʼsir | The decision to raise ticket prices led to a strong backlash from fans.
## Debate phrases
all things considered | har tomonlama oʻylab koʻrganda | All things considered, the debate went better than we expected.
by the same token | shu mantiqqa koʻra, shuningdek | Teachers should respect students; by the same token, students should respect teachers.
it stands to reason | oʻz-oʻzidan ravshan, mantiqan toʻgʻri | It stands to reason that tired drivers cause more accidents.
play devil's advocate | shaytonning advokati boʻlmoq | Let me play devil's advocate and argue for the opposite view.
beg the question | savol tugʻdirmoq, shubha uygʻotmoq | The high price begs the question of who can actually afford it.
moot point | bahsli masala, ahamiyatsiz holat | Whether we could have won is now a moot point.
in a nutshell | qilib aytganda, qisqacha | In a nutshell, the project failed because of poor planning.
the long and the short of it | gapning qisqasi | The long and the short of it is that we need more time.
for the sake of argument | bahs uchun, faraz qilaylik | For the sake of argument, let's say the price doubles next year.
conversely | aksincha, buning aksi oʻlaroq | Some people find the city exciting; conversely, others find it exhausting.
