# UNIT: Xarakter nuanslari | Nozik shaxsiyat xususiyatlari va xulq-atvor
## Ijobiy xususiyatlar
conscientious | vijdonli, ishiga masʼuliyatli | She is a conscientious worker who never misses a deadline.
resourceful | tadbirkor, topqir | Omar is resourceful and always finds a way to fix things.
compassionate | rahmdil, shafqatli | The nurse was compassionate towards every frightened patient on the ward.
magnanimous | oliyjanob | He was magnanimous in victory and praised his opponent warmly.
diligent | tirishqoq, mehnatsevar | Yuki is a diligent student who reviews her notes every evening.
level-headed | vazmin, sovuqqon | We need a level-headed person to handle this crisis calmly.
open-minded | keng fikrli, ochiq | My grandmother is surprisingly open-minded about new ideas and lifestyles.
tactful | xushmuomala, odobli | Try to be tactful when you tell him about the mistake.
unassuming | kamtarin, oddiy | Despite his success, Lukas remained a quiet and unassuming man.
dependable | ishonchli, suyansa boʻladigan | Anna is the most dependable colleague I have ever worked with.
## Salbiy xususiyatlar
conceited | oʻziga bino qoʻygan, maqtanchoq | He is so conceited that he never admits he is wrong.
obstinate | qaysar, oʻjar | The obstinate child refused to put on his coat in the rain.
spiteful | kek saqlovchi, oʻch olishni yaxshi koʻradigan | That was a spiteful comment, and I think you know it.
gullible | sodda, har narsaga inonaveradigan | Don't be so gullible; that email is obviously a scam.
self-centred | xudbin, manman | A self-centred person rarely asks how other people are feeling.
petty | mayda-chuyda narsalarga eʼtibor beradigan | Arguing about who paid for the coffee seems rather petty.
vain | kekkaygan, manman | He is too vain to wear his glasses in public.
moody | injiq, kayfiyati oʻzgaruvchan | Teenagers can be moody, especially early in the morning.
manipulative | manipulyativ, odamlardan foydalanadigan | She finally realised that her friend had been manipulative for years.
tight-fisted | xasis, qattiqqoʻl | My tight-fisted uncle never leaves a tip in restaurants.
## Ijtimoiy xulq-atvor
gregarious | kirishimli, omma-xaloyiqni yoqtiradigan | Diego is gregarious and loves big, noisy family dinners.
reserved | vazmin, tortinchoq | At first Emre seemed reserved, but he is actually very warm.
aloof | sovuqqon, uzoqni tutuvchi | The new manager seemed aloof and rarely chatted with the staff.
approachable | samimiy, muloqotga kirishish oson | Our professor is approachable, so students ask her lots of questions.
considerate | eʼtiborli, boshqalarni oʻylaydigan | It was very considerate of you to call before visiting us.
nosy | qiziquvchan, burnini tiqadigan | Our nosy neighbour always asks where we are going and why.
boisterous | shovqin-suronli, shoʻx | The boisterous fans sang loudly all the way home.
courteous | xushmuomala, muloyim | The hotel staff were courteous and helpful throughout our stay.
tactless | odobsiz, oʻylab gapirmaydigan | His tactless joke about her age upset everyone at the table.
introvert | ofitsial boʻlmagan tarzda oʻziga berilgan (introversiya) | As an introvert, Sofia recharges by spending time alone with a book.
## Ish uslubi
meticulous | sinchkov, oʻta eʼtiborli | Camille is meticulous about checking every figure in the report twice.
methodical | tartibli, tizimli | A methodical approach will save us a lot of time in the long run.
proactive | faol, tashabbuskor | Be proactive and solve problems before they get any worse.
sloppy | tartibsiz, chala-pula qilingan | The report was sloppy and full of spelling mistakes.
perfectionist | mukammallikka intiluvchi | As a perfectionist, Maria finds it hard to finish her projects.
persistent | qatʼiyatli, tinchimas | If you are persistent, you will eventually get an interview somewhere.
single-minded | maqsadiga sodiq, qatʼiy | She was single-minded in her pursuit of a career in medicine.
laid-back | xotirjam, begʻam | Our laid-back boss doesn't mind if we arrive a little late.
workaholic | ishqiboz, mehnatkash (ishga berilib ketgan) | Ali became a workaholic and completely forgot to take holidays.
complacent | xotirjamlikka berilgan, oʻziga bino qoʻygan | After two easy wins, the team became complacent and lost.
## Hissiy munosabatlar
thin-skinned | taʼsirchan, xafagina | Don't be so thin-skinned; it was only a friendly joke.
hot-headed | qizgʻonchiq, tez tutashib ketadigan | The hot-headed player was sent off after only ten minutes.
even-tempered | vazmin, sokin | My father is even-tempered and rarely raises his voice at anyone.
irritable | jahldor, jizzaki | I get irritable when I haven't had enough sleep.
resilient | chidamli, tez oʻzini oʻnglab oladigan | Children are often far more resilient than adults think.
temperamental | injiq, kayfiyati tez oʻzgaradigan | The temperamental chef shouted whenever a dish was late.
sentimental | taʼsirchan, dilbar | She is very sentimental and keeps every birthday card she receives.
excitable | tez hayajonlanadigan | Our excitable puppy jumps on every visitor at the door.
melancholic | mahzun, gʻamgin | The film had a melancholic ending that stayed with me for days.
composed | oʻzini tuta oladigan, sovuqqon | Even under pressure, the pilot remained calm and composed.
## Nutq uslubi
outspoken | ochiqkoʻngil, gapni yashirmaydigan | She is outspoken and always says exactly what she thinks.
blunt | loʻnda, tap tortmaydigan | His blunt feedback hurt a little, but it was really useful.
articulate | oʻz fikrini ravon yetkazadigan | Omar is an articulate speaker who explains complex ideas clearly.
chatty | sergap, gapdon | The chatty taxi driver told us his whole life story.
sarcastic | kinoyali, kesatuvchi | He made a sarcastic remark about my cooking in front of guests.
witty | ziyrak, oʻtkir tilli | Her witty speech made the whole room burst out laughing.
soft-spoken | muloyim ovozli | The soft-spoken teacher never needed to shout at her pupils.
evasive | boʻyin tovlovchi, chalgʻituvchi | When I asked about the money, he suddenly became evasive.
condescending | kamsitib gapiruvchi, tepadan qaraydigan | I hate it when people use a condescending tone with waiters.
eloquent | notiq, taʼsirli gapiradigan | The bride's father gave an eloquent and moving speech at the wedding.
## Munosabat va dunyoqarash
cynical | kinoyali, ishonchsiz | After many years in politics, he had become rather cynical.
naive | sodda, goʻl | It was naive of me to believe all his promises.
pragmatic | amaliy, real hayotga asoslangan | We need a pragmatic solution now, not a perfect one.
idealistic | gʻoyatda orzuga beriluvchi (idealist) | As a young teacher, Lukas was idealistic and full of energy.
narrow-minded | tor doirada oʻylaydigan, qoloq | It is narrow-minded to judge people by their accent.
indecisive | ikkilanuvchi, qatʼiyatsiz | I'm so indecisive that choosing a restaurant takes me ages.
judgmental | qoralovchi, tanqidiy baholovchi | Try not to be judgmental when your friends share their problems.
self-assured | oʻziga ishongan | She gave a calm and self-assured presentation to the board.
apathetic | befarq, loqayd | Many young people feel apathetic about local elections.
principled | prinsiplari bor, qatʼiy tamoyilga ega | A principled journalist refuses to publish rumours without checking them.
## Xulq-atvor feʼllari
boast | maqtanish, kekkayish | He likes to boast about his new car to everyone.
sulk | arazlash, labini boʻyash | Stop sulking and tell me what is wrong.
flatter | xomtama qilish, xushomad qilish | You are only flattering me because you want a favour.
nag | tinmay mingʻirlash, gʻoʻngʻillash | My mother keeps nagging me about tidying my room.
procrastinate | keyinga qoldirish, choʻzish | I always procrastinate when I have a long essay to write.
show off | maqtanmoq, koʻz-koʻq qilmoq | He only bought that expensive watch to show off.
meddle | aralashish, burnini suqish | Please don't meddle in our family affairs any more.
grumble | gʻoʻngʻirlash, noliysh | Diego grumbled about the weather all weekend long.
dither | ikkilanmoq, dovdirab qolmoq | Stop dithering and just choose a colour for the walls.
tease | qitiqlamoq, masxara qilmoq | Her brothers used to tease her about her curly hair.
## Odamlarni tavsiflovchi otlar
extrovert | ochiqkoʻngil, muloqotsevar kishi | Emre is a true extrovert who loves meeting new people.
know-it-all | hamma narsani bilaman deb maqtanguvchi | Nobody likes a know-it-all who corrects everyone in meetings.
busybody | birovlarning ishiga burnini suquvchi | The village busybody seemed to know everyone's secrets.
pushover | boʻshashgan, koʻndirishi oson odam | Don't be a pushover; say no if you're too busy.
daydreamer | xayolparast | The daydreamer in the back row never heard the teacher's question.
go-getter | tashabbuskor, tirishqoq | The company is looking for a young and energetic go-getter.
couch potato | dangasa, oʻrnidan turmaydigan odam | My brother is a couch potato who watches TV all weekend.
troublemaker | janjalkash, boshqalar boshiga balo boʻladigan odam | The teacher moved the troublemaker to the front of the class.
role model | namuna, oʻrnak boʻladigan shaxs | Her grandmother has always been her greatest role model.
loner | yolgʻizlikni yoqtiruvchi kishi | He was a loner at school but made many friends at university.
## Xarakter idiomalar
a heart of gold | oltin yurak, mehribon | Behind his rough voice, Ali really has a heart of gold.
a cold fish | sovuqqon, hissiyotsiz odam | Her new boyfriend seems a bit of a cold fish at first.
down-to-earth | sodda, real fikrlaydigan | Despite her success, Maria is very friendly and down-to-earth.
a wet blanket | kayfiyatni buzuvchi, bezovta qiluvchi | Don't be such a wet blanket; come dancing with us!
full of oneself | oʻzini osmonda deb biluvchi | Since his promotion, he has been completely full of himself.
thick-skinned | qalin teri, tanqidlarga beeʼtibor | You need to be thick-skinned to work as a stand-up comedian.
a people person | odamovi, jamoatchi | As a real people person, she is perfect for customer service.
keep oneself to oneself | oʻzi bilan oʻzi, birovga qoʻshilmaydigan | Our neighbour is quiet and mostly keeps himself to himself.
a social butterfly | har davrada topiladigan, xushmuomala | At parties Sofia is a real social butterfly who talks to everyone.
two-faced | ikki yuzlamachi | I can't trust him any more because he is so two-faced.
