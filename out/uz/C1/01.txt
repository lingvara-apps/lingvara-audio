# UNIT: Akademik nutq | Ilmiy yozuv, argument, akademik qoliplar
## Akademik jarayon
monograph | monografiya | She published a monograph on medieval trade routes across the North Sea.
dissertation | doktorlik dissertatsiyasi | Omar spent four years writing his dissertation on coastal erosion.
peer review | taqriz | The article finally passed peer review after two rounds of revisions.
viva | dissertatsiya himoyasi | Her viva is scheduled for the second week of June.
tenure | akademik lavozim | After ten years of teaching, he was finally granted tenure.
citation | iqtibos | Every citation must follow the style required by the journal.
bibliography | adabiyotlar roʻyxati | The bibliography runs to more than forty pages.
footnote | izoh, ostxat | The author explains the term in a short footnote on page twelve.
appendix (appendices) | ilova | The full questionnaire is included in the appendix at the end.
symposium (symposia) | simpozium | Yuki presented her early findings at an international symposium in Lisbon.
## Mantiq va argument
line of reasoning | fikrlash silsilasi | I find it hard to follow her line of reasoning in the second chapter.
inference | xulosa | The inference he draws from such limited data seems rather bold.
logical leap | mantiqiy sakrash | Moving from one survey to a universal law is a huge logical leap.
induction | induksiya | Most scientific laws are reached through induction from repeated observations.
syllogism | sillogizm | The lecturer opened the class with a classic syllogism about mortality.
fallacy | mantiqiy xato | Blaming the speaker instead of the idea is a common fallacy.
sweeping generalisation | haddan tashqari umumlashtirish | Saying that all students dislike statistics is a sweeping generalisation.
red herring | chalgʻituvchi mavzu | The question about funding was a red herring that distracted from the real issue.
corollary | tabiiy oqibat | Rising prices are a natural corollary of the shortage.
tautology | tautologiya | Saying that free gifts cost nothing is a tautology.
## Tadqiqot usuli
methodology | metodologiya | The reviewers questioned the methodology rather than the results.
hypothesis (hypotheses) | gipoteza | The data did not support our original hypothesis.
variable | oʻzgaruvchan miqdor | We controlled for age, income and education as separate variables.
sample size | tanlanma hajmi | With such a small sample size, the results cannot be generalised.
control group | nazorat guruhi | Participants in the control group received no treatment at all.
longitudinal | lonqitudinal, uzoq muddatli | A longitudinal study followed the same families for twenty years.
qualitative | sifatli | The project combines qualitative interviews with a large online survey.
quantitative | miqdoriy | Quantitative methods alone could not explain why people changed their habits.
replicate | takrorlamoq | Other labs have so far failed to replicate the experiment.
fieldwork | dala tadqiqoti | Diego did his fieldwork in small fishing villages along the coast.
## Maʼlumotlar va tahlil
dataset | maʼlumotlar bazasi | The researchers made their entire dataset publicly available.
variance | dispersiya, oʻzgaruvchanlik | The variance between the two groups was larger than the team had expected.
causation | sabab-oqibat bogʻliqligi | The authors are careful not to confuse correlation with causation.
outlier | chetga chiqqan qiymat | One outlier distorted the average, so we removed it from the analysis.
statistically significant | statistik jihatdan ahamiyatli | The difference between the two groups was not statistically significant.
margin of error | xatolik chegarasi | The poll has a margin of error of three percentage points.
standard deviation | standart ogʻish | A small standard deviation means that most results lie close to the average.
skew | buzib koʻrsatmoq | A few extremely rich households can skew the income figures.
extrapolate | ekstrapolyatsiya qilmoq | It is risky to extrapolate from one season's data to the next decade.
anomaly | anomaliya, ogʻish | At first, the team dismissed the strange reading as a minor anomaly.
## Daʼvo va dalil feʼllari
posit | ilgari surmoq | The theory posits that language shapes the way we perceive colour.
purport | daʼvo qilmoq | The study purports to show a link between sleep and memory.
bear out (bore, borne) | tasdiqlamoq | The latest findings bear out what Yuki suspected years ago.
corroborate | tasdiqlamoq, dalillamoq | Two independent witnesses corroborate her version of events.
invalidate | haqiqiy emas deb topmoq | A single error in the sampling could invalidate the entire experiment.
bolster | kuchaytirmoq, qoʻllab-quvvatlamoq | Diego added three new case studies to bolster his argument.
attest | tasdiqlamoq, guvohlik bermoq | Hundreds of surviving letters attest to their long friendship.
allege | daʼvo qilmoq (isbotsiz) | Critics allege that the figures were adjusted to look more favourable.
vindicate | oqlamoq, haqligini isbotlamoq | The latest findings seem to vindicate her controversial theory.
debunk | fosh qilmoq, asossizligini koʻrsatmoq | The documentary sets out to debunk several popular myths about diet.
## Tahlil va sharh feʼllari
scrutinise | diqqat bilan oʻrganmoq | The committee will scrutinise every page of the final report.
elucidate | oydinlashtirmoq | The second chapter elucidates the link between climate and migration.
delineate | chegarasini belgilamoq, tasvirlamoq | The introduction clearly delineates the scope of the study.
juxtapose | yonma-yon qoʻymoq | The author juxtaposes urban and rural attitudes towards family.
synthesise | sintez qilmoq | A good review should synthesise decades of research into a clear picture.
conceptualise | konseptuallashtirmoq | The author conceptualises memory as a social rather than an individual process.
construe | talqin qilmoq | Her silence was construed as agreement by the rest of the committee.
expound | batafsil tushuntirmoq | In his final lecture, he expounded his views on moral responsibility.
problematise | muammolashtirmoq | Recent studies problematise the simple division between nature and culture.
contextualise | kontekstga qoʻymoq | It is important to contextualise these letters within the politics of the time.
## Nazariya va tushuncha
paradigm | paradigma | Quantum theory marked a profound shift in the scientific paradigm.
framework | ramka, asosiy tuzilma | The study uses a theoretical framework borrowed from economics.
construct | konstrukt, tushuncha | Many sociologists see childhood as a social construct.
axiom | aksioma | The whole system rests on a handful of simple axioms.
postulate | postulat, faraz | The second postulate of the theory remains highly controversial.
dichotomy | dixotomiya, ikkiga boʻlinish | The book challenges the false dichotomy between reason and emotion.
nuance | nuans, nozik farq | The translation loses much of the nuance of the original poem.
discourse | nutq, diskurs | Environmental issues now dominate public discourse in many countries.
epistemology | epistemologiya | Her course on epistemology asks how we can know anything for certain.
taxonomy | taksonomiya, tasnif | The researchers proposed a new taxonomy of learning difficulties.
## Ehtiyotkorona til
arguably | ehtimol, bahsli boʻlsa-da | This is arguably the most important discovery of the century.
ostensibly | goʻyo, tashqi koʻrinishidan | The meeting was ostensibly about funding, but nobody mentioned money.
presumably | chamasi, aftidan | The manuscript was presumably written in the early eighteenth century.
tentative | taxminiy, vaqtinchalik | These are only tentative conclusions based on the first year of data.
speculative | spekulyativ, taxminga asoslangan | At this stage, any explanation for the pattern remains largely speculative.
conceivably | nazariy jihatdan mumkinki | The effect could conceivably be stronger in younger children.
to some extent | muayyan darajada | To some extent, the results confirm what teachers had long suspected.
it would appear that | koʻrinishidan | It would appear that the virus spreads more slowly in dry weather.
by and large | umuman olganda | By and large, the students responded positively to the new course.
caveat | ogohlantirish, shart | One important caveat is that all participants were university students.
## Akademik sifatlar
empirical | empirik, tajribaga asoslangan | There is little empirical evidence to support this popular claim.
rigorous | qattiqqoʻl, puxta | The new drug went through rigorous testing before it was approved.
salient | koʻzga tashlanadigan, muhim | The most salient feature of the data is the sharp drop in 2009.
cogent | ishontirarli, asosli | She made a cogent case for changing the way we measure poverty.
seminal | poydevor qoʻyuvchi, muhim | His seminal paper on memory is still cited by thousands of researchers.
inconclusive | aniq natija bermaydigan | The results of the trial were inconclusive, so a larger study is planned.
interdisciplinary | fanlararo | The centre encourages interdisciplinary research between biology and computing.
peripheral | ikkinchi darajali, chekka | These questions are peripheral to the main argument of the book.
robust | mustahkam, ishonchli | The findings proved robust across several different countries.
spurious | soxta, aldovchi | The apparent link between the two trends turned out to be spurious.
## Akademik qoliplar
in light of | yorugʻida, hisobga olib | In light of these findings, the policy should be reconsidered.
with regard to | -ga kelsak, boʻyicha | With regard to funding, the university has promised further support.
notwithstanding | -ga qaramay | Notwithstanding its limitations, the study offers valuable insights.
vis-a-vis | bilan solishtirganda, nisbatan | The paper examines the position of small states vis-a-vis their larger neighbours.
inter alia | jumladan, boshqalar qatori | The report covers, inter alia, housing, transport and public health.
it is worth noting that | taʼkidlash joizki | It is worth noting that the survey was carried out before the crisis.
to put it another way | boshqacha qilib aytganda | To put it another way, the cheaper option may cost more in the long run.
as outlined above | yuqorida bayon etilganidek | As outlined above, the method has three main stages.
this paper argues that | ushbu maqola shuni taʼkidlaydi | This paper argues that early bilingualism improves concentration.
further research is needed | qoʻshimcha tadqiqot talab etiladi | Further research is needed to confirm these results in older adults.
