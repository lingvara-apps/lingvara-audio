# UNIT: Fikr bildirish | Fikr, qoʻshilish, eʼtiroz, koʻndirish, munosabatlar
## Fikr bildirish
opinion | fikr, qarash | In my opinion, the new park is the best thing in town.
point of view | nuqtai nazar | From my point of view, the plan is far too expensive.
perspective | perspektiva, nigoh | Living abroad gave Emre a completely new perspective on life.
belief | eʼtiqod, ishonch | It is my firm belief that everyone deserves a second chance.
impression | taassurot | My first impression of the city was that it was very friendly.
reasonable | maʼqul, oqilona | I think that's a perfectly reasonable thing to ask.
personally | shaxsan | Personally, I prefer reading the news to watching it on TV.
as far as I'm concerned | men uchun, oʻzimcha | As far as I'm concerned, the meeting was a waste of time.
honestly | ochigʻini aytsam, rosti | Honestly, I don't think the plan will work.
judgement | mulohaza, hukm | In my judgement, we should wait until the prices fall.
## Qoʻshilish
absolutely | mutlaqo, aniq | Absolutely, I think that's a brilliant idea for the party.
exactly | aynan, xuddi shunday | Exactly, that's what I was trying to say all along.
I couldn't agree more | qoʻshilmasdan ilojim yoʻq, toʻppa-toʻgʻri | You think we need more cycle lanes? I couldn't agree more.
fair point | oʻrinli fikr | That's a fair point; we should check the prices first.
approve | maʼqullamoq, tasdiqlamoq | My parents didn't approve of my decision to leave university.
approval | maʼqullash, rozilik | The new playground still needs the council's approval.
in favour of | tarafdor | Are you in favour of banning cars from the city centre?
go along with | qoʻshilmoq, koʻnmoq | I'll go along with your plan, even though I have doubts.
you can say that again | ming marta qoʻshilaman, aynan shunday | This queue is taking forever. You can say that again!
I'm with you | siz bilan bir fikrdaman, qoʻshilaman | I'm with you on this one; the hotel was far too noisy.
## Eʼtiroz etish
clash | toʻqnash kelmoq, zid kelmoq | My views often clash with my father's on politics.
objection | eʼtiroz | Does anyone have any objection to starting the meeting early?
oppose | qarshi chiqmoq | Many residents strongly oppose the plan to build a new motorway.
on the contrary | aksincha | It wasn't boring at all; on the contrary, it was fascinating.
I'm not so sure | unchalik amin emasman | You think he'll say yes? I'm not so sure about that.
reject | rad etmoq | The committee rejected our proposal without any explanation.
unreasonable | asossiz, mantiqsiz | It's unreasonable to expect us to work every weekend.
I beg to differ | kechirasiz-u, qoʻshilmayman | You say the film was dull, but I beg to differ.
not necessarily | shart emas, har doim ham emas | Expensive restaurants are not necessarily better than cheap ones.
challenge | shubha ostiga qoʻymoq, eʼtiroz bildirmoq | Sofia challenged the referee's decision after the game.
## Koʻndirish
persuade | koʻndirmoq | Sofia persuaded her brother to join the gym with her.
convince | ishontirmoq | It took me hours to convince my dad to buy a dog.
convincing | ishontarli | She gave a convincing reason for missing the deadline.
persuasive | ishontirish qobiliyatiga ega | Omar wrote a persuasive letter asking for a refund.
urge | qattiq tavsiya qilmoq, undamoq | Doctors urge people to drink more water in hot weather.
talk into | koʻndirib bajarishga majburlamoq | Omar talked me into going camping in the rain.
bring round | fikrini oʻzgartirib koʻndirmoq | Mum didn't like the idea at first, but we brought her round.
justify | oqlamoq, asoslab bermoq | How can you justify spending so much on a phone?
justification | asos, vaj | There is no justification for being rude to waiters.
logical | mantiqiy | The most logical solution is to share the car.
## Munozara
debate | bahs, munozara | There was a lively debate about social media in class today.
issue | masala, muammo | Climate change is an issue that affects every country.
controversial | bahsli | The film's ending was quite controversial among critics.
debatable | bahsli, munozarali | Whether the new rules will help is still debatable.
side | tomon | Whose side are you on in this argument, mine or hers?
respond | javob bermoq, munosabat bildirmoq | How did your boss respond when you asked for a raise?
pros and cons | ijobiy va salbiy tomonlari | Let's think about the pros and cons before we decide.
speak out | ochiqchasiga gapirmoq, ovoz chiqarmoq | Few people dared to speak out against the new rules.
maintain | daʼvo qilmoq, taʼkidlamoq | Lukas still maintains that he was not driving too fast.
that's beside the point | gap bu haqda emas | It's a nice car, but that's beside the point; we can't afford it.
## Aniqlik va shubha
certain | aniq, ishonchli | I'm certain that I locked the door before we left.
doubt | shubha | There's no doubt that she is the best player in the team.
doubtful | shubhali | It's doubtful whether the concert will go ahead in this storm.
obviously | aniqki, tabiiyki | Obviously, nobody wants to work on a Sunday morning.
apparently | koʻrinishidan | Apparently, the new café on the corner is really good.
undoubtedly | shubhasiz | This is undoubtedly the hottest summer we've ever had.
surely | harqalay, albatta | Surely you don't believe everything you read online?
unconvinced | ishontirilmagan, ishonmagan | Diego remained unconvinced by the salesman's promises.
sceptical | shubhachi, gumonfurs | I'm sceptical about diets that promise quick results.
I doubt it | hech ham unday deb oʻylamayman | Will the bus come on time today? I doubt it.
## Tepki ifadeleri
you must be joking | shaka qilayotgan boʻlsangiz kerak | They want fifty pounds for a sandwich? You must be joking!
what a shame | naqadar achinarli | You missed the last bus? What a shame!
good for you | barakalla, ofarin | You finally passed your driving test? Good for you!
how awful | naqadar dahshatli | Your bag was stolen on holiday? How awful!
I see what you mean | nima demoqchi ekaningizni tushunib turibman | I see what you mean, but I still think we should wait.
that makes sense | mantiqqa toʻgʻri keladi, tushunarli | Leaving early to avoid the traffic? That makes sense.
fancy that | buni qarang-a | Fancy that, Yuki and Lukas are getting married!
whatever | nima boʻlsa ham, farqi yoʻq | Whatever you decide, I'll support you all the way.
come off it | qoʻysangiz-chi, boʻrttirmang | Come off it! You didn't really meet a famous singer.
point taken | tushunarli, haqli ekansiz | Point taken; I'll be more careful with the budget next time.
