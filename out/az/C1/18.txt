# UNIT: Rəsmi üslub | Bürokratik dil, rəsmi yazışma, qəliblər
## Rəsmi yazışma
herewith | əlavədə, işbu məktupla | Please find herewith a copy of the signed agreement for your records.
enclose | əlavə etmək, iliştirmək | I enclose a copy of my birth certificate as requested.
covering letter | müşayiət məktubu, ön yazı | Always send a short covering letter with your CV.
the undersigned | aşağıda imzası olanlar | We, the undersigned, request that the library remain open on Sundays.
memorandum (memoranda) | xatırlatma qeydi, memorandum | The director circulated a memorandum about the new security procedures.
addressee | ünvan sahibi, məktubu alan | If the addressee has moved, the letter will be returned to the sender.
letterhead | antetli vərəq | The reference must be printed on the university's official letterhead.
carbon copy | surət, nüsxə | A carbon copy of the decision was sent to the regional office.
acknowledge receipt | alındığını təsdiq etmək | Please acknowledge receipt of this notice by replying to this email.
in receipt of | əldə etmiş olaraq, almış olaraq | We are in receipt of your letter dated the fourth of March.
## Rəsmi feillər

endeavour | səy göstərmək, çalışmaq | We will endeavour to respond to all enquiries within two working days.
ascertain | müəyyən etmək, dəqiqləşdirmək | The inspectors are trying to ascertain the cause of the fire.
expedite | sürətləndirmək | Paying an extra fee will expedite the processing of your visa.
rectify | düzəltmək, aradan qaldırmaq | The bank has promised to rectify the error as soon as possible.
procure | təmin etmək, əldə etmək | The hospital struggled to procure enough equipment during the winter.
reimburse | xərci geri ödəmək | The company will reimburse your travel expenses on presentation of receipts.
remit | pul köçürmək, havalə etmək | Kindly remit the outstanding amount within fourteen days.
dispatch | göndərmək, yola salmaq | Your order was dispatched this morning and should arrive on Friday.

## Rəsmi qoşma və bağlayıcılar
in accordance with | uyğun olaraq, -ə əsasən | The waste must be disposed of in accordance with local regulations.
thereof | onun, bunun (rəsmi) | The tenant is responsible for the property and any part thereof.
whereby | vasitəsilə, sayəsində | They set up a system whereby staff can report problems anonymously.
prior to | -dən əvvəl (rəsmi) | Passengers must check in at least two hours prior to departure.
in lieu of | əvəzinə | Staff may take extra days off in lieu of overtime pay.
subject to | -dən asılı olaraq, tabe | All prices are subject to change without prior notice.
in respect of | ilə əlaqədar, barəsində | No payment has been received in respect of the March invoice.
save for | -dən başqa, istisna olmaqla | The building was empty, save for a security guard on the ground floor.
for the purposes of | məqsədilə, baxımından | For the purposes of this agreement, a working day excludes public holidays.
in the absence of | yoxluğunda, olmadıqda | In the absence of the chair, her deputy will lead the meeting.

further to | -ə istinadən, davamı olaraq | Further to our telephone conversation, I am sending the revised schedule.
with reference to | ilə əlaqədar, istinadən | I am writing with reference to the vacancy advertised on your website.
yours faithfully | dərin hörmətlə (adı bilinmədikdə) | The letter began with "Dear Sir or Madam" and ended with "Yours faithfully".
kind regards | ən xoş arzularla, hörmətlə | She always ends her work emails with "Kind regards" and her full name.
to whom it may concern | aidiyyəti şəxsə | The reference letter was addressed "To whom it may concern".
for your attention | diqqətinizə, məlumat üçün | I am forwarding this complaint for your attention and further action.

I look forward to hearing from you | cavabınızı səbirsizliklə gözləyirəm | Thank you for considering my application; I look forward to hearing from you.
