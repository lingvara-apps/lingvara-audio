# UNIT: Danışıq dili | Hissəciklər, nidasözlər, gündəlik jarqon
## Dolğu sözləri
you know | bilirsiniz, yəni | It was one of those days when, you know, nothing really works.
I mean | yəni, demək istəyirəm ki | The film was fine, I mean, it just wasn't what I expected.
kind of | bir növ, bir qədər | I'm kind of tired after the trip, so let's stay in tonight.
sort of | demək olar ki, bir növ | Emre sort of agreed, but he didn't sound very convinced.
basically | əsasən, qısası | Basically, we need more time and a bit more money to finish.
literally | məcazi mənada deyil, sözün əsl mənasında | I was literally the only person in the whole cinema last night.
or something | və ya elə bir şey, falan | Let's grab a coffee or something before the meeting starts.
and stuff | və s., falan filan | We talked about work and stuff, nothing too serious really.
if you like | necə deyərlər, tabiri caizse | The village was, if you like, a little world of its own.
you see | görürsənmi, bax belə | You see, I never learned to drive, so I always take the bus.
## Nidalar
whoa | hop, dayan görək | Whoa, slow down, you're driving far too fast on this road!
gosh | vay, aman | Gosh, I didn't realise it was already so late in the evening.
blimey | hay elə, yox artıq (Britaniya) | Blimey, have you seen how much they charge for parking here?
yikes | eyvah, aman tanrım | Yikes, I just noticed I sent that email to the wrong person.
whoops | hoppala, üzr istəyirəm | Whoops, I think I've just spilled coffee all over your notes.
phew | oh, nə yaxşı | Phew, we caught the last train just before the doors closed.
ugh | öf, of | Ugh, it's raining again and I forgot my umbrella at home.
eww | iyrənc, ıyy | Eww, there's something sticky all over the handle of this door.
aha | hah, bildim | Aha, so that's why the printer kept jamming all morning!
huh | hə?, hı | Huh, I never thought Omar would actually move abroad one day.
## Reaksiya ifadələri
no way | ola bilməz, yox artıq | No way, you got the job after only one interview?
you bet | əlbəttə, hem də necə | Are you coming to the party on Saturday? You bet I am!
fair enough | başadüşüləndir, məqbuldur | You need more time to decide? Fair enough, let's talk next week.
tell me about it | mənə demə, elə mən də onu deyirəm | This traffic is unbearable today. Tell me about it, I've been stuck for an hour.
you don't say | yox artıq, ciddi deyirsən | The shop is closed on Sunday? You don't say, what a surprise.
big deal | böyük bir şey, nə böyük məsələ | So he has a new car, big deal, it doesn't make him a better person.
so what | nə olsun, dəxli nədir | So what if we're a little late, the party goes on until midnight.
my bad | mənim səhvimdir, bağışla | My bad, I totally forgot to send you the address yesterday.
no kidding | zarafat etmirəm, doğrudanmı | No kidding, Yuki really ran the whole marathon without any training.
go figure | indi gəl anla, gör kimdən soruşursan | He hates travelling but became a pilot, go figure.
## Müraciət və salamlaşma
mate | dostum (Britaniya) | Cheers for the help yesterday, mate, I really owe you one.
buddy | qardaş, dostum (ABŞ) | Hey buddy, could you give me a hand with these heavy boxes?
dude | qaqadaş, ahbap (ABŞ) | Dude, you have to try the burgers at that new place downtown.
folks | camaat, xalq | Okay folks, the bus leaves in ten minutes, so please don't be late.
cheers | sağ ol, təşəkkürlər | Cheers for holding the door, that bag was heavier than I thought.
ta | çox sağ ol (Britaniya, səmimi) | Ta for the tea, love, it's exactly what I needed after work.
howdy | salam (ABŞ, kənd yeri) | Howdy, neighbour, are you coming to the barbecue this weekend?
what's up | necəsən, nə var nə yox | What's up, Diego? I haven't heard from you in weeks.
long time no see | görüşmədiyimiz uzun zaman oldu | Long time no see, Camille, you look great after your holiday!
catch you later | sonra görüşərik | I've got to run to my lecture now, catch you later at lunch.
## Jarqon sifətlər
gutted | məyus, darmadağın olmuş | Lukas was absolutely gutted when his team lost the final.
knackered | əldən düşmüş, yorğun-argın | I'm completely knackered after moving furniture all day long.
chuffed | kefi kök, məmnun | Maria was really chuffed with the birthday present we got her.
dodgy | şübhəli, etibarsız | That website looks a bit dodgy, I wouldn't enter my card details there.
skint | pulsuz, zəlil (Britaniya) | I'd love to come to the festival, but I'm a bit skint this month.
hyped | həyəcanlı, qızışmış | The kids are so hyped about the school trip to the seaside.
chill | sakit, rahat | The new manager is pretty chill, she never shouts at anyone.
peckish | azca acıxmış | I'm feeling a bit peckish, shall we grab a sandwich somewhere?
gobsmacked | şoka düşmüş, mat qalmış | We were gobsmacked when Ali announced he was leaving for Australia.
minted | varlı, pul içində üzən | Her parents must be minted to own a house like that by the sea.
## Jarqon isimlər
bloke | kişi, oğlan (Britaniya) | The bloke next door helped me fix my bike this morning.
quid | sterlinq (jarqon) | The tickets were only ten quid each, so we bought four.
buck | dollar (jarqon) | Can you lend me twenty bucks until Friday?
grand | min (pul vahidi) | They paid about five grand for that old car, which seems a lot.
telly | televizor (Britaniya) | There's nothing good on the telly tonight, let's read instead.
loo | tualet (Britaniya) | Excuse me, do you know where the loo is in this building?
fiver | beşlik (pul vahidi) | I found a fiver in the pocket of my old winter coat.
grub | yemək, qida | The grub at the student canteen is cheap but surprisingly tasty.
cuppa | bir fincan çay (Britaniya) | Sit down and have a cuppa, you look like you need a break.
brekkie | səhər yeməyi (jarqon) | We had a huge brekkie at the café before going for a hike.
## Jarqon fellər
crash | sızmaq (yata bilmək), gecələmək | Can I crash at your place tonight? I missed the last bus.
bail | tədbirdən yayınmaq, sözündən dönmək | Sorry, I have to bail tonight, something came up at work.
ditch | başından eləmək, tərk etmək | We ditched the car and walked the rest of the way into town.
freak out | panikaya düşmək, ağlını itirmək | Don't freak out, but I think I've lost the keys to the flat.
binge | axına düşmək, fasiləsiz izləmək | We binged the whole series in one weekend and regretted nothing.
splurge | şütümək, israf etmək | After the exams, Anna splurged on a fancy dinner with her friends.
nick | oğurlamaq, aşırımak (Britaniya) | Someone nicked my bike from outside the library yesterday afternoon.
chuck | tullamaq, fırlatmaq | Just chuck your coat on the sofa and make yourself at home.
fancy | könlü çəkmək, istəmək | Do you fancy going to the cinema after dinner tonight?
reckon | zənn etmək, güman etmək | I reckon it'll rain later, so take a jacket with you.
## Gücləndirici sözlər
dead | dəhşət, çox (Britaniya jarqonu) | The new restaurant is dead good, you should definitely go there.
way | qat-qat, çox daha | This phone is way better than the one I had before.
wicked | möhtəşəm, super (jarqon) | That concert last night was wicked, the crowd was amazing.
proper | əsl, gerçək (Britaniya) | We had a proper English breakfast with beans, eggs and toast.
mega | nəhəng, həddindən artıq | The queue for the new ride at the park was mega long.
hella | həddən artıq, çox (ABŞ jarqonu) | That road trip along the coast was hella fun, let's do it again.
flipping | lənətə gəlmiş (yüngül) | The flipping printer has broken down again just before my deadline.
jolly | xeyli, olduqca (Britaniya, köhnə) | That was a jolly good effort, you should be proud of yourself.
ever so | son dərəcə, çox-çox (Britaniya, nəzakətli) | Thank you ever so much for looking after the cat while we were away.
a tad | bir az, azacıq | The soup is a tad too salty, but otherwise it's delicious.
## Danışıq qısaltmaları
gonna | -əcək (-eceksən) (going to əvəzinə) | I'm gonna call my mum after work and tell her the news.
wanna | istəmək (want to əvəzinə) | Do you wanna come with us to the beach this afternoon?
gotta | məcbur olmaq (got to əvəzinə) | Sorry, I've gotta go, my train leaves in fifteen minutes.
dunno | bilmirəm (don't know əvəzinə) | I dunno where Omar put the keys, ask him when he gets back.
lemme | icazə ver (let me əvəzinə) | Lemme check my calendar before I promise anything for next week.
gimme | mənə ver (give me əvəzinə) | Gimme a minute, I'm just finishing this email to the client.
y'all | hamınız (ABŞ cənubu) | Are y'all coming to the family dinner on Sunday evening?
ain't | deyil (isn't/am not/aren't əvəzinə) | That ain't the way we do things around here, my friend.
innit | elə deyilmi (Britaniya jarqonu) | It's freezing outside today, innit? Better take a scarf.
cos | çünki (because əvəzinə) | I stayed home cos I wasn't feeling great this morning.

long story short | qısası, uzun sözün qısası | Long story short, we missed the ferry and spent the night on the island.
if you ask me | məndən soruşursansa | If you ask me, the old design looked much better than the new one.
to be fair | haqsızlıq etməyək, düzünü desək | To be fair, he did warn us that the road would be closed.
just saying | sadəcə deyirəm də | That jacket looked better on you than this one, just saying.
no offence | xətrinə dəyməsin | No offence, but your cooking has been a bit salty lately.
between you and me | aramızda qalsın, sirr olsun | Between you and me, I think Lukas is planning to quit next month.
for what it's worth | nə dərəcədə önəmlidir bilmirəm amma | For what it's worth, I think you made the right decision.
at the end of the day | günün sonunda, hər şeydən əvvəl | At the end of the day, it's your money, so it's your choice.
