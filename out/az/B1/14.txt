# UNIT: Phrasal verbs I | look up, give up, turn down, put off…
## Look ile
look up | lüğətdə axtarmaq | If you don't know a word, look it up in the dictionary.
look out for | gözətlemek, ehtiyatlı olmaq | Look out for pickpockets when you're in the city centre.
look on | kənardan izləmək, tamaşa etmək | A crowd of tourists looked on as the band played.
look ahead | gələcəyə baxmaq, gələcəyi düşünmək | Let's forget last year and look ahead to the new season.
look into | araşdırmaq, tədqiq etmək | The manager promised to look into my complaint.
look out | diqqət etmək, ehtiyatlı olmaq | Look out! There's a car coming round the corner.
look through | gözdən keçirmək, nəzərdən keçirmək | Could you look through my essay before I hand it in?
look back on | xatırlamaq, keçmişi yada salmaq | I look back on my school days with great pleasure.
look down on | yuxarıdan aşağı baxmaq, aşağılamaq | She looks down on anyone who doesn't have a degree.
look up to | nümunə götürmək, hörmət etmək | Emre has always looked up to his older brother.
## Get ile
get over (got, got) | sağalmaq, öhdəsindən gəlmək | It took her months to get over the break-up.
get along with (got, got) | yola getmək, xoş münasibətdə olmaq | Do you get along with your new flatmates?
get by (got, got) | dolanmaq, çətinliklə də olsa keçinmək | We can get by on one salary if we're careful.
get rid of (got, got) | qurtulmaq, xilas olmaq | I need to get rid of all these old magazines.
get away with (got, got) | cəzasız qutarmaq, qurtulmaq | He cheated in the exam and got away with it.
get in (got, got) | (nəqliyyata) minmək, içəri girmək | Get in, I'll give you a lift to the station.
get off (got, got) | (nəqliyyatdan) düşmək | We need to get off at the next stop.
get through (got, got) | əlaqə yaratmaq, zəngi çatdırmaq | I tried calling the bank but couldn't get through.
get down to (got, got) | (işə) girişmək, başlamaq | Stop chatting and get down to work, everyone!
get across (got, got) | çatdırmaq, izah edib başa salmaq | The teacher used pictures to get her message across.
## Give ve take ile
give up (gave, given) | təslim olmaq, əl çəkmək | Don't give up now; you've almost finished the course.
give back (gave, given) | geri qaytarmaq | Please give back my charger when you've finished.
give away (gave, given) | hədiyyə etmək, bağışlamaq | Anna gave away all her old toys to the children's hospital.
give in (gave, given) | güzəsətə getmək, boyun əymək | The child cried until his parents finally gave in.
give out (gave, given) | paylamaq | The teacher gave out the exam papers at nine o'clock.
take up (took, taken) | (məşğuliyyətə) başlayaraq məşğul olmaq | Yuki took up yoga to help her relax after work.
take over (took, taken) | idarəni ələ almaq, öhdəsinə götürmək | Sofia will take over the project while I'm away.
take back (took, taken) | geri qaytarmaq; sözünü geri götürmək | These shoes are too small, so I'll take them back.
take on (took, taken) | (iş) götürmək, öhdəsinə götürmək | I can't take on any more work this month.
take apart (took, taken) | hissələrə ayırmaq, sökmək | Lukas took apart the old radio to see how it worked.
## Turn ve put ile
turn down | rədd etmək; səsi azaltmaq | She turned down the job because the pay was too low.
turn up | səsi artırmaq; gözlənilmədən peyda olmaq | Can you turn up the radio? I love this song.
turn around | geriyə dönmək | She turned around when she heard her name.
turn back | geri qayıtmaq | The road was flooded, so we had to turn back.
turn into | çevrilmək, dəyişib başqa şey olmaq | The small café turned into a very popular restaurant.
turn out | axırda bəlli olmaq, çıxmaq | The weather turned out to be lovely after all.
put off (put, put) | təxirə salmaq | We had to put off the meeting until next Monday.
put forward (put, put) | irəli sürmək | Ali put forward a plan to save money on energy.
put up with (put, put) | dözmək, səbir etmək | I can't put up with this noise any longer.
put together (put, put) | bir araya gətirmək, yığmaq | It took us three hours to put together the new bookcase.
## Run ve come ile
run after (ran, run) | dalınca qaçmaq | The little boy ran after the ball into the garden.
run into (ran, run) | təsadüfən rastlaşmaq | I ran into my old teacher at the supermarket yesterday.
run away (ran, run) | qaçıb getmək | The dog ran away when it heard the fireworks.
run over (ran, run) | maşın vurmaq, üstündən keçmək | Be careful, or you'll run over that cat!
come across (came, come) | təsadüfən tapmaq, rast gəlmək | I came across some old photos while cleaning the attic.
come up with (came, come) | fikir tapmaq, irəli sürmək | Diego came up with a great idea for the school trip.
come back (came, come) | geri gəlmək, qayıtmaq | When are you coming back from your trip?
come in (came, come) | içəri daxil olmaq | Come in and sit down; the kettle has just boiled.
come along (came, come) | birlikdə gəlmək, müşayiət etmək | We're going to the beach; why don't you come along?
come down with (came, come) | xəstələnmək | I think I'm coming down with a cold.
## Go ve break ile
go on (went, gone) | davam etmək | Please go on with your story; I'm listening.
go off (went, gone) | (siqnal) çalmaq; xarab olmaq | My alarm didn't go off this morning, so I was late.
go through (went, gone) | (çətin günlər) yaşamaq, başından keçirmək | She went through a difficult time after losing her job.
go over (went, gone) | gözdən keçirmək, yoxlamaq | Let's go over the plan one more time before we start.
go ahead (went, gone) | buyurmaq, irəli getmək | Can I borrow your pen? Sure, go ahead.
go back (went, gone) | geri qayıtmaq | I'd love to go back to Scotland one day.
break down (broke, broken) | xarab olmaq, sıradan çıxmaq | Our car broke down in the middle of the motorway.
break off (broke, broken) | kəsmək, dayandırmaq | They broke off their engagement just before the wedding.
break into (broke, broken) | zorla daxil olmaq, oğurluğa girmək | Someone broke into our house while we were away.
break out (broke, broken) | baş vermək, alovlanmaq | A fire broke out in the kitchen late last night.
## Evde

throw away (threw, thrown) | zibilə tullamaq, atmaq | Don't throw away those boxes; we can use them.
tidy up | səliqə-sahman yaratmaq, yığışdırmaq | We need to tidy up before the guests arrive.
wash up | qab-qacaq yumaq | I'll cook dinner if you wash up afterwards.
sort out | səliqəyə salmaq, həll etmək | I need a whole day to sort out my wardrobe.
clear up | səliqəyə salmaq; hava açılmaq | Who is going to clear up this mess in the kitchen?
clean out | tamamilə təmizləmək, boşaltmaq | We cleaned out the garage and found my old bike.
wipe off | silib təmizləmək | Wipe off the crumbs before you put the tablecloth on.
use up | işlədib qurtarmaq, sərf edib bitirmək | We used up all the eggs making the birthday cake.
switch off | söndürmək (cihazı) | Switch off your phone during the film, please.

carry on | davam etmək | Carry on talking; I'm just going to make some tea.

drop out | məktəbdən və ya universitetdən çıxmaq | He dropped out of university to start a band.
