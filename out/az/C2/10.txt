# UNIT: Deyimlər və frazeoloji birləşmələr | get, take, put və come ilə ifadələr
## get ilə ifadələr: anlamaq və fərqinə varmaq
get the drift | nə demək istəndiyini anlamaq | I won't explain every detail, but I think you get the drift.
get the gist | ana xəttini qavramaq | I didn't catch every word, but I got the gist of his argument.
get the picture | vəziyyəti başa düşmək | Nobody called her back for a week, so she finally got the picture.
get to the bottom of | işin əslini üzə çıxarmaq | The detective swore she would get to the bottom of the missing paintings.
get wise to | fərqinə varmaq | Customers eventually got wise to the company's hidden fees and left.
get one's wires crossed | səhv başa düşmək | We must have got our wires crossed, because I waited at the wrong café.
get the message | işarəni anlamaq | When he kept yawning loudly, the guests finally got the message and left.
get a word in edgeways | sözə qarışa bilmək | Omar talked so much at dinner that nobody could get a word in edgeways.
get one's bearings | istiqaməti tapmaq | It took Yuki a few weeks to get her bearings in the new department.
get ahead of oneself | qabağa qaçmaq | Let's not get ahead of ourselves; we haven't even signed the contract yet.
## get ilə ifadələr: qisas və şans
get even with | qisas almaq | He spent years plotting to get even with the partner who had cheated him.
get one's own back | əvəzini çıxmaq | After the prank, Sofia got her own back by hiding his car keys.
get a kick out of | böyük həzz almaq | My grandfather gets a real kick out of beating us at chess.
get the short end of the stick | zərərçəkən tərəf olmaq | In every group project, the quietest student gets the short end of the stick.
get away with murder | cəzasız qurtarmaq | The youngest child gets away with murder because everyone spoils him.
get a raw deal | haqsızlığa məruz qalmaq | The workers felt they had got a raw deal from the new management.
get the better of | üstün gəlmək | In the end, curiosity got the better of her and she opened the letter.
get a rise out of | əsəbiləşdirmək | He only says those things to get a rise out of you, so ignore him.
get off lightly | ucuz qurtarmaq | Considering the damage, you got off lightly with just a small fine.
get one's comeuppance | layiqini almaq | The arrogant villain gets his comeuppance in the final chapter.
## get ilə ifadələr: hərəkətə keçmək
get one's act together | özünü ələ almaq | If you don't get your act together, you'll fail the final exams.
get a grip | səbirli olmaq | Get a grip, Lukas; it's only a job interview, not the end of the world.
get carried away | özünü itirmək | I got a bit carried away at the market and bought far too much cheese.
get a move on | tələsmək | We'd better get a move on or we'll miss the last ferry to the island.
get cracking | dərhal işə girişmək | The deadline is on Friday, so let's get cracking on the report.
get round to | vaxt tapıb etmək | I finally got round to fixing the leaking tap after three months.
get a second wind | təzədən güc toplamaq | Halfway through the marathon, Diego got a second wind and sped up.
get the green light | icazə almaq | The architects finally got the green light to start building the bridge.
get nowhere fast | heç bir irəliləyiş əldə etməmək | Arguing with the ticket machine, we were clearly getting nowhere fast.
get one's skates on | tələsmək (danışıq) | Get your skates on, Anna, the taxi has been waiting for ten minutes.
## take ilə ifadələr: münasibət və reaksiya
take something in one's stride | soyuqqanlıqla qarşılamaq | She took the sudden promotion in her stride and calmly reorganised the team.
take umbrage at | inciyib küsmək | The chef took umbrage at the critic's comments about his signature dish.
take exception to | etiraz etmək | Several members took exception to the chairman's dismissive tone.
take a dim view of | xoşlamamaq | The headmistress takes a dim view of students using phones in class.
take heed of | qulaq asmaq | Climbers who fail to take heed of the weather warnings put lives at risk.
take a hard line | sərt mövqe tutmaq | The government has decided to take a hard line on tax evasion this year.
take the high road | ləyaqətli davranmaq | Instead of replying to the insults, Emre chose to take the high road.
take it or leave it | istərsən qəbul et, istərsən etmə | This is my final offer for the car, so take it or leave it.
take the heat | tənqidə sinə gərmək | When the launch failed, the project manager had to take the heat alone.
take someone to task | haqq-hesab çəkmək | The editor took the young reporter to task for not checking his sources.
## take ilə ifadələr: təşəbbüs və dəyişiklik
take the reins | rəhbərliyi ələ almaq | When her father retired, Sofia took the reins of the family hotel.
take stock of | vəziyyəti təhlil etmək | After the crisis, the company paused to take stock of its finances.
take a toll on | mənfi təsir etmək | Years of night shifts had taken a heavy toll on his health.
take the edge off | təsirini azaltmaq | A cup of strong tea took the edge off the cold winter morning.
take a back seat | arxa planda qalmaq | After retiring, she was happy to take a back seat and let her son run the farm.
take a stab at | bəxtini sınamaq | I've never baked bread before, but I'll take a stab at it this weekend.
take root | kök salmaq | The idea of a four-day working week has slowly taken root in many firms.
take shape | formalasmaq | As the weeks passed, the plans for the new museum began to take shape.
take a turn for the worse | pisləşmək | The patient's condition took a turn for the worse during the night.
take the floor | söz almaq | When Maria took the floor, the noisy conference hall fell silent.
## take ilə ifadələr: sosial əlaqələr
take a pew | oturmaq (danışıq) | Come in and take a pew; the kettle has just boiled.
take a leaf out of someone's book | nümunə götürmək | You should take a leaf out of Ali's book and save a little every month.
take pride of place | baş yerdə durmaq | Her grandmother's old clock takes pride of place in the living room.
take the liberty of | cəsarət edib etmək | I took the liberty of booking a table for us at the seafood restaurant.
take the rap for | başqasının günahını üzərinə götürmək | He refused to take the rap for a mistake his manager had made.
take it upon oneself | üzərinə götürmək | Camille took it upon herself to organise the neighbourhood clean-up.
take the bait | yemi udmaq | He tried to provoke me with a rude comment, but I didn't take the bait.
take someone's word for it | sözünə inanmaq | I haven't seen the film, but I'll take your word for it that it's brilliant.
take the easy way out | asan yolu seçmək | Quitting would be taking the easy way out, so she decided to keep trying.
take one's leave | icazə istəyib getmək (rəsmi) | The ambassador bowed politely and took his leave before midnight.
## put ilə ifadələr: mane olmaq və bitirmək
put a damper on | həvəsini qırmaq | The sudden storm put a damper on the village summer festival.
put paid to | son qoymaq | A serious knee injury put paid to his dreams of playing professionally.
put a spanner in the works | mane olmaq | The strike at the port has put a spanner in the works for our delivery.
put something on the back burner | təxirə salmaq | We've had to put the kitchen renovation on the back burner this year.
put something to bed | yekunlaşdırmaq | Let's put this issue to bed before we move on to the budget.
put up a fight | mübarizə aparmaq | The defending champions put up a good fight but lost in the final minutes.
put someone on the spot | çətin vəziyyətə salmaq | The journalist put the minister on the spot with an unexpected question.
put someone in their place | yerini göstərmək | When he interrupted her again, she calmly put him in his place.
put one's oar in | burnunu soxmaq | Nobody asked for his opinion, but he still had to put his oar in.
put someone through their paces | bacarığını yoxlamaq | The coach put the new players through their paces on the first day.
## put ilə ifadələr: düşüncə və söz
put two and two together | məntiqi nəticə çıxarmaq | When I saw the empty cake box and his guilty smile, I put two and two together.
put the record straight | həqiqəti ortaya qoymaq | The author gave an interview to put the record straight about her past.
to put it mildly | yumşaq desək | The hotel was not exactly luxurious, to put it mildly.
put out feelers | ehtiyatla yoxlamaq | The company has put out feelers to see if anyone wants to buy the brand.
put pen to paper | qələmə almaq | It took him ten years to finally put pen to paper and write his memoirs.
put a premium on | böyük önəm vermək | Our school puts a premium on creativity rather than memorisation.
put stock in | etibar etmək | I don't put much stock in horoscopes or fortune tellers.
put in a good word for | xoş sözünü demək | Could you put in a good word for me with the head of marketing?
put one's cards on the table | kartları açıq oynamaq | Let me put my cards on the table: we can't afford to pay more than this.
put something into perspective | düzgün qiymətləndirmək | Visiting the hospital put my small problems into perspective.
## come ilə ifadələr: qəbul etmək və üzə çıxmaq
come to terms with | barışmaq | It took him a long time to come to terms with his father's death.
come clean | etiraf etmək | Eventually she came clean and admitted she had broken the vase.
come to light | üzə çıxmaq | New evidence came to light that proved the prisoner was innocent.
come to grips with | öhdəsindən gəlməyə çalışmaq | Older staff are still coming to grips with the new software.
come of age | yaşına çatmaq; yetkinləşmək | When the twins came of age, they inherited the family vineyard.
come into one's own | özünü doğrultmaq | The quiet student really came into her own in the debating club.
come to the fore | ön plana çıxmaq | During the flood, the town's sense of solidarity came to the fore.
come to pass | həyata keçmək (ədəbi) | Everything the old woman had predicted eventually came to pass.
come full circle | əvvəlki nöqtəsinə qayıtmaq | After decades abroad, he came full circle and returned to his home village.
come out of one's shell | qabığından çıxmaq | Since joining the theatre group, our shy son has come out of his shell.
## come ilə ifadələr: nəticələr və şərtlər
come to the rescue | köməyə gəlmək | When our car broke down, a friendly farmer came to the rescue with his tractor.
come to blows | əlbəyaxa olmaq | The two neighbours almost came to blows over a parking space.
come what may | nə olursa olsun | Come what may, I'll be there for your wedding next spring.
come as no surprise | təəccüblü olmamaq | It will come as no surprise that the trains are late again.
come down to earth | reallığa qayıtmaq | After the holiday, it was hard to come down to earth and face my inbox.
come apart at the seams | dikiş yerindən sökülmək | Without a strong leader, the coalition began to come apart at the seams.
come under fire | tənqid atəşinə tutulmaq | The new rules have come under fire from teachers and parents alike.
come off second best | məğlub olmaq | Whenever my brother argues with our grandmother, he comes off second best.
come in handy | lazım olmaq | Keep that old rope in the car; it might come in handy someday.
come to nothing | puç olmaq | All their careful plans for the expedition came to nothing in the end.
