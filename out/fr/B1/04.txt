# UNIT: Médias et actualité | Presse, TV, réseaux sociaux, information
## La presse et les médias
the press | la presse | The press waited outside the hotel all afternoon.
readership | le lectorat | The magazine has a young readership, mostly students.
reporter | le journaliste, le reporter | A young reporter asked the mayor a difficult question.
editor | le rédacteur en chef, l'éditeur | The editor decided to cut the story to three hundred words.
headline | le gros titre, la manchette | The headline on today's paper is about the floods.
article | l'article | I read an interesting article about sleep on the train.
columnist | le chroniqueur | My favourite columnist writes every Sunday about family life.
front page | la une, la première page | Their wedding photo appeared on the front page of the local paper.
tabloid | le tabloïd, le journal à sensation | Tabloids often print gossip about famous people.
circulation | le tirage, la diffusion | The newspaper's circulation has fallen sharply in recent years.
## Les types d'actualités
breaking news | un flash d'information, les dernières actualités | We interrupt this programme for some breaking news.
news story | un reportage, un article d'actualité | The news story about the lost dog had a happy ending.
current affairs | l'actualité | Emre reads about current affairs every morning before work.
feature article | un article de fond, un grand reportage | The magazine ran a long feature article on young farmers.
opinion piece | une tribune, un article d'opinion | Her opinion piece about school lunches got hundreds of comments.
exclusive | une interview exclusive, un scoop | The channel got an exclusive with the winning team.
scoop | un scoop, une exclusivité | The young journalist got the biggest scoop of the year.
bulletin | un bulletin d'information | There is a short news bulletin every hour on this station.
newsflash | un flash spécial, une information de dernière heure | A newsflash interrupted the film to report the fire.
correspondent | le correspondant | Our correspondent in Tokyo sent this report this morning.
## La télévision
broadcast (broadcast, broadcast) | diffuser | The final will be broadcast live on several channels.
presenter | le présentateur, l'animateur | The presenter smiled and welcomed the guests to the show.
viewer | le téléspectateur | Millions of viewers watched the ceremony last night.
news anchor | le présentateur du journal télévisé | The news anchor read the bad news in a calm voice.
commercial | la publicité, le spot publicitaire | There are too many commercials during this film.
chat show | le talk-show, l'émission de débat | She was a guest on a popular chat show last week.
prime time | la heure de grande écoute | The new series will be shown at prime time on Saturdays.
ratings | les taux d'audience, les audiences | The show was cancelled because its ratings were so low.
remote control | la télécommande | Has anyone seen the remote control? It's not on the sofa.
soap opera | le feuilleton télévisé, le soap opera | My aunt has watched the same soap opera for twenty years.
## La radio et les podcasts
radio station | la station de radio | My dad listens to the same radio station every morning.
listener | l'auditeur | Many listeners called in to share their own stories.
microphone | le microphone | Please speak a little closer to the microphone.
podcast | le podcast | I listen to a history podcast on my way to work.
phone-in | l'émission avec intervention des auditeurs | Diego called a phone-in to complain about the buses.
jingle | le jingle, l'indicatif musical | I can't get that annoying jingle out of my head.
on air | à l'antenne, en direct | Please be quiet; we are on air in two minutes.
tune in | brancher (la radio), syntoniser | Tune in next week to hear the rest of the story.
studio | le studio | The band played two songs live in the radio studio.
episode | l'épisode | In the latest episode, the hosts talk about healthy sleep.
## Les réseaux sociaux
content creator | le créateur de contenu | Yuki left her office job to become a full-time content creator.
blogger | le blogueur | A travel blogger wrote about our small hotel last summer.
meme | le mème | My brother sends me at least ten funny memes every day.
vlogger | le vidéaste web, le vlogger | The vlogger films her daily life in a small Irish village.
clickbait | le piège à clics | That headline is just clickbait; the article says almost nothing.
trending | tendance, en vogue | That song has been trending all week.
troll | le troll | Just ignore the trolls who leave nasty comments under your videos.
repost | repartager | Please don't repost my photos without asking me first.
caption | la légende (d'une photo) | Write a funny caption under the picture of the cat.
mute | mettre en sourdine | I muted the group chat because it never stopped buzzing.
## L'information et la fiabilité
source | la source | Always check the source before you believe a story.
trustworthy | digne de confiance, fiable | Local newspapers are often more trustworthy than random websites.
rumour | la rumeur | There is a rumour that the factory will close soon.
hoax | le canular, la fausse information | The story about the shark in the river turned out to be a hoax.
media literacy | l'éducation aux médias | Schools should teach media literacy from an early age.
allegation | l'allégation, l'accusation | The singer said the allegations were completely untrue.
misinformation | la désinformation, la fausse information | Misinformation spreads quickly during a crisis.
fake news | les fausses nouvelles, les fake news | Teachers are helping students learn to recognise fake news.
biased | partial, orienté | Some viewers think the programme is biased against farmers.
credible | crédible | The police found the witness completely credible.
## Le journalisme
uncover | dévoiler, mettre au jour | Two reporters uncovered a serious problem at the hospital.
anonymous | anonyme | The information came from an anonymous source inside the hospital.
press conference | la conférence de presse | The coach answered questions at a press conference after the match.
spokesperson | le porte-parole | A spokesperson for the airline apologised for the delays.
press release | le communiqué de presse | The museum sent out a press release about the new exhibition.
scandal | le scandale | The scandal was on every front page for a week.
newsroom | la salle de rédaction | The newsroom was busy and noisy on election night.
freedom of the press | la liberté de la presse | Freedom of the press is protected by law in many countries.
eyewitness | le témoin oculaire | An eyewitness described the accident to reporters.
interviewee | la personne interrogée | The interviewee seemed nervous at the start of the conversation.
## La publicité
advertising | la publicité | Advertising aimed at children is strictly controlled in some countries.
advertise | faire de la publicité | The café advertises on local radio every weekend.
publicity | la publicité, la promotion | The film got a lot of publicity before it came out.
slogan | le slogan | Everyone remembers the slogan, but nobody remembers the product.
commercial break | la page publicitaire, la coupure pub | I'll make some tea during the commercial break.
marketing | le marketing | Lukas works in marketing for a small travel company.
billboard | le panneau publicitaire | A huge billboard went up next to the motorway.
promote | promouvoir | The singer is travelling around Europe to promote her new album.
leaflet | le prospectus, le dépliant | Someone put a leaflet about a new pizza place in our letterbox.
catchy | accrocheur, entraînant | The advert had a catchy tune that everybody was humming.
## Les verbes des médias
announce | annoncer | The government announced the new holiday dates this morning.
reveal | révéler | The interview revealed a lot about her childhood.
inform | informer | Please inform us if your address changes.
expose | exposer, dénoncer | The programme exposed the poor conditions in some factories.
exaggerate | exagérer | The newspapers exaggerated the danger of the storm.
mislead (misled, misled) | induire en erreur, tromper | The advert misled customers about the real price.
cover | couvrir (un sujet, un événement) | She has covered sports news for the radio for many years.
confirm | confirmer | The club confirmed that the player will leave in summer.
react | réagir | How did people react to the news on social media?
air | être diffusé, diffuser | The last part of the series will air next Friday.
## Les expressions des médias
hit the headlines | faire la une, faire les gros titres | The small village hit the headlines after the discovery.
make the news | faire l'actualité, passer aux informations | Ali's brave rescue of a dog made the news.
in the public eye | sous le feu des projecteurs, sur le devant de la scène | Living in the public eye can be very stressful.
off the record | de manière informelle, officieusement | The minister told us off the record that he was unhappy.
behind the scenes | dans les coulisses | The film shows what happens behind the scenes of a TV studio.
public opinion | l'opinion publique | Public opinion has changed a lot over the past few years.
mass media | les médias de masse | The mass media has a strong influence on young people.
news agency | l'agence de presse | Most local papers buy their foreign stories from a news agency.
a slow news day | un jour creux (en actualité) | It must be a slow news day if they're reporting on a lost cat.
read between the lines | lire entre les lignes | If you read between the lines, the report is quite critical.
