# UNIT: Style formel | Langage bureaucratique, correspondance officielle, formules
## Correspondance officielle
herewith | ci-joint, par la présente | Please find herewith a copy of the signed agreement for your records.
enclose | joindre, inclure | I enclose a copy of my birth certificate as requested.
covering letter | lettre de motivation, lettre d'accompagnement | Always send a short covering letter with your CV.
the undersigned | les soussignés | We, the undersigned, request that the library remain open on Sundays.
memorandum (memoranda) | mémorandum, note d'information | The director circulated a memorandum about the new security procedures.
addressee | destinataire | If the addressee has moved, the letter will be returned to the sender.
letterhead | en-tête | The reference must be printed on the university's official letterhead.
carbon copy | copie conforme, copie carbone | A carbon copy of the decision was sent to the regional office.
acknowledge receipt | accuser réception | Please acknowledge receipt of this notice by replying to this email.
in receipt of | en possession de, ayant reçu | We are in receipt of your letter dated the fourth of March.
## Verbes formels
commence | commencer, débuter (soutenu) | Construction work will commence on the first of next month.
terminate | résilier, mettre fin | Either party may terminate the contract with thirty days' notice.
endeavour | s'efforcer, tâcher | We will endeavour to respond to all enquiries within two working days.
ascertain | vérifier, s'assurer | The inspectors are trying to ascertain the cause of the fire.
expedite | accélérer, hâter | Paying an extra fee will expedite the processing of your visa.
rectify | rectifier, corriger | The bank has promised to rectify the error as soon as possible.
procure | se procurer, acquérir | The hospital struggled to procure enough equipment during the winter.
reimburse | rembourser | The company will reimburse your travel expenses on presentation of receipts.
remit | envoyer, régler (fonds) | Kindly remit the outstanding amount within fourteen days.
dispatch | expédier, envoyer | Your order was dispatched this morning and should arrive on Friday.
## Procédures administratives
paperwork | formalités administratives, paperasse | Ali spent the whole afternoon dealing with paperwork for the new office.
directive | directive, instruction | All departments must follow the new directive on data storage.
bylaw | arrêté municipal, règlement local | A local bylaw forbids cycling on the seafront promenade.
formalities | formalités | The customs formalities took longer than the flight itself.
documentation | documentation, dossiers | Without proper documentation, your application cannot be processed.
submission | soumission, dépôt | The deadline for the submission of proposals is the end of May.
processing fee | frais de dossier | A processing fee of twenty euros is charged for each application.
stipulation | stipulation, condition | The grant came with the stipulation that the results be made public.
provision | disposition, clause | A new provision allows employees to work from home two days a week.
inquiry | enquête, instruction | The ministry has launched an inquiry into the delays at the border.
## Candidatures et documents
eligibility | éligibilité, admissibilité | Eligibility for the grant depends on your income and place of residence.
waiver | renonciation, décharge | Participants must sign a waiver before taking part in the climb.
notarize | faire légaliser, authentifier par un notaire | The translation must be notarized before you submit it to the consulate.
proof of address | justificatif de domicile | A recent utility bill is accepted as proof of address.
dossier | dossier (pièces justificatives) | The lawyer prepared a thick dossier on the company's past contracts.
expiry | expiration, échéance | Check the expiry date on your passport before booking the trip.
exemption | exemption, dispense | Students on low incomes can apply for an exemption from the fee.
countersign | contresigner | The form must be countersigned by your line manager.
attestation | attestation, certificat | The embassy asked for an attestation of her degree from the university.
supporting documents | pièces justificatives | Upload all supporting documents in a single PDF file.
## Prépositions formelles
in accordance with | conformément à | The waste must be disposed of in accordance with local regulations.
thereof | y afférent, de ce / de cela | The tenant is responsible for the property and any part thereof.
whereby | selon lequel, par lequel | They set up a system whereby staff can report problems anonymously.
prior to | avant, préalable à | Passengers must check in at least two hours prior to departure.
in lieu of | au lieu de, en lieu et place de | Staff may take extra days off in lieu of overtime pay.
subject to | sous réserve de, soumis à | All prices are subject to change without prior notice.
in respect of | au sujet de, concernant | No payment has been received in respect of the March invoice.
save for | hormis, excepté | The building was empty, save for a security guard on the ground floor.
for the purposes of | aux fins de, pour les besoins de | For the purposes of this agreement, a working day excludes public holidays.
in the absence of | en l'absence de | In the absence of the chair, her deputy will lead the meeting.
## Formules épistolaires
please do not hesitate to contact us | n'hésitez pas à nous contacter | If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
we regret to inform you | nous avons le regret de vous informer | We regret to inform you that your application has been unsuccessful.
further to | suite à, conformément à | Further to our telephone conversation, I am sending the revised schedule.
with reference to | concernant, se référant à | I am writing with reference to the vacancy advertised on your website.
yours faithfully | veuillez agréer... (formule finale sans nom) | The letter began with "Dear Sir or Madam" and ended with "Yours faithfully".
kind regards | bien cordialement, meilleures salutations | She always ends her work emails with "Kind regards" and her full name.
to whom it may concern | à qui de droit | The reference letter was addressed "To whom it may concern".
for your attention | à votre attention | I am forwarding this complaint for your attention and further action.
please find attached | veuillez trouver ci-joint | Please find attached the minutes of yesterday's meeting.
I look forward to hearing from you | dans l'attente de vous lire | Thank you for considering my application; I look forward to hearing from you.
## Institutions et fonctionnaires
town clerk | secrétaire général de mairie | The town clerk keeps the official records of every council meeting.
municipality | municipalité, commune | The municipality is responsible for street lighting and rubbish collection.
commissioner | commissaire, membre de commission | The commissioner announced new rules for public transport fares.
bureaucrat | fonctionnaire, bureaucrate | Sofia complained that a single bureaucrat had delayed her permit for months.
functionary | petit fonctionnaire, agent subalterne | A minor functionary stamped the papers without even reading them.
secretariat | secrétariat | Questions about the conference should be sent to the secretariat.
officialdom | l'administration, la technocratie | Lukas spent weeks battling officialdom to get his residence permit.
inspector | inspecteur, contrôleur | A health inspector visits the restaurant twice a year.
auditor | auditeur, commissaire aux comptes | The external auditor found several gaps in the accounts.
liaison officer | agent de liaison, officier de liaison | Each school has a liaison officer who works with the local council.
## Adjectifs formels
mandatory | obligatoire | Wearing a helmet on the construction site is mandatory.
provisional | provisoire | The dates are provisional and may still change.
statutory | légal, réglementaire | Employees are entitled to twenty days of statutory annual leave.
administrative | administratif | Half of the budget goes on administrative costs.
pertinent | pertinent, approprié | Please include only the information that is pertinent to your claim.
requisite | requis, nécessaire | Few candidates had the requisite experience for the position.
applicable | applicable | Please tick the box if this condition is applicable to you.
discretionary | discrétionnaire, facultatif | The manager has discretionary power to approve small expenses.
ineligible | inéligible, non admissible | Applicants who miss the deadline are ineligible for funding.
pending | en attente, pendant | His application is still pending, so he cannot travel yet.
## Procédure de réunion
agenda | ordre du jour | The new parking rules are the first item on the agenda.
matters arising | questions diverses (issues du PV précédent) | Under matters arising, the treasurer reported on last month's repairs.
convene | convoquer | The board will convene an emergency meeting on Monday.
apologies for absence | excuses (absences) | The secretary read out the apologies for absence before the meeting began.
unanimous | unanime | The decision to close the old bridge was unanimous.
any other business | questions diverses (fin de réunion) | Under any other business, Emre raised the issue of the broken lift.
point of order | rappel au règlement | She rose on a point of order to question the voting procedure.
proxy | procuration | If you cannot attend, you may vote by proxy.
standing orders | règlement intérieur | Under the standing orders, each speaker has five minutes.
casting vote | voix prépondérante | With the votes tied, the chair used her casting vote.
## Adverbes et durées formels
duly | dûment | The form was duly signed and returned before the deadline.
forthwith | immédiatement, sur-le-champ | Any employee breaking these rules will be dismissed forthwith.
expressly | expressément | Smoking is expressly forbidden in all parts of the building.
hereafter | ci-après | The company, hereafter called "the employer", agrees to the following terms.
with immediate effect | avec effet immédiat | The director has resigned with immediate effect.
until further notice | jusqu'à nouvel ordre | The museum will remain closed until further notice.
in due course | le moment venu, en temps utile | You will be informed of the result in due course.
as of | à compter de, dès | As of the first of January, all fees will be paid online.
regrettably | malencontreusement, au regret de | Regrettably, we are unable to offer you a place this year.
kindly | veuillez (formule de politesse) | Visitors are kindly requested to switch off their mobile phones.
