## Les mouvements littéraires
Romanticism | romantisme | Romanticism celebrated nature, emotion and the power of the imagination.
Realism | réalisme | Realism tried to show ordinary life without decoration or idealisation.
Naturalism | naturalisme | Naturalism showed characters shaped by poverty, heredity and their environment.
Modernism | modernisme | Modernism broke with traditional forms after the First World War.
Postmodernism | postmodernisme | Postmodernism often plays with the reader and mixes high and popular culture.
the Gothic | littérature gothique | Gothic fiction is full of ruined castles, family curses and mysterious strangers.
magic realism | réalisme magique | In works of magic realism, ghosts sit at the dinner table as if nothing were strange.
aestheticism | esthétisme | Aestheticism defended the idea of art for art's sake.
the avant-garde | avant-garde | The avant-garde rejected the rules that earlier writers had followed.
existentialism | existentialisme | Existentialism shaped many novels about freedom, choice and the absurd.
