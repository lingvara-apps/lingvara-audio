# UNIT: Langage d'entreprise | Gestion, reporting financier, contrats
## Structure d'entreprise
subsidiary | filiale, succursale | The firm sold its Brazilian subsidiary to a local competitor.
parent company | maison mère, société mère | All major decisions still have to be approved by the parent company.
conglomerate | conglomérat | The conglomerate owns hotels, shipping lines and a chain of bakeries.
holding company | société holding, holding | The holding company owns shares in more than twenty smaller firms.
division | division, pôle | Sofia runs the European division of a large logistics group.
joint venture | coentreprise, entreprise commune | The two companies formed a joint venture to build wind farms.
consortium (consortia) | consortium | A consortium of five banks agreed to finance the new bridge.
affiliate | filiale, société affiliée | The company opened an affiliate in Lisbon to serve southern Europe.
spin-off | entreprise issue d'une autre, filiale autonome | The research unit became a successful spin-off within three years.
sole trader | entreprise individuelle, entrepreneur individuel | As a sole trader, Emre is personally responsible for all his business debts.
## Gouvernance
board of directors | conseil d'administration | The board of directors meets four times a year.
chairperson | président du conseil d'administration | The chairperson opened the annual meeting with a short speech.
chief executive | directeur général, PDG | The new chief executive promised to cut costs without closing any branches.
non-executive director | administrateur non exécutif, administrateur indépendant | She sits on two boards as a non-executive director.
governance | gouvernance | Investors demanded better governance after the accounting scandal.
fiduciary duty | obligation fiduciaire | Directors have a fiduciary duty to act in the best interests of the company.
oversight | surveillance, contrôle | The scandal revealed a serious lack of oversight in the finance department.
compliance | conformité | Our compliance team checks every new product against the regulations.
audit committee | comité d'audit | The audit committee raised concerns about the way expenses were reported.
whistle-blower | lanceur d'alerte | A whistle-blower revealed that safety tests had been skipped.
## Stratégie
consolidate | consolider, regrouper | The firm plans to consolidate its three regional offices into one.
divest | céder, se dessaisir de | The group decided to divest its loss-making retail division.
restructure | restructurer | The airline had to restructure its debt to avoid bankruptcy.
downsize | réduire ses effectifs, restructurer | When orders fell, the factory was forced to downsize.
offshore | délocaliser | Many manufacturers chose to offshore production to cut labour costs.
rebrand | changer de marque, refondre l'identité visuelle | After the scandal, the airline decided to rebrand with a new name and logo.
competitive edge | avantage concurrentiel | Fast delivery gives the start-up a clear competitive edge over its rivals.
economies of scale | économies d'échelle | Larger factories benefit from economies of scale and lower unit costs.
core business | cœur de métier, activité principale | After years of expansion, they decided to focus on their core business.
roadmap | feuille de route | The management presented a five-year roadmap for digital growth.
## États financiers
balance sheet | bilan | The balance sheet shows that the company has very little debt.
income statement | compte de résultat | Analysts went through the income statement line by line.
cash flow | flux de trésorerie, cash-flow | Despite high sales, the shop had serious cash flow problems.
turnover | chiffre d'affaires | The bakery's annual turnover has doubled in five years.
gross margin | marge brute | Rising energy prices have squeezed our gross margin.
net profit | bénéfice net | Net profit fell by twelve percent compared to last year.
fiscal year | exercice fiscal | The company's fiscal year ends on the thirty-first of March.
quarterly | trimestriel | The quarterly results were better than most analysts expected.
annual report | rapport annuel | The annual report includes a section on environmental impact.
outlook | perspectives, prévisions | The board described the financial outlook for next year as cautiously positive.
## Comptabilité
asset | actif, bien | The building is the company's most valuable asset.
liability | passif, dette | Unpaid salaries appear as a liability on the balance sheet.
depreciation | amortissement | The cost of the machines is spread over ten years through depreciation.
write off | passer en pertes, amortir | The bank had to write off millions in unpaid loans.
overheads | frais généraux | Moving to a smaller office will reduce our overheads.
accrual | régularisation, charges constatées d'avance | Accountants record an accrual for expenses that have not yet been paid.
ledger | grand livre, livre comptable | Every transaction is recorded in the general ledger.
accounts receivable | clients, créances clients | Our accounts receivable have grown because several clients are paying late.
discrepancy | écart, divergence | The auditors found a small discrepancy between the two sets of figures.
audit | audit, contrôle des comptes | An external audit is carried out at the end of every financial year.
## Investissement et capital
equity | capitaux propres, actions | The founders still hold most of the equity in the company.
stock option | option sur actions, stock-option | Senior staff receive stock options as part of their annual package.
dividend | dividende | The company has paid a dividend every year since 1990.
private equity | capital-investissement, fonds d'investissement | A private equity firm bought the struggling hotel chain last spring.
flotation | introduction en bourse | The flotation of the company raised more money than expected.
share price | cours de l'action, prix de l'action | The share price fell sharply after the disappointing results were announced.
return on investment | retour sur investissement, rentabilité | The board expects a return on investment within three years.
portfolio | portefeuille | Maria keeps a balanced portfolio of shares and bonds.
liquidity | liquidité | The bank needed emergency support to restore liquidity.
insolvent | insolvable | By the end of the year the company was effectively insolvent.
## Contrats
addendum (addenda) | avenant, addendum | Both parties signed an addendum extending the delivery deadline.
terms and conditions | conditions générales | Please read the terms and conditions carefully before signing.
binding | contraignant, obligatoire | Once both parties sign, the agreement becomes legally binding.
breach | violation, rupture | Delivering the goods late was a clear breach of contract.
termination | résiliation, fin | The termination of the agreement takes effect after thirty days.
indemnity | indemnité, garantie | The supplier provides an indemnity against any damage caused by its products.
non-disclosure agreement | accord de non-divulgation, accord de confidentialité | All new employees must sign a non-disclosure agreement.
renewal | renouvellement | The lease comes up for renewal next spring.
penalty clause | clause pénale | A penalty clause charges the builder for every day of delay.
force majeure | force majeure | The flood was treated as force majeure, so nobody had to pay compensation.
## Négociation et accord
bargaining chip | moyen de pression, monnaie d'échange | The union used the threat of a strike as a bargaining chip.
sticking point | point de désaccord, pierre d'achoppement | The length of the contract remained the main sticking point in the talks.
opening bid | première offre | Their opening bid was far lower than we had anticipated.
standoff | impasse, blocage | After a week-long standoff, both sides finally returned to the table.
red line | ligne rouge | Job security for existing staff is a red line for the union.
split the difference | couper la poire en deux, faire moitié-moitié | They asked for ten percent, we offered six, so we agreed to split the difference.
memorandum of understanding | protocole d'accord, protocole d'entente | The two universities signed a memorandum of understanding on student exchange.
due diligence | audit d'acquisition, vérification préalable | Before the purchase, lawyers carried out extensive due diligence.
initial | parapher | Please initial each page of the agreement before signing the last one.
stipulate | stipuler, prévoir | The contract stipulates that payment must be made within thirty days.
## Ressources humaines
recruitment | recrutement | The recruitment process includes two interviews and a written test.
talent pool | vivier de talents | The company opened an office in Krakow to tap into a wider talent pool.
succession planning | planification de la succession | Good succession planning ensures that key roles never stay empty for long.
exit interview | entretien de départ | During her exit interview, Sofia mentioned the lack of flexible hours.
remuneration | rémunération | The report criticised the high remuneration of senior managers.
fringe benefits | avantages en nature, avantages sociaux | Fringe benefits include private health insurance and a company car.
severance pay | indemnité de licenciement | Employees who lose their jobs will receive six months of severance pay.
attrition | rotation du personnel, attrition | High attrition among young staff is costing the company a lot of money.
secondment | détachement | He is on a six-month secondment to the Singapore office.
probation period | période d'essai | New employees are on a three-month probation period.
## Formules d'entreprise
going forward | à l'avenir, dorénavant | Going forward, all reports must be submitted by Friday.
flag up | signaler, attirer l'attention sur | Please flag up any delays in the schedule as soon as you notice them.
action item | tâche à accomplir, action à mener | Each action item from the meeting now has a clear owner.
on the agenda | à l'ordre du jour | Salary reviews are not on the agenda for today's meeting.
as per | conformément à, selon | As per our agreement, the second payment is due in May.
for your perusal | pour lecture, pour examen | I have attached the draft budget for your perusal.
at your earliest convenience | dès que possible, à votre convenance | Please return the signed copy at your earliest convenience.
key performance indicator | indicateur clé de performance | Customer satisfaction is our most important key performance indicator.
best practice | bonne pratique | The guide summarises best practice for handling customer complaints.
value proposition | proposition de valeur | Our value proposition is fast delivery at a fair price.
