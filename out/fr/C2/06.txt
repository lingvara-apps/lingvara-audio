# UNIT: Culture du monde anglophone | Traditions, fêtes, cuisine, vie quotidienne
## Fêtes et jours spéciaux
Thanksgiving | Thanksgiving (États-Unis, Canada) | Every Thanksgiving the whole family gathers at Grandma's house for a huge dinner.
Bonfire Night | la nuit des feux de joie (5 novembre, Royaume-Uni) | On Bonfire Night the sky over London is full of fireworks.
Boxing Day | le lendemain de Noël (26 décembre) | Many people spend Boxing Day watching football or hunting for bargains in the sales.
Hogmanay | la fête du Nouvel An (Écosse) | Edinburgh's Hogmanay street party attracts visitors from all over the world.
St Patrick's Day | la Saint-Patrick (Irlande, 17 mars) | On St Patrick's Day people wear green and crowd into the streets for parades.
Burns Night | la nuit de Burns (en l'honneur du poète Robert Burns, Écosse) | At our Burns Night supper, Lukas read a poem aloud before the haggis was served.
Pancake Day | le mardi gras (jour des crêpes) | On Pancake Day the children compete to see who can flip a pancake the highest.
Anzac Day | l'Anzac Day (25 avril, Australie et Nouvelle-Zélande) | On Anzac Day thousands of Australians and New Zealanders attend dawn services, some of them at Gallipoli.
Remembrance Day | le jour du Souvenir (11 novembre, commémoration des guerres) | At eleven o'clock on Remembrance Day, the whole country falls silent for two minutes.
Groundhog Day | le jour de la marmotte (2 février, États-Unis) ; train-train quotidien | Every morning at this job feels like Groundhog Day: same emails, same problems.
